# Sendai

Capital of Miyagi Prefecture and former castle town of Date Masamune, founder of the Sendai domain, whose mausoleum (Zuihoden) and shrine Osaki Hachimangu (a National Treasure) are its main historic sights. Known for grilled beef tongue (gyutan), zunda (sweet edamame paste), the Tanabata festival every August 6-8 and the SENDAI Pageant of Starlight illumination in December. Matsushima Bay, one of the Three Views of Japan, is the usual side trip.

## How to get there

- From Tokyo by Tohoku Shinkansen: Hayabusa reserved ordinary seat ¥11,630 in regular season (fare ¥6,270 + limited express ¥5,360, after JR East's March 2026 fare revision); Yamabiko reserved ¥11,310. Reserved seats are ¥200 cheaper in off-peak season, ¥200 more in peak season and ¥400 more in the busiest season; Eki-net 'Shinkansen eTicket' takes ¥200 off reserved seats all year. Travel time not confirmed in this research.

## Getting around

- Loople Sendai sightseeing loop bus (one-way loop from Sendai Station; Zuihoden-mae is the 4th stop). Fares from 1 Oct 2026: single ride ¥300, Loople 1-day pass ¥700, Loople + subway 1-day pass ¥1,000 (were ¥630 and ¥920 before; passes bought by 30 Sep 2026 stay valid). The 1-day pass gives a discount at Zuihoden (¥460 instead of ¥570).
- Osaki Hachimangu: Sendai city bus from Sendai Station west bus pool towards Minami-Yoshinari, about 20 min to 'Osaki-Hachimangu-mae', then a 5-min walk; or JR Senzan Line to Kunimi or Tohoku-Fukushidai-mae and walk 15-25 min.
- Matsushima: JR to Matsushima-Kaigan Station (Zuiganji is a 10-min walk from it).

## Local food

- Gyutan (charcoal-grilled beef tongue): created in Sendai in 1948 by Sano Keishiro, who seasoned it with salt and grilled it over charcoal; the historic shop is Aji Tasuke in Kokubuncho, a few minutes from Kotodai-koen subway station (11:30-21:30, closed Mondays as of 2025). 'Gyutan-dori' inside JR Sendai Station gathers many gyutan specialists; Tanya Zenjiro Sendai Ekimae honten is about 2 min from the station.
- Zunda: boiled edamame mashed with sugar, a Miyagi specialty eaten on mochi (zunda mochi); 'Zunda Komichi' inside JR Sendai Station has zunda shops, and Cafe Tamazawa (Ichibancho) serves it.

## Seasons

- August: Sendai Tanabata Matsuri every Aug 6-8, with the Tanabata fireworks on the evening of Aug 5.
- December: SENDAI Pageant of Starlight along Jozenji-dori (2026: Dec 4-27, planned) and the Sendai Christmas Market in Nishikicho Park (2026: Dec 4-25).
- Winter: Zuihoden closes earlier from Dec 1 to Jan 31 (last entry 16:00) and is closed on Dec 31; Matsushima cruises run a shorter schedule from November to mid-March (10:00-15:00 departures).
- New Year: Osaki Hachimangu is crowded on Jan 1-3 and on Jan 14 (Matsutaki-sai / Dontosai fire festival).
- Autumn: Rinnoji garden is a foliage spot; Zuiganji in Matsushima holds paid evening visits in autumn (2026: Oct 30-Nov 23, 17:00-21:00).
