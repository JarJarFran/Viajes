# Rinnoji Temple Garden

## Description

Temple in Kitayama, Aoba-ku, with a strolling garden behind the main hall, entered by the path to the right of the hall through a coin gate. The seated Shaka statue in the three-storey pagoda is shown on the 1st and 15th of each month.
