# Sendai Castle Ruins - Aoba Castle Exhibition Hall

## Description

Exhibition hall on the Honmaru (main bailey) site of Sendai (Aoba) Castle, inside the Aoba Castle Honmaru Kaikan. Shows letters and armour of Date Masamune, with a CG theatre and a VR walk through the castle as it was.

## Tips

Prices come from a travel-information site; the official site could not be checked. Address: 1 Kawauchi, Aoba-ku (tel. 022-222-0218).
