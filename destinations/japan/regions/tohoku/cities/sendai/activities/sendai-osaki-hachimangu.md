# Osaki Hachimangu Shrine

## Description

Shrine whose gongen-style main hall was built in 1604-1607 with a donation from Date Masamune, a masterpiece of Momoyama architecture designated a National Treasure (1903, re-designated 1952). The exterior can be viewed freely.

## How to get there

Sendai city bus from Sendai Station west bus pool towards Minami-Yoshinari, about 20 min to 'Osaki-Hachimangu-mae', then a 5-min walk; or 15-25 min on foot from JR Kunimi or Tohoku-Fukushidai-mae stations (Senzan Line).

## Tips

About 30 min for a visit, 1 hour with goshuin and side shrines; weekday mornings and after 15:00 are quieter. Jan 14 hosts the Matsutaki-sai (Dontosai) fire festival.
