# Zuiganji Temple (Matsushima)

## Description

National Treasure Zen temple in Matsushima, regarded as Tohoku's leading Zen temple, on the shore of Matsushima Bay (one of the Three Views of Japan). Usually combined with a bay cruise as a day trip from Sendai.

## How to get there

10-min walk from JR Matsushima-Kaigan Station; no dedicated parking.

## Tips

Closing times differ between sources (one: Apr-Sep to 16:30, Mar/Oct 16:00, Feb/Nov 15:30, Jan/Dec 15:00; another: to 17:00, Mar/Oct 16:30, Feb/Nov 16:00, Dec/Jan 15:30; a July 2026 guide gives November close 16:00, last entry 15:30); some listings still show the old ¥700 fee. Open every day.
