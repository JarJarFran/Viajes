# Matsushima Bay Island Cruise (Niomaru course)

## Description

Round cruise of about 50 minutes among the pine-covered islets of Matsushima Bay, passing landmarks such as Nio-jima. Run by the Matsushima Shimameguri Kankosen cooperative.
