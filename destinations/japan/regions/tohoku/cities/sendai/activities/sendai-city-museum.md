# Sendai City Museum

## Description

The city's history museum, reopened on 2 April 2024 after about two and a half years of renovation (new display cases, LED lighting, hands-on corner). Holds a permanent exhibition plus rotating special exhibitions.

## Tips

Check the special-exhibition schedule: during special exhibitions the special ticket also covers the permanent galleries. Fee revisions after 2024 could not be checked.
