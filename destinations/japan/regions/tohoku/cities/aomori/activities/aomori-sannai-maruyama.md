# Sannai-Maruyama Jomon Site

## Description

One of Japan's largest Jomon-period settlement sites, with reconstructed pit dwellings and a large six-pillar building, plus the Sanmaru Museum displaying excavated pottery and clay figures. Part of the 'Jomon Prehistoric Sites in Northern Japan'.

## How to get there

Western outskirts of Aomori city, next to the Aomori Museum of Art (bus route not confirmed here).

## Tips

Some older pages list ¥410 and other summer-hour periods; the official site gives ¥500 and the hours above.
