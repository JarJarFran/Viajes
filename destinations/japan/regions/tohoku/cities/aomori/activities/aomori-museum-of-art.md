# Aomori Museum of Art

## Description

Prefectural art museum next to the Sannai-Maruyama site, known for Yoshitomo Nara's large 'Aomori-ken' dog sculpture and Marc Chagall's backdrops for the ballet 'Aleko'.

## How to get there

Next to the Sannai-Maruyama Jomon site.
