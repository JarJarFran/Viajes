# Nebuta Museum Wa Rasse

## Description

Museum dedicated to the Aomori Nebuta Matsuri, displaying full-size illuminated nebuta floats from recent festivals, with exhibits on their making and festival music and dance.

## How to get there

Waterfront next to JR Aomori Station.

## Tips

Jalan still lists ¥600 (old price); ¥620 is the current adult fee. The city page lists only 31 Dec–1 Jan as closing days, while tourism sites add 9–10 Aug.
