# Aomori

Capital of Aomori Prefecture at the northern tip of Honshu, on Mutsu Bay. Best known for the Aomori Nebuta Matsuri, whose giant illuminated floats are shown all year at the Nebuta Museum Wa Rasse, and for the Sannai-Maruyama Jomon site, part of the 'Jomon Prehistoric Sites in Northern Japan'.

## How to get there

- Tohoku Shinkansen Hayabusa Tokyo → Shin-Aomori: reserved ordinary seat ¥18,110 one way in regular season (after JR East's 14 Mar 2026 fare revision; ¥17,910 with the Eki-net Shinkansen eTicket). Reserved seats are ¥200 cheaper in off-peak season, ¥200 more in peak and ¥400 more in the busiest season. Older pages still show ¥17,670 (pre-revision). Travel time not confirmed in this research.
- Eki-net 'Tokudane 14' (25% off, ¥13,420 reserved) was reported as sold only until 16 Sep 2026; check whether a discount ticket is still offered.
- From Shin-Aomori, JR local trains run to Aomori Station in the city centre (time and fare not confirmed here).

## Getting around

- Sannai-Maruyama Jomon site and the Aomori Museum of Art are next to each other; a same-day Aomori Museum of Art collection-exhibition ticket stub gives a discount at Sannai-Maruyama.
- Nebuta Museum Wa Rasse is in the waterfront area by JR Aomori Station. City bus fares and day passes were not confirmed in this research.

## Local food

- Nokke-don — rice bowl topped with sashimi chosen piece by piece from market stalls with a ticket book; the Furukawa Fish Market (Aomori Gyosai Center) near Aomori Station is the place to try it (not verified in this research).
- Hotate (scallops) from Mutsu Bay — eaten raw, grilled or as kaiyaki miso cooked in a shell (not verified in this research).
- Apples — Aomori is Japan's main apple-growing prefecture; apple pies, juice and cider are sold at stations and shops (not verified in this research).
- Miso curry milk ramen — Aomori city's local ramen variety (not verified in this research).

## Seasons

- Aomori Nebuta Matsuri every 2–7 August; the Nebuta Museum Wa Rasse is open until 19:00 from May to August.
- Year-end closures: Wa Rasse closed 31 Dec–1 Jan; Sannai-Maruyama closed 30 Dec–1 Jan and every 4th Monday.
