# Aomori Nebuta Matsuri

## Description

One of Tohoku's major summer festivals: large lantern floats of warriors and mythical figures paraded with taiko drums and haneto dancers. The prize-winning floats are later displayed at the Nebuta Museum Wa Rasse.
