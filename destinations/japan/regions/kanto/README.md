# Kanto

Japan's most populated plain and the country's political and economic center. A mix of megalopolis (Tokyo-Yokohama), historic temples and pilgrimage sites (Kamakura, Nikko) and mountain hot-spring resorts (Hakone), all within two hours by train.

## What defines this region

- Tokyo as the epicenter, with day trips to satellite cities.
- Kamakura and Nikko: historic heritage (Great Buddha, samurai-era shrines).
- Hakone: onsen with views of Mount Fuji.
- Mount Fuji lies to the west, on the border with Chubu (Kawaguchiko area).
