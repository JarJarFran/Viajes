# Hase-dera

## Description

Hillside temple with an 11-headed Kannon over 9 m tall, a cave of small Benzaiten statues and a terrace looking over Yuigahama bay.

## How to get there

Enoden line to Hase Station, 5 min walk; 5 min from the Great Buddha.

## Tips

Winter hours end around 16:30. The view terrace and its small restaurant are best on clear mornings.
