# Great Buddha of Kamakura (Kotoku-in)

## Description

13.35 m bronze Amida Buddha from 1252, sitting in the open air since a 15th-century tsunami washed away its hall. National Treasure.

## How to get there

Enoden line to Hase Station, 7 min walk (Hase-dera is on the way).

## Tips

You can step inside the statue (¥50, 8:00–16:30). Go early; tour groups arrive late morning.
