# Day trip to Kamakura & Enoshima

## Description

Former samurai capital 1 h from Tokyo: Great Buddha (Kotoku-in, ¥300), Tsurugaoka Hachimangu (free), Hokoku-ji bamboo temple, Hasedera, plus Enoshima island (caves, botanical garden, views of Fuji on clear days) across the bridge.

## How to get there

JR Yokosuka Line from Tokyo Station to Kamakura (~1 h); Enoshima by Enoden line from Kamakura.

## Tips

Full day. In winter: crisp air, Fuji views likely from Enoshima. Combine with the seaside lunch (shirasu whitebait).
