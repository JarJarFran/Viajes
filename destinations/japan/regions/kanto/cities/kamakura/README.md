# Kamakura

Former samurai capital (12th–14th c.), 1 h from Tokyo. Temples, the Great Buddha, and the seaside Enoshima island.

## How to get there

- JR Yokosuka Line from Tokyo Station to Kamakura (~1 h, ~¥1,000 return).
- Enoshima: Enoden line from Kamakura Station.

## What to see and do

- [Day trip to Kamakura & Enoshima](activities/kamakura-day-trip.md) — Great Buddha ¥300, Tsurugaoka Hachimangu, Hasedera, Hokoku-ji, Enoshima (Fuji views on clear winter days)

## Where to eat

- Shirasu (whitebait) bowls by the beach; cafes around Tsurugaoka Hachimangu.
