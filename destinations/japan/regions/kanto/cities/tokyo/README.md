# Tokyo

Capital of Japan and the world's largest metropolitan area. Every ward (Shibuya, Shinjuku, Asakusa, Ueno...) has its own personality.

## How to get there

- **Narita Airport (NRT)**: ~60 km away, Narita Express (JR) or Keisei Skyliner to Ueno/Shinjuku (~1 h).
- **Haneda Airport (HND)**: ~15 km from the center, Keikyu train or Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsuchō/Shinagawa (~30 min).
- By bullet train: Tokyo Station (Tokaido/Tohoku Shinkansen lines). From Kyoto: Hikari ~2 h 15 min (~¥14,000); Nozomi not covered by JR Pass.

## Seasonal events and New Year closures

- [Winter illuminations](events/tokyo-winter-illuminations.md) — Marunouchi and Shinjuku Southern Terrace to ~Feb 14, Shinjuku Chuo Park to ~mid-January, Caretta Shiodome, Roppongi Keyakizaka; Yomiuriland Jewellumination on selected days
- [New Year sales / fukubukuro](events/tokyo-new-year-sales.md) — mostly Jan 1–7; some campaigns run to mid/late January
- [Grand Sumo Tournament](events/tokyo-sumo-tournament.md) — January, May and September at Ryogoku Kokugikan
- Hatsumode (Jan 1–3) at Meiji Jingu draws 3M+ visitors; Zojo-ji and smaller shrines are calmer
- New Year closures: Imperial Palace East Gardens Dec 28–Jan 3 (and every Mon/Fri), Hama-rikyu Dec 29–Jan 1, TMG observatories Dec 29–31 and Jan 2–3, Tsukiji Outer Market ~Dec 31–Jan 4

## Where to eat

- Izakayas in **Omoide Yokocho** (Shinjuku) and under the tracks of **Yurakucho**.
- Ramen at any station; department stores (depachika) have food floors in the basement.
- Tsukiji Outer Market for breakfast.

## Transport

- JR Yamanote loop + Tokyo Metro/Toei subway. **IC card (Suica/Pasmo)** needed — JR Pass does not cover subways.
- Tokyo Subway 24/48/72 h tickets for heavy metro days.
