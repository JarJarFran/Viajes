# Tokyo

Capital of Japan and the world's largest metropolitan area. Every ward (Shibuya, Shinjuku, Asakusa, Ueno...) has its own personality.

## How to get there

- **Narita Airport (NRT)**: ~60 km away, Narita Express (JR) or Keisei Skyliner to Ueno/Shinjuku (~1 h).
- **Haneda Airport (HND)**: ~15 km from the center, Keikyu train or Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsuchō/Shinagawa (~30 min).
- By bullet train: Tokyo Station (Tokaido/Tohoku Shinkansen lines). From Kyoto: Hikari ~2 h 15 min (~¥14,000); Nozomi not covered by JR Pass.

- [Winter illuminations](activities/tokyo-winter-illuminations.md) — Marunouchi (to ~Feb 14), Shinjuku Southern Terrace (to ~Feb 14), Shinjuku Chuo Park (to ~Jan 12), Caretta Shiodome, Roppongi Keyakizaka; Yomiuriland Jewellumination Sat Jan 17/24/31

## Where to eat

- Izakayas in **Omoide Yokocho** (Shinjuku) and under the tracks of **Yurakucho**.
- Ramen at any station; department stores (depachika) have food floors in the basement.
- Tsukiji Outer Market for breakfast.

## Transport

- JR Yamanote loop + Tokyo Metro/Toei subway. **IC card (Suica/Pasmo)** needed — JR Pass does not cover subways.
- Tokyo Subway 24/48/72 h tickets for heavy metro days.
