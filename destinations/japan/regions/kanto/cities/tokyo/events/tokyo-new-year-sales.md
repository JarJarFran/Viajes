# New Year sales & fukubukuro (lucky bags)

## Description

Department stores and electronics chains sell fukubukuro mystery lucky bags and New Year discounts. Most fukubukuro are gone after the first week, but some sales run to mid/late January.

## How to get there

Shinjuku (Takashimaya Times Square, Isetan), Shibuya (109, Parco), Ginza.

## Tips

After the first week, check remaining sales (often listed as "hatsuuri") at major department stores; some chains (Yodobashi, Bic Camera) run New Year campaigns into mid-January.
