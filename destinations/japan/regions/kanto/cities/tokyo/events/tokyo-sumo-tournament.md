# Grand Sumo Tournament (Ryogoku Kokugikan, January)

## Description

January Grand Sumo Tournament (Hatsu Basho) at Ryogoku Kokugikan. 15 days, ~8:30–18:00.

## How to get there

JR Sobu Line / Toei Oedo Line to Ryogoku.

## Tips

Book tickets as soon as the general sale opens (see the edition's `on_sale`) — they sell out. Afternoon session (from ~¥3,500) is the realistic option. Queue early for standing-room tickets ¥1,200? Check current prices.
