# Setagaya Boroichi (flea market)

## Description

A 440-year-old street market: ~700 stalls of antiques, kimono, tools, plants and food (try the daikan-mochi) along Boroichi-dori in Setagaya.

## Dates & hours

Every December 15–16 and January 15–16, 9:00–20:00 (times may change with the weather).

## How to get there

Tokyu Setagaya Line to Kamimachi or Setagaya Station (from Sangenjaya on the Den-en-toshi Line).

## Tips

Very crowded around midday; mornings are best for finds. Cash.
