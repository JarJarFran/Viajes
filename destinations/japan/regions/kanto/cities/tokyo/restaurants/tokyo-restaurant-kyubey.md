# Kyubey (Ginza main)

## What to order

Historic Ginza sushi counter (since 1936); omakase from ~¥8,000; lunch sets cheaper.

## Hours & booking

Lunch 11:30-14:00, dinner 17:00-22:00; reserve ahead.

## Tips

Counter seats are the experience; English-speaking staff at the main counter.
