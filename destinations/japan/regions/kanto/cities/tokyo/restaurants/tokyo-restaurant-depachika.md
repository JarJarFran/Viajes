# Depachika food halls (Isetan Shinjuku / Mitsukoshi Ginza)

## What to order

Basement food halls of department stores: bento, sushi, wagashi, free samples; great for picnics and gifts.

## Hours & booking

~10:00-20:00; best deals after ~19:00 (discounted bento).

## Tips

Grab a discounted bento for the hotel; amazing variety and quality.
