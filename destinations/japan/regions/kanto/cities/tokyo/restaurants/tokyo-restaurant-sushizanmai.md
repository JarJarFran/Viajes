# Sushi Zanmai (Tsukiji)

## What to order

Counter sushi near the market; set menus ¥3,000-5,000; a la carte cheaper early.

## Hours & booking

Early morning-evening; the Tsukiji branch opens ~5:00 for the market crowd.

## Tips

Go at opening for the freshest cuts.
