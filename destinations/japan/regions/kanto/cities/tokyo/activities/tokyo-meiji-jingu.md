# Meiji Shrine (Meiji Jingu)

## Description

Shinto shrine dedicated to Emperor Meiji and Empress Shoken, set in a forest of 100,000 trees donated from all over Japan. One of the most visited places at New Year (hatsumode).

## How to get there

- JR Yamanote line to Harajuku; or Chiyoda/Fukutoshin metro lines to Meiji-jingumae.

## Tips

- Walk through nearby Yoyogi Park (on Sundays there is a lively street-performance and dance scene).
- You can write wishes on an ema (votive plaque).
