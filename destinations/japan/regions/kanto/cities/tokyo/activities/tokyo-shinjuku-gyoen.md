# Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden

## Description

58 ha garden mixing French, English and Japanese styles; greenhouse; very peaceful in winter.

## How to get there

Tokyo Metro Marunouchi Line to Shinjuku-sanchome or JR Shinjuku to Shinjuku Gyoen-mae gate.

## Tips

No alcohol allowed. Combine with Shinjuku's free TMG observatory (45F) and Omoide Yokocho for dinner.
