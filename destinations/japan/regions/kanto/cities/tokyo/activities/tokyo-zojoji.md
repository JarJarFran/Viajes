# Zojo-ji Temple

## Description

Major temple at the foot of Tokyo Tower with a 1,000-year history; one of Tokyo's NYE joya-no-kane (bell) venues with the tower lit behind it.

## How to get there

Toei Oedo/Mita lines to Onarimon; 5 min from Tokyo Tower.

## Tips

Photogenic with Tokyo Tower behind. Hatsumode queues on Jan 1–3 (we're in Tokyo from Jan 9 — calm by then).
