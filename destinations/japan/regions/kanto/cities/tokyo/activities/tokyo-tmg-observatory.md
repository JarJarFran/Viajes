# Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building observatory (45F)

## Description

Free 45th-floor observation decks (North & South towers) in Shinjuku, comparable views to Tokyo Tower for free.

## How to get there

Toei Shinjuku Line to Tochomae, then walk, or JR Shinjuku West Exit (10 min).

## Tips

Closes ~17:30 (North tower to 17:00–17:30 depending on season); check seasonal hours. Great sunset + night city lights, free.
