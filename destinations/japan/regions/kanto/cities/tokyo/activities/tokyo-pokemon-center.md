# Pokemon Center (Tokyo Station / Nihonbashi)

## Description

Official Pokemon stores: Tokyo Station Character Street (Ichibangai) and Nihonbashi (Pokemon Center Mega Tokyo with cafe).

## How to get there

Tokyo Station (Character Street, Yaesu exit) or Nihonbashi Station.

## Tips

Great for anime_manga_and_pop_culture fans and gifts. Mega Tokyo has exclusive Pikachu merch.
