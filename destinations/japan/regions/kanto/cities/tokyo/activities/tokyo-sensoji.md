# Senso-ji Temple (Asakusa)

## Description

Tokyo's oldest Buddhist temple (founded in 645 AD), dedicated to Kannon. You enter through the Kaminarimon ("Thunder Gate") with its giant red lantern and the Nakamise-dori shopping street (~250 m of traditional stalls).

## How to get there

- Tokyo Metro: Ginza or Asakusa line to Asakusa station; Toei Asakusa line to Asakusa.

## Tips

- Go early (8:00) to avoid crowds; the Nakamise stalls open around 10:00.
- In the main hall you can toss a coin, fan yourself with the incense smoke and draw an omikuji (fortune slip).
