# Ghibli Museum (Mitaka)

## Description

Hayao Miyazaki-designed museum of Studio Ghibli: original sketches, a short exclusive film, the Catbus.

## How to get there

JR Chuo Line to Mitaka (20 min from Shinjuku) + community bus ¥230 each way, or 15 min walk.

## Tips

Tickets sell out in minutes — buy on the 10th at 10:00 JST for the following month. Anime fans: priority.
