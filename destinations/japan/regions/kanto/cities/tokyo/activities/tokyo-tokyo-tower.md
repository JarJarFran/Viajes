# Tokyo Tower

## Description

333 m red-lattice tower (Eiffel-inspired), main deck 150 m with Fuji views on clear winter days.

## How to get there

Toei Oedo Line to Akabanebashi or Onarimon; or JR Hamamatsucho + walk.

## Tips

Free alternative with similar views: TMG Building observatory. Combine with Zojo-ji next door.
