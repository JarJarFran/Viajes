# Mori Art Museum (Roppongi Hills 53F)

## Description

Top-floor contemporary art museum in Roppongi Hills with city views.

## How to get there

Hibiya/Tozai? Use Hibiya Line or Oedo Line to Roppongi, then Roppongi Hills.

## Tips

Pair with Tokyo City View (52F, below) in one Roppongi visit.
