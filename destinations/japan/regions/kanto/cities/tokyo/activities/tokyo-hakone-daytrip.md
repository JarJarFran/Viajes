# Day trip to Hakone (onsen, Lake Ashi)

## Description

Hot-spring resort: pirate-ship cruise on Lake Ashi, ropeway over Owakudani, open-air museums, ryokan onsen.

## How to get there

Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto; Freepass covers the local loop (train, ropeway, bus, boat).

## Tips

Buy the Freepass at Shinjuku. Winter: crisp air, great Fuji visibility.
