# Tokyo Skytree

## Description

634 m tower, tallest in Japan. On clear winter days Mt Fuji is visible from the decks.

## How to get there

Tobu Skytree Line or Metro Hanzomon/Asakusa lines to Oshiage Station.

## Tips

Pre-book a timed slot online. Combine with the Sumida river walk and Asakusa nearby.
