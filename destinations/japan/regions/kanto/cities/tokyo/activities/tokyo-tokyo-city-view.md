# Tokyo City View (Roppongi Hills 52F)

## Description

Open-air + indoor observation deck 250 m up with 360° views incl. Tokyo Tower.

## How to get there

Roppongi Station (Hibiya/Oedo), Roppongi Hills Mori Tower.

## Tips

Sunset slot is best. Compare with free TMG observatory and paid Skytree.
