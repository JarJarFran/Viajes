# Ueno Park & museums

## Description

Big park with a museum cluster: Tokyo National Museum (Japan's largest art collection), National Museum of Western Art (Le Corbusier, UNESCO), Science Museum, zoo. Plus Ameya-Yokocho market street.

## How to get there

JR Yamanote/Ueno Station, or Ginza/Hibiya lines to Ueno.

## Tips

Full day if you enter 2+ museums; or a relaxed park + market stroll. Winter: few crowds, good light.
