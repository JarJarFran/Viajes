# Tokyo → Kamakura (day trip)

## Description

Train leg for the Kamakura/Enoshima day trip.

## How to get there

Tokyo Station (Yokosuka Line) or Shinjuku (Shonan-Shinjuku Line). IC card (Suica/Pasmo).

## Tips

Shonan-Shinjuku from Shinjuku is direct and fast if staying west.
