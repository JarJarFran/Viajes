# Tsukiji Outer Market

## Description

After the fish auction moved to the Toyosu inner market in 2018, the Tsukiji Outer Market is still the place to have breakfast: stalls with tamagoyaki (rolled omelette), sushi, fresh seafood, knives and live knife skills.

## How to get there

- Hibiya metro line to Tsukiji; or Toei Oedo line to Tsukiji-Shijo.

## Tips

- Go early for breakfast (it opens around 5:00-6:00, many stalls close around 13:00-14:00).
- Bring cash; few places take card.
