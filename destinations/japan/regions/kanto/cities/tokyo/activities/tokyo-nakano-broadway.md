# Nakano Broadway

## Description

1966 arcade of ~200 subculture shops: figures, manga (incl. rare), idol goods, retro toys, Mandarake.

## How to get there

JR Chuo/Sobu Line or Toei Oedo Line to Nakano Station.

## Tips

Otaku paradise next to Akihabara. Mandarake's main store is here.
