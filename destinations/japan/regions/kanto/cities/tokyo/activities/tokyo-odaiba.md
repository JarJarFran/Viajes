# Odaiba island (Gundam, beach, Rainbow Bridge)

## Description

Man-made island in Tokyo Bay: life-size Unicorn Gundam statue, small beach with skyline views, malls (DiverCity, Aqua City), Rainbow Bridge views at sunset/night.

## How to get there

Yurikamome line from Shimbashi across Rainbow Bridge to Odaiba-Kaihinkoen.

## Tips

Sunset at the beach or Gundam statue is the classic. teamLab Planets is at Toyosu nearby (different area).
