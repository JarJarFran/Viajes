# teamLab Borderless (Azabudai Hills)

## Description

Larger, mapless digital art museum inside Azabudai Hills (Roppongi): 50+ artworks, rooms flow into each other. Reopened Feb 2024 at this venue.

## How to get there

Hibiya Line to Kamiyacho, or bus; inside Azabudai Hills.

## Tips

Reserve 14+ days ahead — sells out. 2.5–3 h minimum. Choose between this and Planets.
