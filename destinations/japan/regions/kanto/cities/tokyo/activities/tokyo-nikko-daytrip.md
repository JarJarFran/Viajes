# Day trip to Nikko (Toshogu shrine)

## Description

UNESCO shrines and mausoleum of Tokugawa Ieyasu in the mountains: Toshogu, Shinkyo bridge, Kegon falls.

## How to get there

Tobu from Asakusa (Nikko/Tobu-Nikko) or JR Tohoku shinkansen to Utsunomiya + JR Nikko line.

## Tips

Full day (~2 h each way). January: cold/snow possible, fewer crowds. Book Tobu limited express seats.
