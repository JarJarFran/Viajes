# Edo-Tokyo Museum (Ryogoku)

## Description

Tokyo/Edo history from 1603: full-scale reconstructions of merchant houses, a replica Nihonbashi bridge. Next to the sumo arena (Ryogoku Kokugikan).

## How to get there

JR Sobu Line or Toei Oedo Line to Ryogoku, 3 min walk.

## Tips

Pair with a sumo tournament day (Jan tournament: Jan 11–25, 2026) or just the Kokugikan exterior.
