# Day trip to Yokohama (Chinatown, Minato Mirai)

## Description

Port city 30 min away: Japan's biggest Chinatown, Minato Mirai waterfront, Landmark Tower, Cup Noodles Museum Yokohama, ramen museum.

## How to get there

JR Tokaido/Shonan lines from Tokyo or Shinagawa to Yokohama.

## Tips

Easy half/full day. NYE 2025 had fireworks at Yokohama bay; winter illuminations along the waterfront.
