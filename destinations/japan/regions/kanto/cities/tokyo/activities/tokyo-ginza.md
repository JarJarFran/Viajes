# Ginza (shopping & depachika)

## Description

Tokyo's luxury district: flagship stores, department stores (Mitsukoshi, Matsuya) with legendary basement food halls, art galleries.

## How to get there

Ginza Station (Ginza/Marunouchi/Hibiya lines).

## Tips

Weekends the main street is car-free 12:00–17:00. Kabukiza theater nearby.
