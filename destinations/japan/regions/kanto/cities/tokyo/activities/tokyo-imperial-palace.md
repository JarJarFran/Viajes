# Imperial Palace East Gardens

## Description

Former Edo Castle grounds: Nijubashi bridge, East Gardens with the ruins of the castle tower, moats.

## How to get there

Tokyo Metro Tozai Line to Takebashi Station, or JR Tokyo Station (15 min walk).

## Tips

Free entry with bag check. Check Imperial Household Agency calendar for New Year closures.
