# Shimokitazawa

## Description

Bohemian neighborhood south of Shibuya: vintage thrift stores, record shops, live houses, cafes.

## How to get there

Odakyu/Inokashira Line to Shimokitazawa Station.

## Tips

Great for vintage hunting; more relaxed than Harajuku.
