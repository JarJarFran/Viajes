# Akihabara (Electric Town)

## Description

Anime, manga, figures, retro games, electronics. Super Potato (retro games), Gundam Cafe, Radio Kaikan, maid cafes (optional).

## How to get there

JR Yamanote/Sobu lines or Hibiya Line to Akihabara Station.

## Tips

Great for anime_manga_and_pop_culture fans. Compare prices before buying; tax-free for tourists over ¥5,000.
