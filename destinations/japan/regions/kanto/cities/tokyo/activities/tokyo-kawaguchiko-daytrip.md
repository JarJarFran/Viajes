# Day trip to Kawaguchiko (Mt Fuji views)

## Description

Lake at the foot of Mt Fuji: Chureito Pagoda (iconic view), ropeway, lake cruise, onsen.

## How to get there

Bus from Shinjuku Busta new south exit, or JR Chuo Line to Otsuki then Fujikyu Railway to Kawaguchiko.

## Tips

Winter is the best season for clear Fuji views. Check bus schedule; book ahead in peak times.
