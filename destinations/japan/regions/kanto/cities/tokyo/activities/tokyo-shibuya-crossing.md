# Shibuya crossing and Hachiko

## Description

The world's most famous pedestrian crossing: up to 3,000 people crossing at once in all directions. Next to the station exit stands the statue of Hachiko, the loyal dog.

## How to get there

- JR Yamanote line, Shonan-Shinjuku line, or metro (Ginza, Hanzomon, Fukutoshin) to Shibuya station.

## Tips

- Watch it from the crossing itself or from above (Shibuya Sky, the Starbucks in the Tsutaya building, or the 2nd floor of Shibuya 109).
- Best at night, with the neon signs on.
