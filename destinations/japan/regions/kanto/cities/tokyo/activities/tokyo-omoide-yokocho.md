# Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane, Shinjuku)

## Description

Yakitori alley under the railway tracks west of Shinjuku Station — smoky, crowded, cheap, atmospheric.

## How to get there

JR Shinjuku Station west exit.

## Tips

Go early or late to find seats. Cash only at many stalls.
