# teamLab Planets (Toyosu)

## Description

Immersive digital art museum: barefoot through water rooms, mirrored flower fields, hanging orchids.

## How to get there

Yurikamome line or bus to Toyosu Market / Shin-Toyosu Station.

## Tips

Book online 1–2 weeks ahead — sells out, especially in peak season. Wear shorts, everything gets wet. Alternative: teamLab Borderless at Azabudai Hills (¥3,600–5,600 dynamic).
