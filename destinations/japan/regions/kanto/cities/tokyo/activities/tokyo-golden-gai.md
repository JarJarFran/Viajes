# Golden Gai (Shinjuku)

## Description

200+ tiny bars in six alleys near Shinjuku Station east exit; each seats ~5–10 people, many themed.

## How to get there

JR Shinjuku Station east exit.

## Tips

Pick bars without cover charge if budget matters; no photos inside most bars. English-friendly ones exist.
