# Sumo morning practice (keiko / asageiko)

## Description

The closest you get to real sumo outside a tournament: wrestlers training in near silence at their stable (heya) in Ryogoku. Since ~2019 most stables no longer accept unguided visitors — a booked small-group tour is the reliable way in (Sumo Experience, Magical Trip, Viator/GetYourGuide; ¥5,000–15,000). Exception: Arashio-beya lets visitors watch free through the windows ~7:30–9:00 (check the stable's practice calendar the same morning; can cancel at short notice).

## How to get there

- Stables cluster in Ryogoku (JR Sobu Line / Toei Oedo Line to Ryogoku Station). Arashio-beya is walkable from the station.

## Tips

- During our trip the January tournament runs Jan 10–24, 2027 — Tokyo stables are closed to visitors then. Options: morning practice on Jan 9 (arrival day, tight), or a staged sumo show with chanko lunch in Ryogoku/Asakusa/Shinjuku (~¥13,000, bookable year-round).
- Strict etiquette: total silence, no food/drink/smoking, remove hats/sunglasses, sit seiza, don't show soles toward the dohyo, phone silent, no flash (some stables ban all photos), no approaching/touching wrestlers, min age 11 on some tours. Breaking rules = being asked to leave.
- Bring a small gift (snacks/drinks) as thanks if watching free from the street.
