# Hama-rikyu Gardens

## Description

Tidal pond garden by Tokyo Bay with teahouse, pine groves, duck ponds; skyscraper backdrop.

## How to get there

Toei Oedo Line to Shiodome or JR Shimbashi; or Toei Asakusa Line to Tsukiji? Use Shiodome/Tsukijishima.

## Tips

Combine with Tsukiji Outer Market breakfast nearby.
