# Harajuku & Takeshita Street

## Description

Youth fashion epicenter next to Meiji Jingu: Takeshita Street's crepes, kawaii shops, and street style; quieter side streets have vintage and cafes.

## How to get there

JR Harajuku Station (Yamanote) or Meiji-jingumae (Chiyoda/Fukutoshin).

## Tips

Pair with Meiji Jingu morning. Sunday: the street sometimes closes to cars. Crepe stands ~¥500–800.
