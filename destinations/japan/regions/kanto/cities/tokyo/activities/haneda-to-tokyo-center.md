# Haneda Airport (HND) → central Tokyo (departure Jan 23)

## Description

Departure transfer on Jan 23 (if flying out of HND).

## How to get there

HND terminals; Keikyu/monorail from Terminal 3 domestic.

## Tips

HND is 15 km from the center — much closer than NRT. Allow 60–90 min door-to-gate.
