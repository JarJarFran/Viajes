# Pola Museum of Art

## Description

Forest-set museum in Sengokuhara with a large collection of Western and Japanese paintings (Impressionists among them). Exhibition 'New Eyes — Monet and 21st-century art' (Monet 100th anniversary, museum's 25th) runs 17 Jun 2026 – 7 Apr 2027.

## How to get there

Sengokuhara area; reached by bus from Gora or Hakone-Yumoto (route details not confirmed here).

## Tips

Online tickets are sold through the official e-tix page.
