# Hakone Shrine

## Description

Shinto shrine in the forest on the shore of Lake Ashi, known for its red 'Peace' torii standing in the water, a popular photo spot seen from the sightseeing ships.

## How to get there

Short walk from Moto-Hakone port (Lake Ashi cruise / buses from Hakone-Yumoto).

## Tips

The lakeside torii draws a photo queue at busy times; arrive early.
