# Lake Ashi sightseeing cruise (Hakone pirate ships)

## Description

Pirate-ship styled sightseeing boats crossing Lake Ashi between Togendai (ropeway terminus) and the ports of Hakone-machi and Moto-Hakone, with views of Mt. Fuji and Hakone Shrine's lakeside torii.

## How to get there

Togendai port (Hakone Ropeway), Hakone-machi port or Moto-Hakone port (buses to Hakone-Yumoto/Odawara).

## Tips

Timetable and last sailings were not confirmed in this research; check them before leaving Owakudani.
