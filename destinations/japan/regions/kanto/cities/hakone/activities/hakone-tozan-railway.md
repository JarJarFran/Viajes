# Hakone Tozan Railway

## Description

Mountain railway climbing from Hakone-Yumoto to Gora through steep switchbacks; the first leg of the classic Hakone loop.

## How to get there

Starts at Hakone-Yumoto Station (Odakyu Romancecar terminus); Odawara–Hakone-Yumoto trains also run.

## Tips

Start the loop in the morning so the ropeway (last service 17:00, 16:15 in Dec–Jan) and the last ships are not missed.
