# Hakone Tozan Cable Car (Gora–Sounzan)

## Description

Funicular of about 10 minutes from Gora up the slope to Sounzan, where it connects with the Hakone Ropeway.

## How to get there

Gora Station (terminus of the Hakone Tozan Railway).

## Tips

The ¥430 fare comes from a 2025 guide; confirm the current fare at the station if not using the Freepass.
