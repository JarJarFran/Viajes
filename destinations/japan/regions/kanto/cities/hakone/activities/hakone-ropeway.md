# Hakone Ropeway (Sounzan–Owakudani–Togendai)

## Description

Gondola line from Sounzan over the steaming volcanic valley of Owakudani down to Togendai on Lake Ashi; Mt. Fuji is visible on clear days.

## How to get there

Sounzan Station (top of the Tozan Cable Car) or Togendai (Lake Ashi pier, buses from Odawara/Hakone-Yumoto).

## Tips

Some sites still list older section fares (e.g. ¥1,050, ¥420); the post-October-2025 uniform ¥2,000 fare is the current one. Check the operating status on the day: closures for wind or volcanic gas are announced the same day.
