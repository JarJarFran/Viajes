# Owakudani

## Description

Active volcanic valley with fumaroles and sulphur vents formed by an eruption of Mt. Hakone; known for kuro-tamago, eggs boiled black in the hot springs.

## How to get there

Owakudani Station on the Hakone Ropeway (middle station), or by bus/car.

## Tips

People with asthma or heart conditions are usually advised to avoid the gas; black eggs (¥500 for 4) can sell out on busy days.
