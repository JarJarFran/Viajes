# Hakone

Hot-spring and mountain resort area in Kanagawa Prefecture, about 1.5 hours from Shinjuku, inside the Fuji-Hakone-Izu National Park. It is usually toured as a loop of mountain railway, cable car, ropeway over the volcanic valley of Owakudani and a sightseeing cruise on Lake Ashi, combined with art museums such as the Hakone Open-Air Museum and the Pola Museum of Art.

## How to get there

- Odakyu Romancecar (limited express) Shinjuku → Hakone-Yumoto: fare ¥1,270 + limited-express fee ¥1,200 = ¥2,470 one way (ticketless limited-express fee ¥1,150; with an IC card the fare is ¥1,261, total ¥2,411). 75–107 min under the 14 Mar 2026 timetable (about 85 min typical). No 2026 Odakyu fare revision was found.
- Without the Romancecar, ordinary Odakyu trains cost only the ¥1,270 fare (Shinjuku → Hakone-Yumoto, changing at Odawara); the fastest Shinjuku–Odawara time returned to 59 min (Romancecar) with the March 2026 timetable. Odawara → Hakone-Yumoto on a Romancecar keeps a ¥200 limited-express fee.
- The Hakone Freepass includes a round trip on Odakyu from the departure station to Odawara and unlimited rides in Hakone; the Romancecar limited-express fee is extra.

## Getting around

- Hakone Freepass (prices since 1 Oct 2025, unchanged in 2026): from Shinjuku 2 days ¥7,100 adult / ¥1,600 child, 3 days ¥7,500 adult / ¥1,850 child; prices differ by departure station (no 1-day version). Unlimited rides on 8 transport modes including the Hakone Tozan Railway, Tozan Cable Car, Hakone Ropeway and the Lake Ashi sightseeing (pirate) ships, plus discounts at about 70 facilities. Not valid on Izu-Hakone buses.
- Individual adult fares after the 1 Oct 2025 revision: Hakone Ropeway ¥2,000 one way (uniform fare) / ¥3,000 round trip; Hakone sightseeing cruise (pirate ship) Togendai ↔ Hakone-machi or Moto-Hakone ¥1,700 one way, Hakone-machi ↔ Moto-Hakone ¥600; Tozan Cable Car Gora ↔ Sounzan about 10 min, ¥430 (figure from a 2025 guide). Hakone Tozan Railway section fares were not confirmed.
- The 2-day version of the ship + ropeway unlimited pass was discontinued with the October 2025 revision (a 'digital pirate ship & ropeway unlimited pass' is still sold online; its price was not confirmed).
- Classic loop: Hakone-Yumoto → (Tozan Railway) Gora → (cable car) Sounzan → (ropeway) Owakudani → Togendai → (ship) Hakone-machi / Moto-Hakone → bus back to Hakone-Yumoto or Odawara.

## Local food

- Kuro-tamago (black eggs): eggs boiled in Owakudani's volcanic hot springs, shells turned black by the sulphur; sold at Owakudani, 4 for ¥500 (2026). Can sell out on busy days.

## Seasons

- Hakone Ropeway hours: 9:00–17:00 from February to November, 9:00–16:15 in December–January.
- The ropeway stops in strong wind (about 30 m/s or more, whole line or part of it) and when volcanic activity or volcanic-gas levels rise; Owakudani's park area is then closed to visitors. Check the day's operating status before starting the loop.
- Pola Museum of Art closes for exhibition changes (e.g. 1–16 Jun 2026) and on 1 Dec 2026; the Hakone Open-Air Museum is open every day.
