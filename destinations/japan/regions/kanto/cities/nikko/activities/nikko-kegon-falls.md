# Kegon Falls

## Description

97 m waterfall draining Lake Chuzenji in Oku-Nikko, often partly frozen in January. Free upper viewpoint; an elevator goes down to the basin platform.

## How to get there

Tobu bus from Tobu-Nikko / JR Nikko stations to Chuzenjiko Onsen (~45–50 min up the Irohazaka switchbacks), then 5 min walk.

## Tips

Elevator Dec–Feb ~9:00–16:30, ¥600. The Akechidaira Ropeway is closed for renovation from Jan 16, 2026 to Aug 31, 2027. Allow ~2 h extra for the bus round trip from the shrines; roads can be icy.
