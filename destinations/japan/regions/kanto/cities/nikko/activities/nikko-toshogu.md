# Nikko Toshogu

## Description

Lavishly carved and gilded mausoleum of Tokugawa Ieyasu (1617, rebuilt 1636), part of the UNESCO "Shrines and Temples of Nikko": Yomeimon gate, the three wise monkeys, the sleeping cat and the stairs to Ieyasu's tomb.

## How to get there

From Tobu-Nikko / JR Nikko stations: bus to Shinkyo or Omotesando (~10 min), or a 30–40 min uphill walk past the Shinkyo bridge.

## Tips

Winter closing time is 16:00 (last entry 15:30); start here in the morning. Sources disagree on the fee (¥1,300–1,700; ¥1,600 is the most cited) — check toshogu.jp. Snow and ice on the stone stairs in January.
