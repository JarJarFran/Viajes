# Rinno-ji Temple

## Description

Nikko's main Buddhist temple, founded in the 8th century: the Sanbutsudo hall with three giant gilded statues, and the Shoyoen garden. Part of the UNESCO site, next to Toshogu.

## How to get there

Between the Shinkyo bridge and Toshogu (5 min walk from either).

## Tips

Sanbutsudo ¥400, garden +¥300. Winter hours 8:00–16:00.
