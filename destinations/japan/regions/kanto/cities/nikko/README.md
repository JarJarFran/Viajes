# Nikko

Mountain town 2 h north of Tokyo with the UNESCO "Shrines and Temples of Nikko" (Toshogu, Rinno-ji, Futarasan) and, 45 min higher up, Lake Chuzenji and Kegon Falls (Oku-Nikko). Cold and often snowy in January; temples close at 16:00 in winter.

## How to get there

- Tobu Limited Express from Asakusa to Tobu-Nikko (~1 h 50 min); JR via Utsunomiya to JR Nikko.
- From the stations: Tobu buses to the shrine area (~10 min) and to Chuzenjiko Onsen / Kegon Falls (~45–50 min).

## What to see and do

- [Nikko Toshogu Shrine](activities/nikko-toshogu.md) — ~¥1,600; winter 9:00–16:00
- [Rinno-ji Temple (Sanbutsudo)](activities/nikko-rinnoji.md) — ¥400; winter 8:00–16:00
- [Kegon Falls](activities/nikko-kegon-falls.md) — free viewpoint, elevator ¥600; the Akechidaira Ropeway is closed until Aug 2027
