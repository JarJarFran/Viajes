# Sofukuji Temple

## Description

Obaku Zen temple founded in 1629 for Nagasaki's Chinese residents, built in Chinese style on a hillside behind the bright red Ryugumon gate (rebuilt 1849). Its Buddha Hall, cut in China and erected here in 1646, is one of the city's oldest buildings.

## How to get there

Short walk from the Sofukuji tram stop, the terminus of tram lines 1 and 4.

## Tips

English descriptions are posted around the inner grounds.
