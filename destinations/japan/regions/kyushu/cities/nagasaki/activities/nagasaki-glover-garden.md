# Glover Garden

## Description

Open-air museum on the hillside where Western merchants settled after 1859, centred on the Former Glover House, Japan's oldest wooden Western-style building, home of the Scottish merchant Thomas Glover. Other Western residences relocated here can be entered, and the garden overlooks the harbour and shipyards.

## How to get there

5 min walk from the Ouratenshudo tram stop (line 5); entrance next to Oura Church.

## Tips

Admission is ¥1,300 (also listed by a ticket reseller for 2026); older guides quote much lower prices. Evening openings until 20:00/21:30 on many days give night views of the harbour.
