# Dejima

## Description

Former artificial island built in 1636 for Portuguese traders and then home to the Dutch trading post, the only Westerners allowed in Japan during two centuries of isolation. Residences, warehouses, walls and gates have been restored or reconstructed on the now land-locked site.

## How to get there

Next to the Dejima tram stop (line 1, about 5 min from Nagasaki Station).

## Tips

The ¥1,100 fee is from japan-guide (not re-confirmed elsewhere); older sources show lower prices. Open until 21:00, so it fits after other sights close.
