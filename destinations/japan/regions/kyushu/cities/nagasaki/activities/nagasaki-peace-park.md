# Nagasaki Peace Park and Hypocenter Park

## Description

Memorial complex for the atomic bombing of 9 August 1945, made up of the Hypocenter Park with a black monolith marking the epicentre and a damaged pillar of the former Urakami Cathedral, and the Memorial Park north across the street with the Peace Statue, a fountain and memorials from around the world.

## How to get there

Short walk from the Peace Park (Heiwa Koen) tram stop, about 10 min by tram line 1 or 3 from Nagasaki Station.

## Tips

The Atomic Bomb Museum and the underground National Peace Memorial Hall stand on the hill above the Hypocenter Park; visit them together.
