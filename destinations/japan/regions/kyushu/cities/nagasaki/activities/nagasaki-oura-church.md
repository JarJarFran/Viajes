# Oura Church

## Description

Catholic church built in 1864 by a French missionary, considered the oldest standing Christian church in Japan and the first Western-style building designated a national treasure. It is dedicated to the 26 Christians executed in Nagasaki in 1597; the adjacent Christian Museum covers the history of Christianity in Japan.

## How to get there

5 min walk from the Ouratenshudo tram stop (line 5), next to the entrance of Glover Garden.

## Tips

Museum explanations are mostly in Japanese. Visit together with Glover Garden.
