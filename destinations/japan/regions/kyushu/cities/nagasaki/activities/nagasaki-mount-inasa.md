# Mount Inasa Night View

## Description

333 m mountain next to central Nagasaki whose night view is ranked among Japan's three best, alongside Mount Hakodate and Mount Rokko. The summit has an observation deck with a restaurant and is reached by a 5-minute ropeway ride, bus plus Slope Car, or car.

## How to get there

Ropeway lower station 5 min by Nagasaki Bus from Nagasaki Station (¥160) or 15 min walk from the Takaramachi tram stop; free evening shuttle bus from selected hotels and Nagasaki Station.

## Tips

The summit car park closes to regular cars on busy evenings; taxis from Nagasaki Station cost just over ¥2,000. Check the ropeway's maintenance period in June.
