# Gunkanjima (Hashima) Landing Tour

## Description

Abandoned coal-mining island about 20 km from Nagasaki Port, once home to more than 5,000 people on 480 × 150 m until the mine closed in April 1974. Several companies run tours that land at a dock on the southern end and visit three observation decks with Japanese-speaking guides; the cruise passes the Mitsubishi shipyards.

## How to get there

Tours leave from Nagasaki Port Ferry Terminal (Ohato tram stop, 3 min by line 1 from Nagasaki Station) or Tokiwa Terminal (Ourakaikandori tram stop).

## Tips

Reserve ahead, especially on weekends and holidays. The Gunkanjima Digital Museum near Oura Church is an alternative when boats cannot sail.
