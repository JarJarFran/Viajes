# Nagasaki Atomic Bomb Museum

## Description

Museum on the bombing of Nagasaki and its aftermath, with the mission to inform future generations about the horror of war. Next to it is the Nagasaki National Peace Memorial Hall for the Atomic Bomb Victims, built mainly underground with water and light.

## How to get there

On the hill above the Hypocenter Park, a short walk from the Peace Park (Heiwa Koen) tram stop (tram line 1 or 3, about 10 min from Nagasaki Station).

## Tips

Combine with the Peace Park and Urakami Cathedral in the Urakami district, north of the centre.
