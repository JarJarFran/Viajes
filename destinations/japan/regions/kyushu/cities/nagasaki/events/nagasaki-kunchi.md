# Nagasaki Kunchi

## Description

Autumn festival of Suwa Shrine, celebrated for about 400 years with dances, Chinese-style dragon dances and ship-shaped floats by city districts, each performing only once every seven years. Groups also parade through the streets visiting shops and households.
