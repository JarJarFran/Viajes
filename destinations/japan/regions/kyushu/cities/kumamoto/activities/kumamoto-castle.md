# Kumamoto Castle

## Description

Castle whose keep reopened after the 2016 earthquakes. Visitors reach it via a raised special viewing passage while stone-wall restoration continues. The 28 July 2026 earthquake brought down about 40 more stone-wall sections. The keep and the paid viewing areas had no major damage, and the castle reopened on 13 Aug 2026.

## Tips

Since the Aug 2026 reopening, part of the small keep's ground floor and some damaged stone walls are off-limits. A reseller notes that on weekdays entry and exit are only through the south gate (Minami-guchi). Reports disagree on the number of collapsed wall sections (27, 39, about 40) and on last-entry time, so check the official site.
