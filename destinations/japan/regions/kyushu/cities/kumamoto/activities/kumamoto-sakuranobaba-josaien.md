# Sakuranobaba Josaien & Wakuwakuza

## Description

Food and souvenir complex at the foot of Kumamoto Castle. It includes Wakuwakuza, a castle-history museum.

## Tips

Buy the castle + Wakuwakuza combined ticket (¥850). The Wakuwakuza price comes from a listing that may be outdated.
