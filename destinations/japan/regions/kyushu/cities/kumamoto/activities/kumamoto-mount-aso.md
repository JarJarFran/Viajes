# Mount Aso (Nakadake crater, Kusasenri)

## Description

Active volcano in a vast caldera east of Kumamoto, usually visited as a day trip. JMA kept eruption alert level 2 into late October 2026. Crater viewing is restricted within about 1 km of Nakadake first crater.

## Tips

A 2026 blog says crater viewing was restricted after a sightseeing helicopter crashed into the crater on 20 Jan 2026, and the alert level rose to 2 on 21 June 2026, with no reopening date announced. Check JMA and aso-volcano.jp before going. Access from Kumamoto city was not re-verified.
