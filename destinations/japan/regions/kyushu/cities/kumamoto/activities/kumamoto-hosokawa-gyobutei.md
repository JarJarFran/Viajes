# Former Hosokawa Gyobu Residence

## Description

Samurai residence of a Hosokawa clan branch near Kumamoto Castle. The building is closed after earthquake damage, and the garden opens seasonally.

## Tips

Sources disagree on the garden openings and hours (rurubu says 8:30-20:00, Jorudan says 9:00-17:00), so check with the Kumamoto Castle general office.
