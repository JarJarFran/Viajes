# Shofukuji Temple

## Description

Japan's first Zen temple, founded in 1195 by the priest Eisai, who brought Rinzai Zen from China. Visitors walk the quiet grounds past the Sanmon Gate (rebuilt 1911), a pond and the Butsuden Hall; several smaller temples stand nearby.

## How to get there

Short walk from Gion Station (Kuko Subway Line, 1 stop/¥210 from Hakata); 15–20 min walk from Hakata Station.

## Tips

Buildings are viewed from outside only. Combine with Kushida Shrine and the other temples of the Gion/Hakata temple district.
