# Fukuoka Castle Ruins (Maizuru Park)

## Description

Remains of the Kuroda clan's 17th-century castle, once the largest on Kyushu and mostly demolished after the Meiji Restoration. Stone walls, a few gates and turrets and parts of the moat survive in Maizuru Park, with views from the former main citadel.

## How to get there

10–15 min walk from Ohori Koen Station (Kuko Subway Line).

## Tips

Maizuru Park is the city's most popular cherry-blossom spot; seasonal illumination events charge admission. Easy to combine with Ohori Park.
