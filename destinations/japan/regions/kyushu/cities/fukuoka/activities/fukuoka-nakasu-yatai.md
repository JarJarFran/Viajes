# Nakasu Yatai (Food Stalls)

## Description

Open-air food stalls seating about seven or eight people each, serving yakitori, oden, Hakata ramen and drinks. The best-known row of around 20 stalls lines the river at the southern end of Nakasu Island; others are scattered around Tenjin and the city centre.

## How to get there

Short walk from Kushida Shrine Station (Nanakuma Subway Line, ¥210 from Hakata).

## Tips

Expect queues at popular stalls on Friday and Saturday evenings. Stalls stay closed in heavy rain or storms.
