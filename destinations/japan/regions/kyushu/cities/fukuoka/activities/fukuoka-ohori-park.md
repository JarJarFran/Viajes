# Ohori Park

## Description

City park built 1926–1929 around a large pond that was once part of Fukuoka Castle's moat, modelled on China's West Lake, with three islands linked by stone bridges and a 2 km path around the water. The Fukuoka Art Museum and a small paid Japanese garden stand at its southern side.

## How to get there

A few steps from Ohori Koen Station (Kuko Subway Line, 10 min and ¥260 from Hakata).

## Tips

The Japanese Garden is closed Mondays (Tuesday if Monday is a holiday) and 29 Dec–3 Jan; the Art Museum is closed Mondays (Tuesday if a holiday) and 28 Dec–4 Jan. The Fukuoka Castle ruins are next door.
