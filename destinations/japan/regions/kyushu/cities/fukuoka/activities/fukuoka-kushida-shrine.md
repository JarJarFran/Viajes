# Kushida Shrine

## Description

Main shrine of Hakata and host shrine of the Hakata Gion Yamakasa festival; the dawn float race on 15 July starts in front of it and the floats circle a pole in its courtyard. The small Hakata History Museum (Hakata Rekishikan) on the grounds shows festival-related items.

## How to get there

Short walk from Kushida Shrine Station (Nanakuma Subway Line); 15–20 min walk from Hakata Station; next to Canal City Hakata.

## Tips

Hakata History Museum: 10:00-17:00, closed Mondays, ¥300 according to a 2019 visitor listing (not re-confirmed). Combine with the Nakasu yatai and Canal City, all within walking distance.
