# Fukuoka Tower and Seaside Momochi

## Description

Waterfront district on reclaimed land built for the 1989 Asia Pacific Expo. The 234 m Fukuoka Tower, covered in over 8,000 mirrors, has an observation deck at 123 m; nearby are the artificial Momochihama beach, the Fukuoka City Museum and Fukuoka PayPay Dome.

## How to get there

15–20 min walk from Nishijin, Tojinmachi or Fujisaki subway stations, or bus (e.g. W1) from Tenjin (15–25 min) or Hakata (20–30 min, ¥260).

## Tips

Fukuoka City Museum is closed Mondays (Tuesday if Monday is a holiday) and 28 Dec–4 Jan. Sunset and night views from the tower are popular.
