# Dazaifu Tenmangu Shrine

## Description

The most important Tenmangu shrine together with Kyoto's Kitano Tenmangu, built over the grave of the scholar Sugawara Michizane (d. 903) and popular with students before exams. A heart-shaped pond with three arched bridges leads to the 1591 main hall and the 'flying plum tree'; about 6,000 plum trees bloom from late February. The three-year renovation of the main hall was completed in May 2026.

## How to get there

5 min walk from Nishitetsu Dazaifu Station (from Nishitetsu Fukuoka/Tenjin via Futsukaichi, 25–40 min, ¥480) or Dazaifu Liner bus from Hakata Bus Center (about 40 min, ¥800).

## Tips

Dazaifu Tenmangu Museum is closed Mondays (Tuesday if Monday is a holiday); Kanko Historical Museum is closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays. A path from the shrine leads to the Kyushu National Museum; exam season brings large crowds.
