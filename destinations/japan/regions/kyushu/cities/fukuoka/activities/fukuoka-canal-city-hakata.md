# Canal City Hakata

## Description

Large shopping and entertainment complex with about 250 shops, cafes and restaurants, a theatre, cinemas and the Grand Hyatt Fukuoka around an artificial canal. The 5th-floor Ramen Stadium gathers eight ramen shops from across Japan, including Hakata ramen.

## How to get there

Short walk from Kushida Shrine Station (Nanakuma Subway Line, 1 stop/¥210 from Hakata); about 15 min walk from Hakata Station or 20 min from Tenjin.

## Tips

During Hakata Gion Yamakasa (1–14 July) one of the large decorative floats stands here.
