# Hakata Gion Yamakasa

## Description

Festival of Kushida Shrine in Hakata, climaxing on 15 July with a dawn time-trial race in which seven neighbourhood teams carry one-ton kakiyama floats along a 5 km course. Over ten-metre decorative floats stand around the city during the first half of July.
