# Beppu

Beppu is a hot-spring city in Oita known for its 'hells' (jigoku), a circuit of colourful boiling hot springs. It is reached from Fukuoka by the Sonic limited express.

## How to get there

- From Hakata (Fukuoka): limited express Sonic, no change, about 2h, ¥6,380 unreserved (Sep 2025). Shinkansen to Kokura + Sonic about 1h40, ¥6,640 unreserved (a 2026 page quotes ¥6,500 / about 95 min). Reserved seats cost ¥200 less in the off-peak season and ¥200 more in peak seasons.

## Getting around

- Kamenoi Bus hells sightseeing bus: guided tour including the hells' combined admission, ¥4,000 (adults), about 3 hours.

## What to see and do

- [Beppu Hells (Jigoku Meguri)](activities/beppu-hells.md) — Combined ticket ¥2,000 (high school and older), ¥1,000 (elementary/junior high) — June 2023 listing; guided Kamenoi hells bus ¥4,000 incl. admission
