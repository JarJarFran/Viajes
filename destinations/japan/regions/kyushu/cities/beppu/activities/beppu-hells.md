# Beppu Hells (Jigoku Meguri)

## Description

Circuit of striking hot springs ('hells') such as Umi Jigoku, visited with one combined ticket. Kamenoi Bus runs a guided sightseeing bus that includes admission.

## Tips

The combined-ticket price comes from a June 2023 listing and was not re-confirmed for 2026. The guided bus (about 3 h) saves arranging transport between the two hell clusters.
