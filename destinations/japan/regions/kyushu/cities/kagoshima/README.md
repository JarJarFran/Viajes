# Kagoshima

Kagoshima is Kyushu's southernmost major city and capital of Kagoshima Prefecture, often compared with Naples for its mild climate, palm-lined streets and the constantly smoking Sakurajima volcano across the bay. It was the seat of the powerful Shimazu clan, central to the Meiji Restoration, and is a base for Kirishima, the Satsuma Peninsula and ferries to Yakushima and Amami.

## How to get there

- From Fukuoka (Hakata): Kyushu Shinkansen to Kagoshima-Chuo, Mizuho about 1h16–1h20.
- From Tokyo by air: Haneda–Kagoshima by JAL, ANA, Skymark and Solaseed, Narita–Kagoshima by Jetstar; about 2 h flight. Regular JAL/ANA about ¥56,000, discount ¥19,000–33,000; Solaseed about 15% cheaper; Skymark and Jetstar cheaper still. Lowest fares seen for Nov–Dec 2026: Jetstar ¥7,940, Skymark ¥10,429, Solaseed ¥11,411, JAL ¥12,921, ANA ¥14,177.
- Kagoshima Airport is far from the centre: airport bus 40–60 min, ¥1,500, stopping at Kagoshima-Chuo Station and Tenmonkan.
- From Tokyo by train: Tokaido, Sanyo and Kyushu Shinkansen with one change (usually Shin-Osaka or Hakata), about 7 h, around ¥32,000.

## Getting around

- Two tram lines serve Kagoshima-Chuo Station, Kagoshima Station and the Tenmonkan downtown in between: flat ¥170 per ride.
- City View Bus (tourist loop): Kagoshima-Chuo Station to Senganen, Shiroyama Park and the Sakurajima ferry terminal, every 30 min, ¥230 per ride or ¥600 for a 1-day pass.
- Cute Pass: unlimited trams, City View Bus, Sakurajima Island View Bus, city buses and the Sakurajima Ferry, 1 day ¥1,300 or 2 consecutive days ¥1,900, plus some admission discounts.
- Senganen Station (opened March 2025): 8 min from Kagoshima-Chuo, ¥240, 1–2 trains per hour, covered by JR passes.
- Sakurajima Ferry ¥250 per adult, 15 min, 4:00–23:30 (no overnight service since 1 Oct 2025). On Sakurajima the Island View Bus loops every 30 min from the ferry terminal to Yunohira (fare by distance or ¥500 1-day pass).

## Local food

- Kurobuta (black Berkshire pork, as tonkatsu or shabu-shabu) and Kuroushi (Kagoshima black beef), advertised by many downtown restaurants.
- Satsumaage — deep-fried minced-fish cakes (bonito, mackerel or sardine) with ginger or vegetables.
- Kibinago — small silver herring served raw as sashimi with vinegared miso; torisashi — very fresh raw chicken with ginger and soy sauce.
- Tonkotsu (Kagoshima style) — pork and miso stew slow-cooked with shochu until the bones are soft.
- Shirokuma — shaved ice with condensed milk and fruit, at Tenmonkan Mujaki (honten about 11:00-18:30/19:00, irregular holidays); Satsuma shochu made from sweet potatoes.

## Seasons

- Mild climate year-round; Sengan-en's early Kanhizakura cherries open from late January, other varieties through mid April.
- Sakurajima erupts in small bursts often several times a day; approaching within 2 km of the craters is prohibited.
- Kagoshima Aquarium closes on the first Monday of December and the following three days.
