# Kagoshima Aquarium (Io World)

## Description

Seven-floor aquarium opened in 1997 beside the Sakurajima ferry terminal, focused on local marine life and the islands to the south. Its Kuroshio Tank holds a whale shark, rays and schools of fish; there are also sea otters, coral reefs and three dolphin shows a day.

## How to get there

5 min walk from the Suizokukan-guchi tram stop (¥170); next to the Sakurajima ferry terminal.

## Tips

Pairs well with a Sakurajima ferry trip from the adjacent terminal.
