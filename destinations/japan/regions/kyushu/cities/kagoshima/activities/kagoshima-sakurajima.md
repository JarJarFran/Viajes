# Sakurajima

## Description

Active volcano across Kinko Bay from central Kagoshima. The city-run Sakurajima Ferry runs to it from early morning to late night.

## How to get there

Sakurajima Ferry from Kagoshima to Sakurajima Port.

## Tips

Fares are paid at Sakurajima Port, not at the Kagoshima side. The ferry no longer runs overnight.
