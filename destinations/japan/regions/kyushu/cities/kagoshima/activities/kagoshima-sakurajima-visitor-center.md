# Sakurajima Visitor Center and Nagisa Lava Trail

## Description

Small free visitor centre near Sakurajima's ferry terminal with models, photos, videos and largely English explanations of the volcano. Beside it, about 3 km of trails cross the 1914 lava field along the shore, and the very long, free Nagisa Park foot bath looks across the bay to the city.

## How to get there

Short walk from Sakurajima Ferry Terminal; the ferry (15 min, ¥250) leaves from a terminal 10 min walk from JR Kagoshima Station or 5 min from the Suizokukan-guchi tram stop.

## Tips

For crater views take the Island View Bus (¥500 day pass) to Yunohira Observation Point (350 m, about 2.5 km from the crater) or rent a car; the Kurokami buried torii shows the scale of the 1914 eruption. Japan-guide still describes the ferry as 24-hour, but it has run 4:00–23:30 since 1 Oct 2025.
