# Sengan-en Garden and Shoko Shuseikan

## Description

Shimazu clan landscape garden built in 1658, using Sakurajima and Kagoshima Bay as borrowed scenery, with the Iso Residence preserved as it was used in the 1890s. The reverberatory furnace foundations in the garden and the Shoko Shuseikan machinery factory (now a museum) are World Heritage parts of the Sites of Japan's Meiji Industrial Revolution.

## How to get there

Next to Senganen Station (opened March 2025; 8 min from Kagoshima-Chuo, ¥240); or City View Bus about 50 min from Kagoshima-Chuo Station.

## Tips

The Iso Residence interior can be visited. The World Heritage charcoal kiln and sluice gate lie several km up in the hills and are hard to reach without a car.
