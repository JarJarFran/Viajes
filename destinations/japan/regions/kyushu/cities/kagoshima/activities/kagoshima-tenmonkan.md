# Tenmonkan

## Description

Kagoshima's central downtown shopping, dining and nightlife area, halfway between Kagoshima-Chuo and Kagoshima stations. It is the place for local restaurants serving kurobuta, satsumaage and shochu, and for Mujaki, the shop known for the shirokuma shaved-ice dessert.

## How to get there

Tenmonkan-dori tram stop (¥170 flat fare); Mujaki is about 2–3 min on foot from the stop. Airport buses and port buses also stop at Tenmonkan.

## Tips

Mujaki closes on irregular days; its hours differ slightly between listings (last order about 18:15–18:30).
