# Shiroyama Park Observatory

## Description

Park on the 107 m Mount Shiroyama, a former castle fortification, whose observatory looks over downtown Kagoshima, Kagoshima Bay and Sakurajima. It was the site of the final battle of the 1877 Satsuma Rebellion, and Saigo Takamori is said to have made his last stand at Saigo Cave in the park.

## How to get there

City View Bus about 25 min from Kagoshima-Chuo Station.

## Tips

The night view is good on clear, moonlit nights. The nearby Castle Park Hotel's Satsuma no Yu outdoor bath is open to day visitors 13:00-21:00 for ¥3,500.
