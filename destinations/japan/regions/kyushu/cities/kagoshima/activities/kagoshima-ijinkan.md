# Ijinkan (Former Residence of Foreign Engineers)

## Description

Residence built for the foreign engineers who helped design and build a nearby textile mill, listed as part of the World Heritage Sites of Japan's Meiji Industrial Revolution. It is open to the public and stands a short walk from Sengan-en.

## How to get there

About 200 m west of the Sengan-en entrance (Senganen Station).

## Tips

Easy add-on after Sengan-en.
