# Reimeikan Museum (Kagoshima Prefectural Museum of Culture)

## Description

Museum of Kagoshima's history and culture from ancient to modern times on three floors, including a large diorama of Showa-era downtown. It stands on the site of Tsurumaru (Kagoshima) Castle, surrounded by remaining moat and stone walls at the foot of Shiroyama, near the statue of Saigo Takamori.

## How to get there

5 min walk from the Shiyakusho-mae tram stop (¥170); City View Bus about 35 min from Kagoshima-Chuo Station.

## Tips

Combine with Shiroyama Observatory above it and the Saigo Takamori statue.
