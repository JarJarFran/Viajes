# Nankinmachi (Kobe Chinatown)

## Description

Kobe's compact Chinatown, a few blocks of Chinese restaurants and street-food stalls near Motomachi.

## How to get there

About 5 min walk from JR/Hanshin Motomachi station.

## Tips

Many restaurants close one weekday and some close between lunch and dinner; check individual shops, especially around holidays.
