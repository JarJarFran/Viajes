# Kobe Port Tower

## Description

108 m tower on Kobe harbour, reopened in 2024 after renovation with a newly opened rooftop deck reached by stairs from the 5th-floor observatory. A Moomin collaboration runs 10 Oct–30 Nov 2026.

## Tips

No re-entry. Sources disagree on whether date/time-specific tickets are needed (official FAQ says not; some ticket sites warn of sell-outs) — buy online on busy evenings. Older sites quoting ¥700 are outdated.
