# Arima Onsen – Kin no Yu public bath

## Description

Municipal day-use bath in Arima Onsen, a historic hot-spring town in northern Kobe, using the 'kinsen' (gold spring) water that is clear when it emerges and turns reddish-brown on contact with air. Reopened on 17 March 2026 after renovation.

## Tips

Usually visited as a half-day or day trip from central Kobe or Osaka. Some third-party sites list different hours (9:00–21:00) and closing days (1st/3rd Tuesday); the Kobe city notice for the 2026 reopening gives 8:00–22:00 and 2nd/4th Tuesday.
