# Weathercock House (Kitano Ijinkan)

## Description

Western-style house built as the residence of German trader Gottfried Thomas, the best-known house of the Kitano Ijinkan district of former foreign residences. Closed from October 2023 for seismic reinforcement and reopened to the public on 18 July 2025.

## Tips

Official Kobe city page (Apr 2026) gives closure on the 3rd Tuesday (next day if a holiday); some third-party sites list other closing days (e.g. first Tuesday of February and June) — check before going. The Moegi House combo ticket is better value if visiting both.
