# Mount Rokko (Rokko Cable & Rokko Garden Terrace)

## Description

Mountain reached by the Rokko Cable funicular (in service since 1932); Rokko Garden Terrace near the top has shops and restaurants and is a popular night-view spot.

## How to get there

Rokko Cable Shita station → Rokko Cable Sanjo station, then Rokko Sanjo Bus (Route 1) ~10 min to Rokko Garden Terrace.

## Tips

Rokko Cable was suspended 5 Jan–10 Apr 2026 for renovation and resumed on 11 Apr 2026. Mount Maya (Kikuseidai) is the alternative night-view spot.
