# Kobe Nunobiki Herb Gardens & Ropeway

## Description

Ropeway from central Kobe up to a hillside herb garden. In peak seasons (summer, weekends, Christmas season) it runs into the evening.

## Tips

Fares rose on 1 Apr 2026; older pages still show ¥2,000 adults. Christmas-season fares were not published in sources seen.
