# Ikuta Shrine

## Description

One of Kobe's oldest Shinto shrines, affectionately called 'Ikuta-san', close to Sannomiya station in the downtown entertainment district and popular as a matchmaking (en-musubi) shrine.

## How to get there

About 5 min walk north of Hankyu Kobe-sannomiya (west exit) along Ikuta Road; ~10 min from JR Sannomiya.

## Tips

Shrine car park (120 spaces): ¥500 for the first 60 min, then ¥100 per 10 min.
