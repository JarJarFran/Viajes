# Sorakuen Garden

## Description

Kobe municipal garden (Kobe-shiritsu Sorakuen), less visited than the Kitano and harbour sights. Hosts an azalea (tsutsuji) event in spring and occasional evening openings in summer (e.g. 'Yusuzumi' evening until 20:00).

## Tips

Information as of March 2026; closing days can change, so check before visiting.
