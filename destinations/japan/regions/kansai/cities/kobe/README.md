# Kobe

Port city in Hyogo known for Kobe beef, the Kitano Ijinkan former foreign residences, its Chinatown (Nankinmachi) and the waterfront Meriken Park with the BE KOBE monument. Mount Rokko and the Arima Onsen hot-spring town are within Kobe city limits.

## How to get there

- From Osaka: Hankyu limited express Osaka-umeda → Kobe-sannomiya ~28 min, ¥330; JR Special Rapid (Shinkaisoku) Osaka → Sannomiya ~21–25 min, ¥420; Hanshin Osaka-umeda → Kobe-sannomiya ¥330 (slower, ~40 min).

## Getting around

- Central sights are walkable: Ikuta Shrine is ~5 min north of Hankyu Kobe-sannomiya (west exit) via Ikuta Road, ~10 min from JR Sannomiya; Nankinmachi is ~5 min from JR/Hanshin Motomachi.
- Mount Rokko: Rokko Cable (reopened 11 Apr 2026 after renovation) runs ~7:10–21:10 every 20 min, adults ¥800 one way / ¥1,550 round trip; Rokko Sanjo Bus (Route 1) from Rokko Cable Sanjo station to Rokko Garden Terrace ~10 min (fare not confirmed).
- Hanshin sells a '1-day Pass Rokko Mountain Set' combining rail and mountain transport (current price not confirmed).

## Local food

- Kobe beef — steak/teppanyaki; book ahead. Specialist chains such as Ishida have several shops around Sannomiya.
- Akashiyaki — soft octopus-and-egg dumplings dipped in dashi (not covered in sauce like Osaka takoyaki).
- Sobameshi — fried rice mixed with chopped yakisoba noodles and yakisoba sauce; a guide recommends Kazamatsuri in Sannomiya-cho.
- Nankinmachi (Chinatown) — street food stalls and Chinese restaurants; hours vary by shop.

## Seasons

- Kobe Luminarie light festival: 29 Jan–7 Feb 2027 (Meriken Park, Higashi Yuenchi, former foreign settlement).
- Kobe Matsuri parade/main festival: Sunday 16 May 2027 (2026: 17 May).
- Nunobiki Ropeway and Herb Gardens closed for inspection 23 Jan–12 Feb 2027; Christmas season (7 Nov–25 Dec 2026) has special fares and late hours.
- New Year closures: Sorakuen Garden closed 29 Dec–3 Jan; Kin no Yu (Arima) closed 1 Jan.
- Sorakuen azalea (tsutsuji) event late April–early May (2026: 18 Apr–6 May).
