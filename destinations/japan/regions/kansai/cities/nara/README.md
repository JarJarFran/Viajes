# Nara

Former capital in Nara Prefecture, known for Nara Park with its free-roaming deer and the temples and shrines around it: Todai-ji with its Great Buddha Hall, Kasuga Taisha and Kofuku-ji. The old merchant quarter of Naramachi lies just south of the park, and the city is a common day trip from Kyoto and Osaka.

## How to get there

- From Kyoto: Kintetsu limited express to Kintetsu-Nara about 35 min, ¥1,280 (¥760 fare + ¥520 limited-express supplement); Kintetsu express without supplement about 46 min, ¥760 (2026 fares per a Nara tourism page).
- From Osaka-Namba: Kintetsu limited express to Kintetsu-Nara about 30-34 min, around ¥1,200-1,300 (not confirmed for 2026).
- Sightseeing limited express AONIYOSHI runs Kyoto - Kintetsu-Nara - Osaka-Namba; Osaka-Namba to Kintetsu-Nara listed at ¥1,780 for one adult.

## Getting around

- Nara Park and Naramachi are free to visit; Nara Park is open 24 hours.
- City bus day-pass prices not confirmed in this research.

- [Todai-ji (Great Buddha Hall)](activities/nara-todaiji.md) — ¥800 adults for the Great Buddha Hall (Daibutsuden)

- [Kofuku-ji](activities/nara-kofukuji.md) — National Treasure Museum ¥700-900 depending on source (one lists ¥900 adults, ¥800 teens, ¥500 children); halls ¥500 each (one guide)

- [Isuien Garden](activities/nara-isuien.md) — ¥1,200 adults, includes the Neiraku Museum
- [Naramachi](activities/nara-naramachi.md) — Free to walk

## Seasons

- Autumn colours in Nara Park and its gardens are a peak season.
- Late January: Wakakusa Yamayaki, the burning of Mt Wakakusa's grass slope on the 4th Saturday of January (23 Jan 2027); cancelled in bad weather.
- Todai-ji's Great Buddha Hall keeps shorter hours from November to March (8:00-17:00 instead of 7:30-17:30).
