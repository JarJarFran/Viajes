# Kofuku-ji

## Description

Temple next to Nara Park whose National Treasure Museum displays Buddhist sculpture. Its five-storey pagoda is enclosed for restoration until 2034 (per one guide).

## Tips

Museum fees differ between sources; expect ¥700-900. The pagoda is under scaffolding, so do not plan around photographing it.
