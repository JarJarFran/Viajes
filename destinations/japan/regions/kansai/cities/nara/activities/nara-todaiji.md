# Todai-ji (Great Buddha Hall)

## Description

Temple whose Great Buddha Hall houses Nara's giant bronze Buddha statue. It stands in Nara Park, where deer roam the approach.

## Tips

Arrive at opening to avoid the day-trip crowds from Kyoto and Osaka.
