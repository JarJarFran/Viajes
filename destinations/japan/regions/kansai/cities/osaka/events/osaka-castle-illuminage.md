# Osaka Castle Illuminage (winter light show)

## Description

Projection mapping and lights on the castle grounds, esp. the Nishinomaru Garden west of the tower. The theme changes every season; it runs through New Year into mid-February.

## How to get there

Same as osaka-castle: Morinomiya Station, 10 min walk.

## Tips

Evening entry to the Nishinomaru Garden is ticketed separately; check current hours before going.
