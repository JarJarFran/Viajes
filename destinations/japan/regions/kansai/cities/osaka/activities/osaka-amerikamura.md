# Amerika-mura (America Village)

## Description

Youth fashion hub west of Shinsaibashi: vintage/thrift shops, streetwear, cafes, live music.

## How to get there

Metro Shinsaibashi or Yotsubashi Station.

## Tips

Best for vintage shopping and people-watching; combine with Shinsaibashi arcade.
