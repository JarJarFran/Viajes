# Hozenji Yokocho (lantern alley)

## Description

Narrow cobbled alley just off Dotonbori with lanterns, the moss-covered Fudo Myoo statue (pour water on it and make a wish), tiny bars and restaurants.

## How to get there

Metro Namba / JR Namba Station.

## Tips

One of the calmest spots minutes from the neon chaos. Good for a quiet dinner.
