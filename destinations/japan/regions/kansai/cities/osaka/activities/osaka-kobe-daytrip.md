# Day trip Osaka → Kobe

## Description

Kobe: port, Chinatown (Nankinmachi), Nunobiki herb garden, Mt Rokko night view, wagyu beef.

## How to get there

Osaka/Umeda Station to Sannomiya.

## Tips

Half day minimum. Mt Rokko night view needs a bus/ropeway from Sannomiya (§1,000–1,500 return).
