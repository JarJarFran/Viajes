# Nakazakicho retro district

## Description

Preserved Showa-era alleys north of Umeda with retro kissaten cafes, craft shops, galleries.

## How to get there

Metro Tanimachi Line to Nakazakicho Station.

## Tips

Quiet contrast to Umeda. Good for coffee and browsing.
