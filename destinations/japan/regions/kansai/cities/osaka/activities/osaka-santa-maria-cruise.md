# Santa Maria bay cruise

## Description

Replica of Columbus's ship sailing Osaka Bay, passing under the Tempozan and Minato bridges. Departs hourly by day from Kaiyukan West Pier.

## How to get there

Kaiyukan West Pier (Osakako).

## Tips

Check the schedule — some days don't sail. 10-min Captain Line ferry also links to USJ.
