# Universal Studios Japan (USJ)

## Description

Super Nintendo World, Harry Potter, Hollywood Dream coaster. Full-day theme park across Osaka Bay.

## How to get there

JR Yumesaki Line from Osaka Station to Universal City (~15 min).

## Tips

Buy dated tickets online in advance. Express Passes sell out on busy days. Single-rider lines help. Not covered by Amazing Pass.
