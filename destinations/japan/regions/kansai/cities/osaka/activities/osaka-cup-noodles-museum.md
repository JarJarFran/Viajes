# Cup Noodles Museum Osaka Ikeda

## Description

Free museum where instant ramen was invented, plus the make-your-own-cup workshop (reserve online).

## How to get there

Hankyu Takarazuka Line from Umeda to Ikeda (~20 min).

## Tips

Workshop slots fill up — book ahead. Closed Tuesdays.
