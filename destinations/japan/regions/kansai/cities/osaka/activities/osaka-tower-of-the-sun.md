# Tower of the Sun (Expo '70 Commemorative Park)

## Description

Iconic 1970 Osaka Expo landmark by Taro Okamoto; park is a green escape in Suita, north of the city.

## How to get there

Metro to Banpaku-kinenkoen (Midosuji Line extension) or bus from Senri-Chuo.

## Tips

Interior tower tours need advance booking; the park alone is free and great for a picnic.
