# Osaka → Kyoto (Jan 2 move)

## Description

Our city move on Jan 2. No shinkansen needed — conventional trains are cheaper and land closer to some sights.

## How to get there

JR: Osaka Station or Shin-Osaka; Hankyu: Umeda; Keihan: Yodoyabashi. ICOCA accepted.

## Tips

With luggage, prefer Hankyu to Kawaramachi or JR to Kyoto Station. Forward luggage with takuhaibin if needed.
