# Tsuruhashi Korea Town

## Description

Osaka's Koreatown around Tsuruhashi Station: yakiniku restaurants, Korean groceries, kimchi stalls.

## How to get there

JR Loop Line or Metro Sennichimae Line to Tsuruhashi.

## Tips

Go hungry; share BBQ platters. Lively evening atmosphere.
