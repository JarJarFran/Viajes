# Osaka Museum of History

## Description

Museum inside a modern tower facing Osaka Castle: life-size Edo-period street reconstructions, castle views from the top floors.

## How to get there

Metro Tanimachi 4-chome Line to Tanimachiyonchome, or Chuo Line to Morinomiya.

## Tips

Top floors give free castle + skyline views even without entering the museum.
