# Tempozan Giant Ferris Wheel

## Description

112.5 m wheel over Osaka Bay: views to Akashi Kaikyo Bridge, Kansai Airport, Rokko mountains. Some gondolas have glass floors. 15 min/ride.

## How to get there

Metro Chuo Line to Osakako; next to Kaiyukan.

## Tips

Pair with Kaiyukan + Santa Maria cruise in one Tempozan Harbor Village day.
