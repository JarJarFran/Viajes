# Dotonbori & Shinsaibashi at night

## Description

Osaka's neon canal: the famous Glico running-man sign, endless stalls and restaurants. The quintessential Osaka evening. Takoyaki (Kukuru, Wanaka), okonomiyaki (Mizuno), kushikatsu stalls.

## How to get there

Osaka Metro Namba Station; walk the canal from Namba to Shinsaibashi arcade.

## Tips

Best after dark. The Tombori River Cruise runs evenings (~30 min). Book popular restaurants ahead; queues are part of the fun.
