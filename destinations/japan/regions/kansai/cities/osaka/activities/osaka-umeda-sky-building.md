# Umeda Sky Building (Floating Garden Observatory)

## Description

Twin towers joined by a circular observatory 173 m up, 40 floors over the Kita district. Best city panorama of Osaka at golden hour.

## How to get there

JR Osaka Station / Osaka Metro Umeda (all lines).

## Tips

Arrive ~60 min before sunset for the queue + golden hour. Covered by the Osaka Amazing Pass.
