# Tombori River Cruise

## Description

30-min boat ride along the Dotonbori canal under the neon signs and bridges.

## How to get there

Boarding pier at Tazaemonbashi, near the Glico sign (Namba).

## Tips

Evening cruises are the classic. Book online for timed slots.
