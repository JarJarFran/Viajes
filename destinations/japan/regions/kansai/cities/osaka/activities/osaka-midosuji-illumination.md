# Midosuji Illumination (winter lights)

## Description

~4 km of Midosuji boulevard lit from Umeda (Hanshin-mae) to Namba, ~17:00–01:00. In our trip window it is ON until Dec 31 — we are in Osaka Dec 26–31, so we can walk it.

## How to get there

Any Osaka Metro station from Umeda to Namba on the Midosuji Line; walk the lit stretch at night.

## Tips

Do it on Dec 26–31 (after Dec 31 the street display switches off; some area programmes in Nakanoshima EAST and Namba Parks run to end of Jan / mid-Feb). Last trains end before 01:00.
