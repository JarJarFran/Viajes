# Osaka Central Public Hall & Nakanoshima

## Description

1918 neo-baroque hall on Nakanoshima island; the surrounding park and river walks are classic Osaka. Winter: projection mapping on the facade during Hikari Renaissance (Dec).

## How to get there

Metro Midosuji Line to Yodoyabashi, or Keihan Nakanoshima Line.

## Tips

Nakanoshima EAST waterside illuminations run to end of January.
