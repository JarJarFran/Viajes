# Spa World (Shinsekai onsen)

## Description

Huge spa complex in Shinsekai: indoor/outdoor baths, sauna, rotenburo, themed floors, plus a food court.

## How to get there

Metro Midosuji/Sakaisuji Line to Dobutsuen-mae (next to Tsutenkaku).

## Tips

Tattoos may be restricted in some baths — check policy. Towels for rent. Combine with Shinsekai evening.
