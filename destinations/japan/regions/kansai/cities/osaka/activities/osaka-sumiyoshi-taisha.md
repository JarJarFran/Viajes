# Sumiyoshi Taisha Shrine

## Description

One of Japan's oldest shrines (3rd century), famous for its arched Sorihashi bridge over the pond and unique shrine architecture. Much calmer than Kyoto's big shrines.

## How to get there

Nankai Main Line to Sumiyoshi Taisha Station (10 min walk).

## Tips

Free entry, opens early. Good add-on on the way south of the center; combine with a Tennoji/Shinsekai afternoon.
