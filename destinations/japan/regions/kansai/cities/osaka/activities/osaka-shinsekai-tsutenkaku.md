# Shinsekai & Tsutenkaku Tower

## Description

Retro 1912 district built around the 1956 Tsutenkaku Tower ("tower reaching heaven"). Local specialty: kushikatsu (deep-fried skewers, no double-dipping rule).

## How to get there

Osaka Metro Midosuji/Sakaisuji Line to Dobutsuen-mae or Eneosaka Station.

## Tips

Combine with a walk through nearby Tennoji Park. Less polished than Dotonbori, more local.
