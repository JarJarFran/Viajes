## Description

Indoor LEGO attraction for kids 3–10: Miniland Osaka, 4D cinema, building zones.

## How to get there

Metro Chuo Line to Osakako, inside Tempozan Marketplace 3F.

## Tips

Only if travelling with kids; adults-only not admitted without a child.
