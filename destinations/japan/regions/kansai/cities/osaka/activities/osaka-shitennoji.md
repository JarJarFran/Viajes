# Shitenno-ji Temple

## Description

Japan's oldest officially administered Buddhist temple (593 AD), five-storey pagoda, stone torii gate.

## How to get there

JR Loop Line / Metro to Tennoji or Shitennoji-mae.

## Tips

Free entry. The 21st-of-month flea/antique market fills the grounds (our Osaka dates Dec 26–Jan 2 don't include a 21st).
