# Kuromon Ichiba Market ("Osaka's Kitchen")

## Description

580 m covered arcade with ~150 stalls: sashimi, A5 wagyu skewers, sea urchin, tamagoyaki, sweets. Ideal for a grazing lunch.

## How to get there

Osaka Metro Sakaisuji Line or Nankai Line to Nippombashi Station (or Namba, 10 min walk).

## Tips

Go before the midday rush (11:00–12:30). Many stalls sell eat-on-the-spot portions. Next to Den Den Town (anime/electronics).
