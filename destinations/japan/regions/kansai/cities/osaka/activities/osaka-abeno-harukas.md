# Abeno Harukas 300 (observatory)

## Description

Observatory on the 58th–60th floors of Japan's tallest building (300 m), above Tennoji.

## How to get there

JR/Metro Tennoji Station.

## Tips

Cheaper alternative to Umeda Sky Building; combine with Shitennoji and Tennoji Park.
