# Den Den Town (Nipponbashi)

## Description

Osaka's Akihabara: electronics, anime, manga, games, figures along the arcade south of Nippombashi.

## How to get there

Metro Sakaisuji Line / Nankai to Nippombashi or Eneosaka.

## Tips

Next to Kuromon Market — chain them in one walk. Compare prices; tax-free over ¥5,000.
