# Namba Yasaka Shrine (lion head)

## Description

Small shrine famous for its giant lion-head stage (12 m) used in kabuki/performances; the head opens as a stage.

## How to get there

Metro Namba Station, 5 min walk.

## Tips

Quick photo stop between Dotonbori and Shinsaibashi.
