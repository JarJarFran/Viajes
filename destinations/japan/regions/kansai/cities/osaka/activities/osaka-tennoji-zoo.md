# Tennoji Park & Osaka City Zoo

## Description

Zoo next to Shitenno-ji in Tennoji Park; compact but pleasant, pandas historically.

## How to get there

JR/Metro Tennoji Station; zoo a 5 min walk.

## Tips

Combine with Shitenno-ji temple and Abeno Harukas in one Tennoji half-day.
