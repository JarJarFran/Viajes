# Osaka Castle

## Description

16th-century castle of Toyotomi Hideyoshi, rebuilt in concrete in 1931. Museum inside the tower; the surrounding park (moat, plum and cherry trees) is free and great for photos.

## How to get there

JR Osaka Loop Line to Morinomiya (10 min walk); opens 9:00. Combine with Osaka Castle Park early, before tour groups.

## Tips

Go at 9:00 opening to beat crowds. In our window the Nishinomaru Garden hosts the Illuminage light show (see osaka-castle-illuminage). Osaka Amazing Pass includes the tower.
