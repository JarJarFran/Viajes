# Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan

## Description

One of the world's best aquariums: 9 m-deep Pacific Ocean tank with a whale shark, manta rays and sunfish, then a spiral through 15 marine zones.

## How to get there

Metro Chuo Line to Osakako Station (10:00–20:00, last entry 19:00). From Namba: Yotsubashi to Hommachi, change to Chuo Line (~25 min).

## Tips

Book skip-the-line online. Combo tickets with Tempozan Ferris Wheel or Santa Maria cruise save 10–20%. NOT included in the Osaka Amazing Pass.
