# Osaka Castle Illuminage (winter light show)

## Description

Projection mapping and lights on the castle grounds, esp. the Nishinomaru Garden west of the tower. 2025 theme: "Toyotomi's Dream... Unification of the Nation". Runs through our whole Osaka stay and into January if we returned.

## How to get there

Same as osaka-castle: Morinomiya Station, 10 min walk.

## Tips

Evening entry to the Nishinomaru Garden is ticketed separately; check current hours before going.
