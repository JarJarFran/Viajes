# Osaka

Japan's third city and its food capital: loud, casual, neon-lit. Best base in Kansai for day trips (Kyoto 15–30 min, Nara 45 min, Kobe 30 min, Himeji 60 min).

## How to get there

- From Kansai Airport (KIX): Nankai or JR Rapid to Namba/Tennoji (~35–50 min); Haruka limited express to Umeda/Shin-Osaka.
- From Kyoto: JR Rapid Shin-Osaka → Kyoto ~30 min (¥580); Hankyu Umeda → Kyoto-Kawaramachi ~43 min (¥410); Shinkansen Nozomi ~13 min (¥1,450 non-reserved).
- Shinkansen stops at Shin-Osaka (not central Osaka); Metro Midosuji Line connects to Umeda/Namba.

## What to see and do

- [Osaka Castle](activities/osaka-castle.md) — tower ¥1,200, park free, opens 9:00
- [Dotonbori & Shinsaibashi at night](activities/osaka-dotonbori-night.md) — Glico sign, street food, river cruise
- [Kuromon Ichiba Market](activities/osaka-kuromon-market.md) — "Osaka's Kitchen", grazing lunch
- [Shinsekai & Tsutenkaku Tower](activities/osaka-shinsekai-tsutenkaku.md) — retro district, kushikatsu
- [Umeda Sky Building](activities/osaka-umeda-sky-building.md) — ¥2,000, best at sunset
- [Sumiyoshi Taisha](activities/osaka-sumiyoshi-taisha.md) — ancient shrine, free, arched bridge

## Events in our trip window (Dec 26 – Jan 2)

- [Midosuji Illumination](activities/osaka-midosuji-illumination.md) — 4 km of lights Umeda→Namba, ON until **Dec 31** (we are here Dec 26–31)
- [Osaka Castle Illuminage](activities/osaka-castle-illuminage.md) — castle grounds light show, Nov 20–Feb 14
- Nakanoshima EAST waterside lights and Namba Parks illuminations run into late January
- New Year: shrines open for hatsumode Jan 1–3; many shops close Jan 1–3

## Where to eat

- Dotonbori: takoyaki (Kukuru, Wanaka), okonomiyaki (Mizuno), kushikatsu (Daruma in Shinsekai)
- Kuromon Market: sashimi, wagyu skewers, sea urchin
- Depachika (department-store food floors) in Umeda: Takashimaya, Daimaru, Lucua

## Transport passes

- **Osaka Amazing Pass**: ¥2,800 (1 day) / ¥3,600 (2 days) — unlimited Osaka Metro + free entry to ~40 attractions (castle tower, Umeda Sky Building, Tsutenkaku). Not USJ/Kaiyukan.
- IC card (ICOCA) for everything else.
