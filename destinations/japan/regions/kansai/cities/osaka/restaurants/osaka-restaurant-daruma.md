# Daruma (Shinsekai)

## What to order

Deep-fried skewers of meat, seafood, vegetables. NEVER double-dip in the communal sauce.

## Hours & booking

11:00-22:30; closed some days - check.

## Tips

Order a beer and a mixed platter; stand at the counter.
