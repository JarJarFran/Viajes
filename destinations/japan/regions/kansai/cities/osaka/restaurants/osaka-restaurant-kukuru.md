# Kukuru

## What to order

Takoyaki (octopus balls) cooked to order at the stall; 6-8 pieces ~¥500-800.

## Hours & booking

~10:00-21:00.

## Tips

Eat walking but stand aside; very hot fresh. Try the sauce+mayo+bonito standard.
