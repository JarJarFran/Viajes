# Mizuno

## What to order

Mizuno-style okonomiyaki (mixed batter, Osaka style) with yam-i-mo; long-running shop since 1945.

## Hours & booking

Lunch/dinner 11:00-22:00; often a queue - arrive at opening.

## Tips

Sit at the counter to watch the teppan griddle. Cash/card both OK.
