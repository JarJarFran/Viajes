# 551 Horai

## What to order

Famous butaman (steamed pork buns) and shumai; also yakisoba.

## Hours & booking

Shops 10:00-20:00; the station branch is handy for departures.

## Tips

Buy a dozen butaman to go - classic Osaka souvenir snack.
