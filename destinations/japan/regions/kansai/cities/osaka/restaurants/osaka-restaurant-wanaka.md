# Wanaka

## What to order

Alternative takoyaki institution next to Kukuru; also sells akashiyaki (soupy takoyaki).

## Hours & booking

~10:00-21:00.

## Tips

Akashiyaki is the soupy Osaka variant - dip in dashi.
