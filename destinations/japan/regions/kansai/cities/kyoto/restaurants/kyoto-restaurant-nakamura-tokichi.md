# Nakamura Tokichi

## What to order

Matcha parfait, matcha soba, warabi mochi - the Uji matcha institution.

## Hours & booking

~10:00-18:00; expect a queue in Uji.

## Tips

Go to the Uji main shop (next to Byodo-in) or the Kawaramachi branch in Kyoto city.
