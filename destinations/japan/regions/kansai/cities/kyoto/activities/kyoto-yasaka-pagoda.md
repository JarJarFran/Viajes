# Yasaka Pagoda (Hokan-ji) & Ishibe-koji

## Description

Five-storey pagoda overlooking Higashiyama, the classic Kyoto postcard; Ishibe-koji stone lane below it.

## How to get there

Bus to Higashiyama-dori / Keihan Gion-Shijo, 10 min walk.

## Tips

Photogenic at dusk. Combine with Kiyomizu-dera walk and Gion dinner.
