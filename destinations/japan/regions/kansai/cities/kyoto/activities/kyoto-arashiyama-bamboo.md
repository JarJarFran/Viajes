# Arashiyama Bamboo Grove & Togetsukyo Bridge

## Description

500 m path of towering bamboo between Tenryu-ji and Okochi Sanso. Combine with Tenryu-ji's pond garden, the monkey park (open in winter, macaques fluffiest) and Togetsukyo Bridge over the Katsura river.

## How to get there

JR Sagano Line from Kyoto Station to Saga-Arashiyama (~20 min), or Hankyu to Arashiyama Station.

## Tips

Arrive before 7:00 — one of Kyoto's most crowded spots by 9:00. The old December Hanatoro lantern event was discontinued (2021); no winter light-up in the grove.
