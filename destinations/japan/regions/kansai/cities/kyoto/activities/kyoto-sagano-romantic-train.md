# Sagano Romantic Train (Torokko)

## Description

Scenic 25-min ride along the Hozu river gorge from Saga-Arashiyama to Kameoka, through tunnels and over bridges; in autumn the foliage is spectacular.

## How to get there

Torokko Saga-Arashiyama Station, next to JR Saga-Arashiyama.

## Tips

Book ahead on weekends/holidays. Combine with the Hozu river boat ride back down (seasonal). Check winter operation days.
