# Nanzen-ji (brick aqueduct)

## Description

Vast Zen complex with the iconic 1888 red-brick aqueduct, forest trails up the hillside.

## How to get there

Kyoto City Subway Tozai Line to Keage Station, then 10 min walk (end of Philosopher's Path).

## Tips

Walk on top of the aqueduct. Free to roam the main grounds.
