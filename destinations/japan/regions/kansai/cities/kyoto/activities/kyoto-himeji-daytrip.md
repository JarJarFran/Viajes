# Day trip to Himeji Castle

## Description

Japan's most spectacular original castle (White Heron), UNESCO, with Koko-en garden next door.

## How to get there

Hikari/Kodama shinkansen Kyoto Station → Himeji.

## Tips

Castle + garden ~3 h. Good rainy-day alternative is Osaka.
