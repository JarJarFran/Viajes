# Iwatayama Monkey Park

## Description

Hilltop park above Arashiyama with ~120 free-roaming Japanese macaques and a heated hut with panoramic Kyoto views.

## How to get there

20 min uphill walk from Hankyu Arashiyama Station (or Saga-Arashiyama).

## Tips

No food inside the park (they confiscate); feed from the hut. Great city view from the top; combine with bamboo grove.
