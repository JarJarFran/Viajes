# Kurama → Kibune mountain hike

## Description

2 h forested ridge walk from the mountain village of Kurama (Kurama-dera temple) down to riverside Kibune; Kurama has an outdoor onsen to soak tired legs.

## How to get there

Eizan Kurama Line from Demachiyanagi to Kurama (~30 min).

## Tips

Start at Kurama (higher) and descend to Kibune for lunch. Wear proper shoes — real forest trail. In January: cold, possibly snow.
