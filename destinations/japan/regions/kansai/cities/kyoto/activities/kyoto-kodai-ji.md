# Kodai-ji (NYE bell ringing)

## Description

Higashiyama temple with illuminated gardens; on Dec 31 it rings its bell starting 23:45 and distributes numbered tickets from 22:00 to the first 108 groups to ring it themselves.

## How to get there

Keihan to Gion-Shijo or Kiyomizu-Gojo, 10 min walk.

## Tips

We're in Osaka on Dec 31, but Kodai-ji also has winter evening illuminations in January — check dates.
