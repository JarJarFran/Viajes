# Toka Ebisu festival at Ebisu-jinja (Gion)

## Description

Festival for Ebisu, god of merchants: stalls in the lane, lucky bamboo branches (fukuzasa) with charms that shopkeepers queue for. Lively in the evenings.

## How to get there

10 min walk from Gion-Shijo (Keihan) or Kiyomizu-Gojo stations, in Gion.

## Tips

WE ARE IN KYOTO ON JAN 8 — the festival's first day falls exactly in our stay. Main day is Jan 10.
