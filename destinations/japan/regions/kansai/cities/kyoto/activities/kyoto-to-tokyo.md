# Kyoto → Tokyo (Jan 8/9 move)

## Description

Our main long leg on Jan 8 or 9. Hikari is the sensible train (Nozomi is faster but needs a ¥4,960 supplement even with a JR Pass, and we're not buying one).

## How to get there

Kyoto Station, Tokaido Shinkansen platforms. Buy tickets at JR ticket office or SmartEX.

## Tips

Reserve seats at New Year period — trains are full. Green car +¥4,870 each way if we want comfort. Forward luggage to Tokyo hotel with takuhaibin.
