# Ryoan-ji (rock garden)

## Description

UNESCO Zen temple famous for its 15-rock dry landscape garden — sit and contemplate 15–20 min.

## How to get there

Kyoto City Bus 59 from Kyoto Station or Ryoanji-michi stop.

## Tips

First hour after opening is quietest. Combine with Kinkaku-ji and Ninna-ji (northwest loop).
