# Eikan-do (Zenrin-ji)

## Description

Temple of the Jodo sect with a pond garden reflecting the pagoda; famous autumn maples, calm in winter.

## How to get there

Kyoto City Bus 5 from Kyoto Station to Eikando-michi.

## Tips

Near Philosopher's Path south end.
