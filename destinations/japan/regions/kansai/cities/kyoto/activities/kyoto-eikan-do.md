# Eikan-do (Zenrin-ji)

## Description

Temple of the Jodo sect with a pond garden reflecting the pagoda; famous autumn maples, calm in winter.

## How to get there

Bus 5/17/32/100 from Kyoto Station to Ginkakuji-michi? No: to Eikando-mae.
