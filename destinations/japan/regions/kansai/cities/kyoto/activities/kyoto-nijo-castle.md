# Nijo Castle

## Description

UNESCO castle of the Tokugawa shoguns, famous for the "nightingale floors" and Ninomaru Palace paintings.

## How to get there

Kyoto City Subway Tozai Line to Nijo-jo-mae Station.

## Tips

In our window: castle closed Dec 29–31; Ninomaru Palace closed Jan 1–3 (grounds open). Best visit Jan 4+.
