# Katsura Imperial Villa

## Description

One of Japan's finest aristocratic villas with a strolling garden; appears in most Kyoto top-30 lists.

## How to get there

Hankyu Kyoto Line to Katsura Station, then bus/walk.

## Tips

Advance application via Imperial Household Agency (weeks ahead); tours in Japanese mainly.
