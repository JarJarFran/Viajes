# Ninna-ji

## Description

UNESCO temple with a five-storey pagoda, the Omuro Imperial Palace quarters and a late-blooming cherry grove.

## How to get there

JR Saga-Arashiyama? No: bus 10/26/59 from Kyoto Station to Ninnaji-mae, or Eizan? Use bus to Omuro/Ninnaji.

## Tips

Pair with Ryoan-ji on the same northwest day.
