# Day trip to Nara (Todai-ji, deer park)

## Description

Old capital 45 min away: Todai-ji's Great Buddha in the world's largest wooden building, Kofuku-ji, Kasuga Taisha, 1,200 bowing deer in Nara Park.

## How to get there

JR Nara Line Miyakoji Rapid from Kyoto Station to Nara (45–50 min, ¥720); Kintetsu to Kintetsu-Nara (~35 min, ¥760).

## Tips

Start at 8:00 in Nara. All sights within one walkable park (~4 h). Back in Kyoto by afternoon.
