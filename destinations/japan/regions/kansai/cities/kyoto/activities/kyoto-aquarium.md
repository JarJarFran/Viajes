# Kyoto Aquarium

## Description

Modern aquarium next to the Railway Museum at Umekoji: dolphin shows, jellyfish, local Kamo river species.

## How to get there

Same as railway museum.

## Tips

Open during New Year (unlike many museums) — good Jan 2–3 option.
