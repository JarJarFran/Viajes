# Tofuku-ji

## Description

Huge Zen temple famous for the Tsutenkyo bridge over a maple valley; rock gardens.

## How to get there

JR Nara Line or Keihan to Tofukuji Station.

## Tips

Best koyo late Nov — in January it's peaceful and nearly empty.
