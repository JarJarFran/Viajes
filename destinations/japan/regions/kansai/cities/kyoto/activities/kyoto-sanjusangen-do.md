# Sanjusangen-do (1,001 Kannon statues)

## Description

Long hall housing 1,001 statues of Kannon, plus a seated Kannon flanked by 28 attendant deities.

## How to get there

Kyoto City Bus 206 from Kyoto Station to Hakubutsukan-mae (~10 min).

## Tips

Open on Jan 1–3 (9:00–16:00 in winter). Photography inside is not allowed.
