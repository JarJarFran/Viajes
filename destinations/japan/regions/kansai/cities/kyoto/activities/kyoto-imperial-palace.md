# Kyoto Imperial Palace (Gosho)

## Description

Former imperial residence: Shishinden hall, gardens, gates. Free entry.

## How to get there

Subway Karasuma Line to Imadegawa Station.

## Tips

In our window (Jan 2–8) it reopens ~Jan 5 — check the Imperial Household Agency calendar.
