# Fushimi Inari Taisha (Senbon Torii)

## Description

Famous shrine with ~10,000 vermilion torii gates forming tunnels up Mount Inari. Full loop to the summit is a real hike (2–3 h); the lower sections are the iconic photo spots.

## How to get there

JR Nara Line to Inari Station or Keihan Main Line to Fushimi-Inari Station (both next to the entrance).

## Tips

Go before 8:00 (ideally 7:00) — crowds build fast. Bring water; few shops open early. Hatsumode (Jan 1–3) makes it extremely busy — we arrive in Kyoto Jan 2, so consider going Jan 4+ or very early.
