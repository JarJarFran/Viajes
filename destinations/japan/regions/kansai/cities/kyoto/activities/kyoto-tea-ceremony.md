# Tea ceremony experience

## Description

Guided matcha preparation and wagashi in a tea house or temple (many offer English-friendly sessions).

## How to get there

Various venues in Higashiyama / near Kyoto Station — book via Voyagin/GetYourGuide or temple sites.

## Tips

Book ahead. Good rainy-day activity in January.
