# Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)

## Description

Zen Buddhist temple covered in gold leaf, reflected in Kyoko-chi pond. UNESCO site.

## How to get there

Kyoto City Bus 205 from Kyoto Station (~40 min) to Kinkakuji-michi stop.

## Tips

Arrive at 9:00 opening. Combine with Ryoan-ji (rock garden, ¥600) and Ninna-ji on the same northwest line.
