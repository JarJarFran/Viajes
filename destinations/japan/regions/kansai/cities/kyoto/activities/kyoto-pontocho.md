# Pontocho alley

## Description

Narrow lantern-lit alley along the Kamo river between Shijo and Sanjo: kaiseki, yakitori, bars. In summer there are kawadoko riverside platforms.

## How to get there

Keihan Sanjo or Shijo, or Hankyu Kawaramachi.

## Tips

Reserve for kaiseki. In winter it's cozy indoor dining.
