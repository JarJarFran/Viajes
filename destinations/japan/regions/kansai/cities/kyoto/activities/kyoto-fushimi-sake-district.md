# Fushimi sake district & Gekkeikan Okura Sake Museum

## Description

Historic sake-brewing district along a willow-lined canal near Fushimi Inari; brewery museums and tastings (pure local groundwater).

## How to get there

Keihan Fushimi-Inari or JR Inari Station.

## Tips

Pair with a morning Fushimi Inari visit. Buy a bottle to take home.
