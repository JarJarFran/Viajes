# Nishiki Market ("Kyoto's Kitchen")

## Description

400 m covered arcade with ~130 stalls: pickles (tsukemono), tofu, yuba, knives, tea, street snacks (tako-tamago, yuba sushi).

## How to get there

Karasuma Line to Shijo Station, or Hankyu to Karasuma/Kawaramachi.

## Tips

In our window (Jan 2–8) expect partial opening until ~Jan 4–5; go Jan 5+. Winter specialties: nishin soba (herring soba), yudofu.
