# Toji Temple (market day)

## Description

Kyoto's tallest pagoda (UNESCO); five-storey structure visible from the shinkansen. Hosts the Kobo-san flea market on the 21st of each month (antiques, ceramics, New Year goods).

## How to get there

10 min walk from Kyoto Station (or Tozai Line to Toji).

## Tips

In our window: Dec 21 market is just before we arrive; Jan 21 falls while we're in Tokyo. Pagoda interior opens only in special windows.
