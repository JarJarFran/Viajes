# Day trip to Uji (Byodo-in, matcha)

## Description

Tea capital: Byodo-in's Phoenix Hall on the 10-yen coin, tea plantations, matcha everything (ice cream, soba, parfaits).

## How to get there

JR Nara Line Kyoto→Uji (~25 min) or Keihan Main Line (~25 min).

## Tips

Half day is enough. Try matcha soba and a bowl at Nakamura Tokichi.
