# Kyoto Railway Museum

## Description

Huge museum at Umekoji Park: real trains incl. shinkansen, simulators, roundhouse.

## How to get there

Bus 6/8/88 from Kyoto Station to Umekoji-koen-mae, or JR to Umekoji-Kyoto-nishi.

## Tips

Next door to Kyoto Aquarium — good wet/rainy January day. Open on Jan 2–3.
