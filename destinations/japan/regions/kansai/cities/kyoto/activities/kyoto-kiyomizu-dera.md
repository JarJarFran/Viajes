# Kiyomizu-dera & Higashiyama slopes

## Description

UNESCO wooden-stage temple on a hillside with panoramic views over Kyoto. Walk down through Sannenzaka/Ninenzaka preserved lanes into Gion.

## How to get there

Kyoto City Bus 206 from Kyoto Station (~15 min) to Gojozaka or Kiyomizu-michi, then 10 min uphill.

## Tips

Start high at Kiyomizu-dera and walk downhill to Gion at dusk. Matcha soft serve on the slope (~¥400–500). New Year's Eve bell ringing here is reservation-only.
