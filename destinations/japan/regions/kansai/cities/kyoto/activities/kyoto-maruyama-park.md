# Maruyama Park

## Description

Kyoto's most famous park, next to Yasaka Shrine and Gion; huge weeping cherry tree; lantern-lit at night.

## How to get there

Keihan Gion-Shijo / bus Yasaka-jinja.

## Tips

Evening atmosphere in January is calm; good picnic/hanami spot in spring.
