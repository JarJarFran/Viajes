# Hatsumode (first shrine visit of the year) at Yasaka Shrine

## Description

First prayer of the year for health and luck: coin offering, bell, omikuji, amulets. Yasaka Shrine also keeps the Okera sacred fire burning from Dec 31 19:30 (fire rope ¥1,000).

## How to get there

Gion-Shijo (Keihan) or Kawaramachi (Hankyu) stations.

## Tips

We are in Kyoto Jan 2–3: go early morning Jan 2 or 3 to avoid the biggest queues. Fushimi Inari is the other big option but much more crowded.
