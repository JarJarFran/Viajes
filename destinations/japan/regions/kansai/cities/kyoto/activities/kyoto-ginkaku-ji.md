# Ginkaku-ji (Silver Pavilion)

## Description

15th-century Zen pavilion (never actually silver) with the Sea of Silver Sand dry garden and moss garden. Pairs with the Philosopher's Path.

## How to get there

Kyoto City Bus 5/17/32 from Kyoto Station or Kawaramachi to Ginkakuji-michi, or Eiden to Ginkakuji-michi Station.

## Tips

Arrive at opening. Walk the Philosopher's Path south to Nanzen-ji afterwards.
