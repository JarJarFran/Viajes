# Gion & Higashiyama at dusk

## Description

Kyoto's geisha district: machiya lanes, paper lanterns, Hanamikoji street, Yasaka Shrine. Pontocho alley along the Kamo river is the classic dinner spot.

## How to get there

Keihan Line to Gion-Shijo Station, or Kyoto City Bus to Gion stop.

## Tips

Best at dusk when lanterns light up. Maiko/geiko sightings most likely on Hanamikoji in the early evening.
