# Sannenzaka & Ninenzaka lanes

## Description

Preserved stone lanes with machiya townhouses, teahouses and souvenir shops on the walk between Kiyomizu-dera and Gion — the classic Kyoto postcard street.

## How to get there

Same as kyoto-kiyomizu-dera: bus 206 to Gojozaka/Kiyomizu-michi, then walk downhill.

## Tips

Shops open ~9:00–10:00; go early (before 9:00) or late afternoon to avoid tour groups. Starbucks and Hard Rock Cafe in traditional buildings nearby.
