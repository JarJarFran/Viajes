# Kennin-ji

## Description

Kyoto's oldest Zen temple, in the heart of Gion, with a famous dragon ceiling painting and dry gardens.

## How to get there

Keihan Gion-Shijo or bus to Gion.

## Tips

Right next to Ebisu-jinja (Toka Ebisu festival Jan 8–12) and Hanamikoji.
