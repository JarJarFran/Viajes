# Karuta Hajime at Yasaka Shrine

## Description

The year's first game of uta-garuta (Hyakunin Isshu poem cards), played in the shrine's Noh stage by women in Heian-period twelve-layer kimono (junihitoe).

## Dates & hours

Every January 3 from 13:00 (about an hour). Free to watch; arrive early for a spot in front of the stage.

## Tips

Combine with the hatsumode crowd at Yasaka in the morning and Maruyama Park next door. The shrine may change or cancel the ceremony — check yasaka-jinja.or.jp.
