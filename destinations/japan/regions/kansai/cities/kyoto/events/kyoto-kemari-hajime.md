# Kemari Hajime at Shimogamo Shrine

## Description

New Year demonstration of kemari, the Heian court ball game: players in court costume keep a deerskin ball in the air inside a roped ground.

## Dates & hours

Every January 4, around 13:30 (some listings say 14:00). Free standing area; paid seats close to the ground.

## Tips

Shimogamo Shrine (UNESCO) and the Tadasu-no-mori forest are 2 km west of Ginkaku-ji — bus or 25 min walk.
