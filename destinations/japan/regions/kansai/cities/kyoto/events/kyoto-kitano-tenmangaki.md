# Fude-hajime-sai and Tenmangaki at Kitano Tenmangu

## Description

First calligraphy of the year at the shrine of the god of learning: visitors write their own kakizome in front of the main hall; on January 3 there is an offertory kyogen performance.

## Dates & hours

January 2–4, calligraphy 10:00–16:00 (paper ¥50, entry ¥200). Kyogen on January 3, 13:00–14:30 (2026 programme).

## Tips

Near Kinkaku-ji (bus 10 min). The shrine is also famous for its monthly Tenjin-san market on the 25th.
