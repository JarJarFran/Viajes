# Joya no Kane (New Year's Eve temple bells)

## Description

At midnight Dec 31, 2026 temples ring their bells 108 times. Main Kyoto venues: Chion-in (bell from ~22:40, 70-ton bell), Kodai-ji (numbered tickets from 22:00, first 108 groups ring it themselves), Chishaku-in (23:40), Eikando (queue 23:00-00:30), Tenryu-ji (Arashiyama), Kiyomizu-dera (reservation-only). Keihan trains run all night Dec 31 18:00 -> Jan 1 07:00.

## Tips

We are in OSAKA on Dec 31, 2026 — Kyoto is 24-28 min away by JR Special Rapid if we want to attend; otherwise Osaka shrines (Shitenno-ji area, Sumiyoshi) also ring, and in Tokyo (from Jan 9) Zojo-ji is calm by then. Decide before booking NYE dinner.
