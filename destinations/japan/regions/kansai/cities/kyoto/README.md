# Kyoto

Former imperial capital (794–1868): 2,000+ temples and shrines, geisha districts, preserved lanes. Sights are spread out — group by area: Higashiyama (east), Arashiyama (west), Rakuhoku (northwest), Fushimi (south).

## How to get there

- From Osaka: JR Rapid ~30 min (¥580) or Hankyu ~43 min (¥410) to Kyoto-Kawaramachi; Shinkansen to Kyoto Station ~15 min from Shin-Osaka.
- From Kansai Airport: Haruka limited express direct to Kyoto Station (~75 min).
- In the city: Kyoto City Bus (flat ¥230) + subway; **Subway & Bus 1-Day Pass ¥1,100** (pays off at 5 rides); weekend/holiday sightseeing buses EX100/EX101 ¥500 covered by the pass.

- [Gion & Higashiyama at dusk](activities/kyoto-gion-higashiyama.md) — free
- [Nishiki Market](activities/kyoto-nishiki-market.md) — free; partially closed first days of January

- [Sanjusangen-do (1,001 Kannons)](activities/kyoto-sanjusangen-do.md) — ¥600, winter 9:00–16:00

## Events in our trip window (Jan 2 – Jan 8)

- [Hatsumode at Yasaka Shrine](activities/kyoto-hatsumode.md) — first shrine visit of the year, **Jan 2–3 we are here**; go early morning
- [Toka Ebisu festival, Ebisu-jinja (Gion)](activities/kyoto-toka-ebisu.md) — **Jan 8–12; Jan 8 is our last day in Kyoto**
- Closures to plan around: Nijo Castle Dec 29–31, Ninomaru Palace Jan 1–3, Imperial Palace Dec 28–Jan 4, National Museum Dec 28–Jan 1, Nishiki Market mostly closed until ~Jan 4–5
- (Near-miss: Shimai-Kobo flea market at Toji is on the 21st of each month — Dec 21 just before we arrive, Jan 21 already in Tokyo)

## Where to eat

- Nishiki Market (from ~Jan 5), Pontocho alley, Kyoto Station building (Porta underground + 10F ramen street)
- Winter specialties: yudofu (Nanzen-ji / Arashiyama Sagano), nishin soba, winter kaiseki, atsu-kan (hot sake)
