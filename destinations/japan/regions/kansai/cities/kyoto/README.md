# Kyoto

Former imperial capital (794–1868): 2,000+ temples and shrines, geisha districts, preserved lanes. Sights are spread out — group by area: Higashiyama (east), Arashiyama (west), Rakuhoku (northwest), Fushimi (south).

## How to get there

- From Osaka: JR Rapid ~30 min (¥580) or Hankyu ~43 min (¥410) to Kyoto-Kawaramachi; Shinkansen to Kyoto Station ~15 min from Shin-Osaka.
- From Kansai Airport: Haruka limited express direct to Kyoto Station (~75 min).
- In the city: Kyoto City Bus (flat ¥230) + subway; **Subway & Bus 1-Day Pass ¥1,100** (pays off at 5 rides); weekend/holiday sightseeing buses EX100/EX101 ¥500 covered by the pass.

## What to see and do

- [Fushimi Inari (Senbon Torii)](activities/kyoto-fushimi-inari.md) — free, 24 h, go before 8:00
- [Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)](activities/kyoto-kinkaku-ji.md) — ¥500, bus 205
- [Kiyomizu-dera & Higashiyama slopes](activities/kyoto-kiyomizu-dera.md) — ¥500, bus 206
- [Arashiyama Bamboo Grove](activities/kyoto-arashiyama-bamboo.md) — free, before 7:00
- [Gion & Higashiyama at dusk](activities/kyoto-gion-higashiyama.md) — free
- [Nishiki Market](activities/kyoto-nishiki-market.md) — free; partially closed first days of January
- [Nijo Castle](activities/kyoto-nijo-castle.md) — ¥800/¥1,300; palace closed Jan 1–3
- [Sanjusangen-do (1,001 Kannons)](activities/kyoto-sanjusangen-do.md) — ¥600, winter 9:00–16:00

## Seasonal events and New Year closures

- [Hatsumode at Yasaka Shrine](events/kyoto-hatsumode.md) — first shrine visit of the year, Dec 31–Jan 3; go early morning
- [Joya no Kane](events/kyoto-joya-no-kane.md) — temple bells on New Year's Eve
- [Toka Ebisu festival, Ebisu-jinja (Gion)](events/kyoto-toka-ebisu.md) — Jan 8–12, main day Jan 10
- [First calligraphy at Kitano Tenmangu](events/kyoto-kitano-tenmangaki.md) — Jan 2–4
- [Karuta Hajime at Yasaka Shrine](events/kyoto-karuta-hajime.md) — Jan 3, 13:00, poem-card game in Heian costume
- [Kemari Hajime at Shimogamo Shrine](events/kyoto-kemari-hajime.md) — Jan 4, ~13:30, Heian court football
- New Year closures: Nijo Castle Dec 29–31 (Ninomaru Palace Jan 1–3), Imperial Palace Dec 28–Jan 4, National Museum Dec 28–Jan 1, Nanzen-ji Dec 28–31, Nishiki Market mostly closed until ~Jan 4–5
- Monthly markets: Kobo-ichi flea market at Toji on the 21st; Tenjin-ichi at Kitano Tenmangu on the 25th

## Where to eat

- Nishiki Market (after the New Year closure), Pontocho alley, Kyoto Station building (Porta underground + 10F ramen street)
- Winter specialties: yudofu (Nanzen-ji / Arashiyama Sagano), nishin soba, winter kaiseki, atsu-kan (hot sake)
