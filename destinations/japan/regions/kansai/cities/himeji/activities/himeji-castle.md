# Himeji Castle

## Description

The "White Heron" castle: the largest and best-preserved original castle keep in Japan (1609), UNESCO World Heritage. Six floors inside the main keep with steep wooden stairs; shoes off.

## How to get there

15–20 min walk from JR Himeji Station along Otemae-dori, or the loop bus.

## Tips

Go at opening: queues for the keep build up by late morning. Since March 2026 adults who do not live in Himeji pay ¥2,500 (residents ¥1,000); under-18s free. The castle + Koko-en combo ticket is ¥2,600.
