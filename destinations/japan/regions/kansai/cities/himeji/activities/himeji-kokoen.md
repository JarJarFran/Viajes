# Koko-en Garden

## Description

Nine walled Edo-style gardens on the site of the samurai residences west of the castle: pond garden, tea garden, pine garden, bamboo garden.

## How to get there

Next to the castle's west side (5 min walk from the castle exit).

## Tips

Buy the castle + Koko-en combo ticket (¥2,600) at the castle. The garden-only fee was revised in March 2026 (sources give ¥310–400) — check the official page.
