# Himeji

Castle town in Hyogo, 1 h west of Osaka by JR: home of Himeji Castle (the best-preserved original castle in Japan, UNESCO) and the Koko-en gardens next to it. Usually visited as a day trip from Osaka or Kyoto.

## How to get there

- From Osaka / Shin-Osaka: JR Special Rapid (~1 h) or Sanyo Shinkansen (~30 min).
- From Kyoto: Shinkansen (~45–55 min) or JR Special Rapid (~1 h 30 min).
- The castle is a 15–20 min walk straight up Otemae-dori from JR Himeji Station.

## What to see and do

- [Himeji Castle](activities/himeji-castle.md) — ¥2,500 for non-residents of Himeji (since Mar 2026); closed Dec 29–30
- [Koko-en Garden](activities/himeji-kokoen.md) — nine walled gardens; combo ticket with the castle ¥2,600
