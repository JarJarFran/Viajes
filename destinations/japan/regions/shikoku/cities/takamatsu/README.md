# Takamatsu

Capital of Kagawa Prefecture and the main ferry gateway to the Seto Inland Sea art islands such as Naoshima. Known for Ritsurin Garden (a Special Place of Scenic Beauty with the borrowed scenery of Mt. Shiun), the ruins of Takamatsu Castle in Tamamo Park, and the Yashima plateau with Yashima-ji, temple 84 of the Shikoku pilgrimage.

## How to get there

- From Okayama: JR Rapid Marine Liner, about 52-55 min, roughly 2 trains per hour; fare ¥1,660 (rapid service, no limited-express charge); a reserved seat costs extra (¥840 per the JR timetable page; one guide says ¥420).

## Getting around

- Kotoden (Takamatsu-Kotohira Electric Railroad): Takamatsu-Chikko station is about 5 min on foot from JR Takamatsu; the Kotohira Line runs to Kotoden-Kotohira (for Kotohira-gu) in about 60 min without changing (fare not confirmed; one older article says ¥700). New rolling stock entered service on this line on 2026-10-01.
- Yashima summit shuttle bus: from the Takamatsu Station bus terminal and from Kotoden Yashima (about 10 min ride) and JR Yashima (about 18 min ride) stations; check yashima-navi.jp for the current timetable.
- Ferries from Takamatsu Port to Naoshima (Miyanoura Port), Shikoku Kisen: car ferry about 50 min, ¥680 one way; passenger-only high-speed boat about 25-30 min, ¥1,590 one way.

## What to see and do

- [Ritsurin Garden](activities/takamatsu-ritsurin-garden.md) — ¥500 adults, ¥170 junior-high age and under, preschoolers free; free on Jan 1 and Mar 16 (opening anniversary)
- [Takamatsu Castle Ruins (Tamamo Park)](activities/takamatsu-castle-tamamo-park.md) — ¥300 adults (18+) since April 2026; under 18 free; older listings show ¥200
- [Naoshima art island (Chichu Art Museum)](activities/takamatsu-naoshima.md) — Chichu Art Museum ¥2,500 online / ¥2,800 on site on weekdays, ¥2,700 / ¥3,000 on weekends and holidays (15 and under free but must reserve); ticket with Night Program ¥3,700 (Fri and Sat); Benesse House Museum ¥1,200 (guide figure); ferry from Takamatsu ¥680 (car ferry) or ¥1,590 (high-speed boat) each way
- [Yashima plateau and Yashima-ji](activities/takamatsu-yashima.md) — Shuttle bus fare not confirmed
- [Shikoku Mura Museum](activities/takamatsu-shikoku-mura.md) — ¥1,600 adults, ¥1,000 university students, ¥600 junior/senior high, elementary and younger free
- [Kotohira-gu (Konpira-san)](activities/takamatsu-kotohira-gu.md) — Grounds free; Treasure House, Shoin and Takahashi Yuichi Museum ¥800 each

## Seasons

- Ritsurin Garden opening hours follow the sun (earliest 5:30 in summer, 7:00 in Dec-Jan); water lilies roughly mid-May to late October; autumn night light-up 2026: Nov 20-29, 17:00-21:00 (last entry 20:30), ¥500.
- Tamamo Park (Takamatsu Castle ruins): free night opening for the cherry blossoms (2026: Mar 27-Apr 5, 17:30-20:00); closed Dec 29-31.
- Naoshima: Chichu Art Museum closed for long-term maintenance 2027-01-12 to 2027-03-19; Naoshima New Museum of Art, Benesse House Museum, Lee Ufan Museum and most Art House Project houses closed 2027-01-12 to 01-18 (outdoor works such as the Pumpkin also not viewable then).
