# Ritsurin Garden

## Description

Former daimyo stroll garden in central Takamatsu with ponds and the borrowed scenery of Mt. Shiun, designated a Special Place of Scenic Beauty. Open every day of the year.

## Tips

Older sources still list ¥410 for adults; 2026 guides give ¥500. Autumn light-up 2026: Nov 20-29, 17:00-21:00 (last entry 20:30), same ¥500 fee.
