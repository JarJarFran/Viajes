# Kotohira-gu (Konpira-san)

## Description

Major shrine in Kotohira whose main hall is reached by a stone stairway of 785 steps. Usually visited as a day trip from Takamatsu.

## How to get there

Kotoden Kotohira Line from Takamatsu-Chikko to Kotoden-Kotohira (terminus, about 60 min, no change); the stone steps start about 10 min walk from JR Kotohira Station.

## Tips

Sources give different walking times to the main hall (about 50 min from Kotoden-Kotohira including the 785 steps).
