# Takamatsu Castle Ruins (Tamamo Park)

## Description

Historic site of Takamatsu Castle, now the municipal Tamamo Park, which also contains the Hiunkaku residence and its garden.

## Tips

Sources disagree on opening hours, which change by month and gate; phone 087-851-1521 to confirm. Free evening opening during cherry-blossom season (2026: Mar 27-Apr 5, 17:30-20:00; normal fee before 17:30).
