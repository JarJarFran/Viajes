# Yashima plateau and Yashima-ji

## Description

Plateau in Takamatsu topped by Yashima-ji, temple 84 of the Shikoku pilgrimage, and the Yashimaru summit visitor facility; popular for sunset views.

## How to get there

Yashima summit shuttle bus from the Takamatsu Station bus terminal, Kotoden Yashima Station (about 10 min) or JR Yashima Station (about 18 min); a marked hiking trail climbs from Yashima Station in about 1 hour.

## Tips

Summit tourist parking is open 6:30-22:00. Check the shuttle timetable on yashima-navi.jp before going.
