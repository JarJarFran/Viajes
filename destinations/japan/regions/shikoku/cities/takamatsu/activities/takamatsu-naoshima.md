# Naoshima art island (Chichu Art Museum)

## Description

Island in the Seto Inland Sea at the heart of Benesse Art Site Naoshima: Tadao Ando's largely underground Chichu Art Museum, Benesse House Museum with Yayoi Kusama's Pumpkin, the Naoshima New Museum of Art (opened spring 2025 near Honmura), the Lee Ufan Museum and the Art House Project in Honmura.

## How to get there

Ferry from Takamatsu Port to Miyanoura Port: car ferry about 50 min (¥680) or passenger high-speed boat about 25-30 min (¥1,590).

## Tips

Chichu tickets are for a set date and time and sold online; if online tickets sell out there are no same-day on-site sales. Museums close Mondays (Tuesday instead when Monday is a holiday); published Chichu hours differ between pages, so check the Benesse calendar.
