# Bansuiso

## Description

French-style mansion built in 1922 by a descendant of the Matsuyama feudal lords after his military studies in France, with a well-preserved Western interior.

## How to get there

5 min walk uphill northwest of the Saka no Ue no Kumo Museum (Okaido tram stop).

## Tips

Open on Mondays that are national holidays (closed other Mondays).
