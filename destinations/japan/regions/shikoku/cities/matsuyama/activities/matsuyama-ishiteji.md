# Ishiteji Temple

## Description

Matsuyama's most famous temple and number 51 of the 88-temple Shikoku Pilgrimage. Its Niomon gate is a National Treasure and the main hall and three-storey pagoda are Important Cultural Properties in Kamakura-period style; a 200 m cave behind the main hall leads to an unusual inner temple.

## How to get there

From Dogo Onsen tram stop, bus 5 min (¥230 IC / ¥250 cash) or a 15-20 min walk.

## Tips

Combine with Dogo Onsen; the dim cave passage with Buddhist statues is worth walking through to the inner temple and its golden dome hall.
