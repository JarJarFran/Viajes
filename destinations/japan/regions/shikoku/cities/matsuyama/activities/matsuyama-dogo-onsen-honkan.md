# Dogo Onsen Honkan

## Description

Wooden public bathhouse built in 1894, the symbol of Dogo Onsen and said to have inspired Studio Ghibli's 'Spirited Away'. It has two baths (the large Kami no Yu and the smaller Tama no Yu), tatami rest rooms, a small exhibition and the Yushinden wing built in 1899 for the Imperial Family.

## How to get there

4 min walk from Dogo Onsen tram terminal (tram line 5 from JR Matsuyama Station, 20 min; line 3 from Matsuyama-shi Station, 15 min).

## Tips

The building reopened fully in July 2024 after long preservation works (one guide); the fee levels above are from japan-guide, updated July 2025. The two large private tatami rooms require advance reservation.
