# Dogo Onsen Asuka no Yu

## Description

Public bathhouse opened in 2017, about 150 m west of the Honkan, designed after the Asuka period with stone baths and hinoki-panelled outdoor baths. The second floor has a large tatami lounge, private rooms and a private bath.

## How to get there

3 min walk from Dogo Onsen tram terminal.

## Tips

A quieter alternative to the Honkan using the same spring water; Tsubaki no Yu next door (opened 1953) is the cheapest bath in Dogo and popular with locals.
