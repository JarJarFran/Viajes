# Shiki Memorial Museum

## Description

Museum on haiku poet Masaoka Shiki (1867-1902), born in Matsuyama, with letters, manuscripts and belongings, plus displays on local history and on Natsume Soseki's novel 'Botchan'. Fair amount of English signage.

## How to get there

A few steps from Dogo Onsen tram terminal.

## Tips

japan-guide's Dogo page says 'most Tuesdays', its museum page says Tuesdays: check before going on a Tuesday.
