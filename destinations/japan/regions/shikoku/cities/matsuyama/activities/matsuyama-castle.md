# Matsuyama Castle

## Description

One of Japan's twelve original castles, built 1602-1628 on Mount Katsuyama; the current three-storey keep dates from 1820 and has multiple wings, turrets and gates. The hilltop gives a view over the city to the Seto Inland Sea, and the keep has exhibits on the feudal period.

## How to get there

Okaido tram stop (line 5 from JR Matsuyama Station, about 10 min), then 5 min on foot to the lower ropeway/chairlift station; or walk up in about 15 min.

## Tips

The single-seat chairlift is the popular way up; the keep and Ninomaru garden close on the 3rd Wednesday of December (dates listed are that rule). The Ninomaru Historic Garden at the southern foot preserves the floor plan of the former palace.
