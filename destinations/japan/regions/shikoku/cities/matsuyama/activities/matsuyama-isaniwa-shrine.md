# Isaniwa Shrine

## Description

Shrine built in 1667 in the Hachiman style, with two connected halls, sheltered corridors and vermilion pillars, reached by a tall flight of stairs above Dogo Onsen.

## How to get there

About 200 m east of Dogo Onsen tram station.

## Tips

Climb the stairs early before visiting the Honkan; Dogo Park with the remains of a castle site is nearby.
