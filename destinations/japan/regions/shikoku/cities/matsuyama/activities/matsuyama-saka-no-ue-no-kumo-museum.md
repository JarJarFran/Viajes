# Saka no Ue no Kumo Museum

## Description

Museum on Shiba Ryotaro's novel 'Saka no Ue no Kumo' (Clouds Above the Hill), set in Meiji-era Matsuyama, housed in a triangular building by Tadao Ando at the foot of the castle hill.

## How to get there

2 min walk from Okaido tram stop.

## Tips

The Ando architecture alone makes it worth a short stop on the way to the castle ropeway.
