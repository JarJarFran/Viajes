# Harimayabashi and downtown arcades

## Description

Small red bridge at the heart of downtown Kochi, famous from a folk love story about a Godaisan priest who bought a hairpin for a local girl. The nearby covered shopping arcades (such as Obiyamachi) sell combs and naruko clappers used in the Yosakoi dance.

## How to get there

10 min walk or 4 min tram ride south of JR Kochi Station; tram lines cross at Harimayabashi.

## Tips

Small and often called underwhelming; best as a stop between the station and the castle.
