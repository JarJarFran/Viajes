# Katsurahama Beach and Sakamoto Ryoma Memorial Museum

## Description

Scenic Pacific beach south of the city with the famous statue of Sakamoto Ryoma, a key figure of the Meiji Restoration, a small shrine on the point, a small aquarium and the Ryoma memorial museum on the hill above. Swimming is prohibited because of strong currents.

## How to get there

Kochiken Kotsu bus from JR Kochi Station, about 35 min, ¥800; or the My Yu Bus via Godaisan (1-day pass ¥1,300).

## Tips

Combine with Godaisan using the My Yu Bus day pass; restaurants at the beach serve katsuo no tataki.
