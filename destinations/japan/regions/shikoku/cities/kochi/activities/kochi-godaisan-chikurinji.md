# Mount Godaisan and Chikurinji Temple

## Description

Small mountain east of downtown with Chikurinji, temple 31 of the Shikoku Pilgrimage, known for its five-storey pagoda, a treasure hall of Buddhist statues and a well-kept garden behind the main hall.

## How to get there

My Yu Bus from JR Kochi Station, 25 min (1-day pass ¥900 Kochi Station-Godaisan); taxi about 20 min, around ¥2,300.

## Tips

The Makino Botanical Garden is next to the temple: plan half a day for both.
