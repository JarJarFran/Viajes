# Kochi Sunday Market

## Description

Open-air street market held since 1690, with about 300 stalls stretching some 1.3 km along Otesuji Street towards Kochi Castle, selling local fruit and vegetables, plants, Tosa kitchen knives, crafts and street food.

## How to get there

About 10 min walk from JR Kochi Station to the market entrance on Otesuji Street; Hasuikemachi-dori tram stop is nearby.

## Tips

Go in the morning for the best choice. Not held when Sunday falls on Jan 1-2 or during Yosakoi: the city lists Aug 10-12, JNTO says Aug 9-12.
