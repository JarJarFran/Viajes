# Hirome Market

## Description

Indoor market with dozens of food and drink stalls around shared tables, the most popular place to try katsuo no tataki and other Kochi dishes with local sake or beer in a lively atmosphere.

## How to get there

Downtown, between the Otesuji Sunday Market street and Kochi Castle, about 15 min on foot from JR Kochi Station.

## Tips

Very busy on weekend evenings: arrive before 18:00 to find a seat. One travel site gives 10:00-23:00 every day, so Sunday's earlier opening is per the listing site WalkerPlus.
