# Kochi Castle

## Description

One of twelve Japanese castles with an original keep, seat of the Yamauchi lords of Tosa; the main buildings date from the 1729-1753 reconstruction. It is the only castle that retains both its original keep and the lord's palace (Kaitokukan), and the keep was also used as a residence; the top floor overlooks downtown.

## How to get there

Short walk from Kochijo-mae tram stop (15 min from Kochi Station, change at Harimayabashi, ¥230), or 20 min on foot from JR Kochi Station / 10 min from Harimayabashi.

## Tips

Typical visit 60-90 minutes; on Sundays combine with the Sunday Market on Otesuji Street, which ends at the castle.
