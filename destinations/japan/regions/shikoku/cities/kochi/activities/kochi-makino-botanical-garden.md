# Kochi Prefectural Makino Botanical Garden

## Description

Large botanical garden on Godaisan named after Kochi-born botanist Makino Tomitaro (1862-1957), with walking paths, a greenhouse and museum halls about his life and research.

## How to get there

My Yu Bus from JR Kochi Station to Godaisan, about 25 min.

## Tips

Allow 1-2 hours; combine with Chikurinji next door.
