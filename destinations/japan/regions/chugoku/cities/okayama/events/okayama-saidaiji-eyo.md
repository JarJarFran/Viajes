# Saidaiji Eyo (Naked Festival)

## Description

Japan's best-known hadaka matsuri, held for some 500 years at Saidaiji temple, where over 9,000 loincloth-clad men are purified with water and compete for luck-bringing sacred sticks; women took part for the first time in 2024.
