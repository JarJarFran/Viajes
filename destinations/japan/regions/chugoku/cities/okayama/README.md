# Okayama

Capital of Okayama Prefecture and the second-largest city of the Chugoku Region, a transport hub where the Sanyo Shinkansen meets the only rail link to Shikoku. It is known for Korakuen, one of Japan's three great landscape gardens, the black 'crow castle' facing it, and as the home of the Momotaro (Peach Boy) folk tale. The preserved canal quarter of Kurashiki is 15–20 minutes away by train.

## How to get there

- From Shin-Osaka: Sanyo Shinkansen Nozomi/Mizuho ~45 min; non-reserved ¥5,610, reserved ¥6,140 (Hikari/Sakura/Kodama) or about ¥6,350–6,460 (Nozomi/Mizuho; sources differ).
- From Tokyo: Tokaido/Sanyo Shinkansen ~3.5 h by Nozomi (~4 h by Hikari), about ¥17,000 one way; JR Pass valid on Hikari (Nozomi needs a supplement). Flights Haneda–Okayama 75 min (discount ~¥13,000–18,000) + airport bus 30 min, ¥1,000; overnight buses ~10 h, ¥6,000–13,000.
- Okayama → Kurashiki: JR Sanyo Line local/rapid trains 15–20 min, ¥330 (JR Pass and Kansai Wide Pass valid); the canal area is a 10–15 min walk from Kurashiki Station's south exit.

## Getting around

- Okayama Electric Tramway (Higashiyama and Sekibashi lines) from in front of Okayama Station: flat ¥160 in the centre; 1-day pass ¥400; Hareka, Suica, Icoca and other IC cards accepted. Shiroshita stop (5 min) is 10–15 min on foot from Korakuen and the castle.
- Korakuen, Okayama Castle and the central museums lie within 15 minutes' walk of each other, about 1.5 km (25–30 min walk) east of Okayama Station.
- Kibi Plain: JR Kibi (Momotaro) Line to Bizen-Ichinomiya 10 min, ¥210 (every 30 min); bicycle rental from ¥1,500/day (+¥500 to drop off at another shop), 9:00–18:00 (Nov–Feb until 17:00, pick-up until 15:30).

## Local food

- Barazushi — Okayama-style scattered sushi rice topped with fresh fish from the Seto Inland Sea.
- Kibi dango — soft, gel-like sweet balls made from millet and rice flour, linked to the Momotaro legend; sold as souvenirs around Okayama Station.
- White peaches and grapes — grown in the northern, mountainous part of the city; sold fresh in season and in sweets.

## Seasons

- Korakuen is open every day: 7:30–18:00 from 20 Mar to Sep, 8:00–17:00 from Oct to 19 Mar.
- Saidaiji Eyo (naked festival) on the third Saturday of February; Momotaro Festival (with the Uraja dance) every August.
- Winter in Kurashiki: Ohara Museum closes at 15:00 Dec–Feb; Ohashi House closes Fridays Dec–Feb. The Kurashiki Archaeological Museum is scheduled to close from April 2027 to March 2030.
- Year end: Okayama Castle closed 29–31 Dec; Yumeji Art Museum 28 Dec–1 Jan.
