# Ohashi House (Kurashiki)

## Description

Residence built in 1796 by the Ohashi family, foremost among Kurashiki's merchants: tatami reception rooms, private quarters, storehouses and kitchen. Its front gate and forecourt imitate samurai-residence architecture, a sign of the family's status.

## How to get there

A couple of minutes' walk from the northern end of the canal area, across Motomachi-dori (turn right at the second traffic lights walking south from Kurashiki Station).

## Tips

Usually much quieter than the canal-side museums.
