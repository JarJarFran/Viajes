# Yumeji Art Museum

## Description

Small museum built for the centenary of Okayama-born artist Takehisa Yumeji (1884–1934), who developed an Art Nouveau style in Japan; it displays his paintings and designs for songbooks, cards and other items.

## How to get there

In the museum cluster near Korakuen and Okayama Castle, reachable by tram or the Korakuen Bus from Okayama Station.

## Tips

Combine with Korakuen; other nearby museums include the Prefectural Museum of Art (¥360) and Hayashibara Museum of Art (¥500).
