# Korakuen Garden

## Description

Landscape garden commissioned in 1687 by the local feudal lord and opened to the public in 1884, ranked with Kenrokuen (Kanazawa) and Kairakuen (Mito) as one of Japan's three best gardens. Besides a large pond, streams and a lookout hill it has unusually spacious lawns, plum, cherry and maple groves, tea and rice fields and a crane aviary, with Okayama Castle as borrowed scenery.

## How to get there

25–30 min walk (1.5 km) east of Okayama Station, or Higashiyama tram to Shiroshita (5 min, ¥160) then 10–15 min on foot to the south gate.

## Tips

The south gate is next to the bridge to Okayama Castle; the ¥720 combined ticket covers both.
