# Kurashiki Bikan Historical Quarter

## Description

Preserved section of Kurashiki's Edo-period canal system, when the town was a shogunate-controlled rice distribution hub named after its storehouses (kura). Willow-lined canals, stone bridges and white-walled, black-tiled storehouses now house cafes, boutiques and small museums (Folkcraft ¥1,200, Toy Museum ¥600), with a quieter storehouse shopping street one block away.

## How to get there

Day trip: JR Sanyo Line Okayama → Kurashiki 15–20 min (¥330), then 10–15 min walk from the south exit (or bus to Ohara Bijutsukan stop, 2 min, ¥200).

## Tips

Walk through the shopping arcade parallel to Motomachi-dori from the station. The Archaeological Museum (Fri–Sun only, ¥500) is scheduled to close from April 2027 to March 2030.
