# Kibi Plain cycling route (Kibitsu Shrine)

## Description

17 km cycling trail across the rural Kibi Plain, centre of the ancient Kibi Kingdom, between Bizen-Ichinomiya and Soja stations, following the legend of Prince Kibitsuhiko that inspired the Momotaro tale. Highlights are Kibitsu Shrine with its long covered corridor, the Tsukuriyama Kofun burial mound and Bitchu-Kokubunji Temple with its 34 m five-storey pagoda (founded 741).

## How to get there

JR Kibi (Momotaro) Line Okayama → Bizen-Ichinomiya 10 min, ¥210 (JR Pass valid); return from Soja (Kibi Line 35–40 min ¥420, or Hakubi Line 30 min ¥510).

## Tips

The route is easier to follow starting from Bizen-Ichinomiya; bikes can be returned at Soja or near Bitchu-Kokubunji.
