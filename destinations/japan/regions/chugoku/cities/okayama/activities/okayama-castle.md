# Okayama Castle

## Description

Black-walled 'crow castle' of 1597 on the Asahi River, opposite Korakuen; the keep was destroyed in 1945 and rebuilt in 1966, and its six floors hold exhibits on the castle's history. The Tsukimi Yagura (moon-viewing turret, 1620) is the only original building left.

## How to get there

Higashiyama tram to Shiroshita (5 min, ¥160) then 10–15 min walk, or 25–30 min on foot from Okayama Station.

## Tips

A studio in the castle park offers Bizen-yaki pottery making (advance reservation recommended).
