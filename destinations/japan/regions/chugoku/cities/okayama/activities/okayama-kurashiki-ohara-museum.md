# Ohara Museum of Art (Kurashiki)

## Description

Japan's oldest private museum of Western art, founded in 1930 by textile magnate Ohara Magosaburo in memory of painter Kojima Torajiro, with works by El Greco, Gauguin, Modigliani, Picasso, Rodin, Klee, Kandinsky and Pollock. Further buildings show crafts and Asiatic art, and the Kojima Torajiro Memorial Hall is in a former bank nearby (the Annex is currently closed).

## How to get there

Northern end of the Kurashiki canal area; Ohara Bijutsukan bus stop (2 min from Kurashiki Station, ¥200) or 10–15 min walk.

## Tips

Closes at 15:00 from December to February.
