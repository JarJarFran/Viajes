# Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum

## Description

Two-building museum in Peace Memorial Park on the history of Hiroshima, the development of the atomic bomb and above all the events of 6 August 1945 and the human suffering that followed. Personal belongings and testimonies make it an emotionally heavy visit.

## How to get there

Tram line 2 or 6 from Hiroshima Station to Genbaku-Domu Mae (15 min, ¥240), then a short walk through the park; also served by the Meipuru-pu loop bus.

## Tips

Advance (timed) reservation is required for entry during the first hour after opening and the last 90 minutes before closing, and optional at other times (Klook from abroad, Asoview in Japan). Hiroshima City is reviewing the ¥200 fee (a change needs a city ordinance; none decided as of the latest reports).
