# Itsukushima Shrine (Miyajima)

## Description

Shrine founded in its present form by Taira no Kiyomori in 1168, with halls and a noh stage on pillars over a tidal inlet and the 'floating' torii gate standing in the sea. The view of the torii is traditionally ranked among Japan's three best views.

## How to get there

10 min walk from the Miyajima ferry pier (ferry 10 min from Miyajimaguchi, ¥200 + ¥100 tax).

## Tips

Check tide tables: the torii appears to float at high tide and can be reached on foot at low tide. Shrine and torii are lit until 23:00 (the shrine cannot be entered after sunset); the five-storied pagoda is under renovation until Nov 2027. Fee from japan-guide (Jan 2025 update).
