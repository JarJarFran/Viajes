# Peace Memorial Park

## Description

Park of over 120,000 m² on what was the political and commercial heart of the city before the bomb, devoted to peace memorial facilities since 1949. It contains the Cenotaph for the A-Bomb Victims (with a register of over 220,000 names), the Children's Peace Monument and the Flame of Peace, aligned with the A-Bomb Dome across the river.

## How to get there

Tram line 2 or 6 from Hiroshima Station to Genbaku-Domu Mae (15 min, ¥240) or Meipuru-pu loop bus; direct boats to Miyajima leave from the park.

## Tips

On 6 August the annual Peace Memorial Ceremony is held here with a moment of silence at 8:15.
