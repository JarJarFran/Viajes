# Mount Misen and Miyajima Ropeway

## Description

Miyajima's highest peak (about 500 m) with views over the Seto Inland Sea, Buddhist halls near the summit linked to Kobo Daishi, and wild deer on the paths. A two-stage ropeway (~20 min) climbs from Momijidani; three trails (Daisho-in, Momijidani, Omoto) take 1.5–2 h on foot.

## How to get there

Lower ropeway station is a 10 min walk inland from Itsukushima Shrine (20 min from the ferry pier); from the upper station it is still ~30 min on a steep trail to the summit.

## Tips

The Daisho-in course has the best views and is the least steep. The Reikado Hall (the 'eternal flame' that lit Hiroshima's Flame of Peace) burned down in spring 2026 and is to be rebuilt.
