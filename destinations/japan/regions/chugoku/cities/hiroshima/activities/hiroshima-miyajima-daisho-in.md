# Daisho-in Temple (Miyajima)

## Description

Important Shingon Buddhist temple at the foot of Mount Misen with sutra-inscribed prayer wheels along its stairs, the Kannon-do and Maniden halls, a Tibetan sand mandala and a cave with 88 icons representing the Shikoku pilgrimage temples.

## How to get there

5 min walk from Itsukushima Shrine, 15 min from the Miyajima ferry pier.

## Tips

The Daisho-in hiking course to the Misen summit (1.5–2 h) starts from the temple grounds.
