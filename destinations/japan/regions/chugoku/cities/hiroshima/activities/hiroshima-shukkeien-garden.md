# Shukkeien Garden

## Description

'Shrunken-scenery garden' laid out from 1620, just after Hiroshima Castle was completed, where valleys, mountains and forests are reproduced in miniature around a central pond. A path circles the pond past tea houses and the Kokokyo bridge; the garden was rebuilt after 1945.

## How to get there

15 min walk from Hiroshima Station, or Shukkeien-mae tram stop (line 9, change at Hacchobori; ~15 min, ¥240), or 4 min by Meipuru-pu loop bus.

## Tips

Combine with Hiroshima Castle, a 10-minute walk away.
