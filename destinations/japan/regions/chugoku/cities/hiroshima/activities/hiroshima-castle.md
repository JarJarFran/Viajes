# Hiroshima Castle

## Description

'Carp Castle', a flatland castle built in 1589 by Mori Terumoto and destroyed by the atomic bomb; the five-storey keep was rebuilt in ferro-concrete in 1958. Some Ninomaru structures (main gate, turrets and a long storehouse) were reconstructed with traditional methods in the 1990s and can be entered for free.

## How to get there

10 min walk from Kamiyacho-nishi/Kamiyacho-higashi tram stops (lines 1, 2, 6; 12 min, ¥240 from Hiroshima Station) or 6 min by Meipuru-pu loop bus; 15 min walk from Peace Park.

## Tips

The keep's interior (museum and top-floor view) closed permanently to the public in March 2026 over earthquake-resistance concerns; the keep still stands and can be seen from outside, and a wooden rebuild is under discussion.
