# Atomic Bomb Dome (Hiroshima Peace Memorial)

## Description

Ruin of the former Prefectural Industrial Promotion Hall, one of the few buildings left standing near the hypocentre, preserved as the Hiroshima Peace Memorial. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

## How to get there

Next to Genbaku-Domu Mae tram stop (lines 2 and 6, 15 min from Hiroshima Station, ¥240).

## Tips

Seen from across the river at the north end of Peace Memorial Park; the interior cannot be entered.
