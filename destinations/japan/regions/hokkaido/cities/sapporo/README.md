# Sapporo

Capital of Hokkaido and its main gateway via New Chitose Airport. Known for winter events such as the Sapporo Snow Festival in Odori Park and the White Illumination, and for local food such as miso ramen, soup curry, jingisukan lamb barbecue and Sapporo beer.

## How to get there

- From Tokyo by plane: Haneda–New Chitose direct flights take about 1 h 35–1 h 40 (JAL, ANA, Skymark, AIRDO, StarFlyer). Fares depend on the date: JAL showed one-way from ¥12,920 for 29 Nov 2026; tour.ne.jp lists a lowest New Chitose→Haneda fare of ¥8,379 for Aug 2026–Jul 2027.
- New Chitose Airport → Sapporo: JR Rapid Airport about 37 min (Special Rapid fastest 33 min), ¥1,230, trains roughly 6:38–23:21. Some older guides still give ¥1,150 (pre-revision fare).

## Getting around

- Subway (Namboku, Tozai, Toho lines): single fares ¥210 (1 zone) to ¥380 (6 zones); unchanged since Oct 2019.
- Subway 1-day pass ¥830 (any day). Donichika Kippu ¥520: unlimited subway on one Saturday, Sunday, public holiday or 12/29–1/3 day (streetcar and buses not included).
- Since 26 Mar 2026 contactless credit-card taps on the subway are capped per day at the pass price (¥830 weekdays, ¥520 weekends/holidays/year-end).
- The streetcar (shiden) fare was raised in Dec 2024 (current amount not confirmed here).

## Local food

- Miso ramen — Sapporo's signature ramen style (created in the 1950s); try the ramen alley in Susukino or long-running shops such as Aji no Sanpei.
- Soup curry — spicy curry broth with large pieces of vegetables and chicken, a local specialty since the 1970s; popular shops include Garaku, Suage and Magic Spice.
- Jingisukan (Genghis Khan) — mutton/lamb grilled on a domed skillet; widely eaten with Sapporo beer, e.g. at the Sapporo Beer Garden next to the Beer Museum.
- Kaisendon — rice bowl topped with raw seafood (crab, salmon roe, sea urchin); classic breakfast at Nijo Market.
- Other Hokkaido dishes to look for: zangi (fried chicken), butadon (pork bowl), Ishikari nabe (salmon hot pot), soft-serve made with Hokkaido milk.

## Seasons

- Snow season runs roughly from late November to early April; the cold peaks in January–February, with daytime highs around or below freezing.
- Winter events: White Illumination and Munich Christmas Market in Odori Park (late Nov–25 Dec), Sapporo Snow Festival (early February).
- June–August is the mildest, most comfortable period.
- Year-end closures: Clock Tower and Former Hokkaido Government Office close 29 Dec–3 Jan; Mt. Moiwa Ropeway closed 31 Dec 2026–1 Jan 2027; Sapporo Beer Museum closed 31 Dec. Hokkaido Shrine has special New Year hours (31 Dec–7 Jan).
