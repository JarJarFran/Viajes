# Sapporo White Illumination & Munich Christmas Market

## Description

Winter illumination across central Sapporo, with its main venue in Odori Park and a German-style Christmas market. Other venues (Ekimae-dori, Minami 1-jo-dori, station south plaza, etc.) stay lit after Odori closes.
