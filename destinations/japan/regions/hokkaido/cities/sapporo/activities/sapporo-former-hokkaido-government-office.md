# Former Hokkaido Government Office (Akarenga)

## Description

Red-brick former seat of the Hokkaido government, reopened on 25 July 2025 after a major renovation. Exhibits on Hokkaido history, with shops on the ground floor and an optional visit to the octagonal tower balcony.

## How to get there

A few minutes' walk from JR Sapporo Station or the subway's Sapporo Station.

## Tips

The paid exhibition area is separate from the free shops; the tower balcony is a separate ¥1,200 option.
