# Mt. Moiwa Ropeway

## Description

Ropeway and mini cable car up Mt. Moiwa to a summit observatory with a panoramic view over Sapporo, best known for its night view.

## How to get there

Streetcar to Ropeway-Iriguchi stop, then the free shuttle bus or a short walk to the base station.

## Tips

One listing (Mar 2026) shows ¥1,800 round trip; ¥2,100 appears in more recent guides. Times Club members get ¥200 off.
