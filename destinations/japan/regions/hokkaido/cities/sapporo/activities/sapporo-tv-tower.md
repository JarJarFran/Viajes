# Sapporo TV Tower

## Description

Broadcast tower at the east end of Odori Park with an observation deck looking down the length of the park. A popular spot to see the White Illumination and Snow Festival from above.

## How to get there

At the east end of Odori Park, a short walk from Odori Station.

## Tips

Some listings still show the old ¥1,000 adult price; ¥1,200 is the figure listed as of April 2026.
