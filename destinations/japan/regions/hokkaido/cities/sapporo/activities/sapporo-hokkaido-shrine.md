# Hokkaido Shrine

## Description

Hokkaido's principal Shinto shrine, set in a forest next to Maruyama Park. It draws large crowds for New Year's first shrine visit.

## How to get there

About 15 minutes' walk from Maruyama-Koen Station (Tozai Line).

## Tips

One guide gives a simpler Nov–Mar 7:00–16:00 schedule; the Visit Hokkaido listing gives 7:00–17:00 for March. Hours differ from Dec 31 to Jan 7.
