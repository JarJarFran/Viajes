# Odori Park

## Description

Long park strip through central Sapporo that hosts the city's main events. In winter it is the main venue of the White Illumination and the Munich Christmas Market (West 1–2 chome, about 45 stalls, Nov 20–Dec 25, 2026) and, in early February, the snow sculptures of the Sapporo Snow Festival (West 1–11 chome in 2027).

## How to get there

Directly above Odori Station (Namboku, Tozai and Toho subway lines).

## Tips

The Odori illumination venue ends on Dec 25; afterwards only other White Illumination venues (e.g. Minami 1-jo-dori, until Mar 14, 2027) stay lit.
