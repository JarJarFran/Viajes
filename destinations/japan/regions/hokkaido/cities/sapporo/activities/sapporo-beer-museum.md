# Sapporo Beer Museum

## Description

Museum on the history of Sapporo beer in a historic red-brick building, reopened after a 'value-up' renovation on 1 July 2026 for the brand's 150th anniversary. Tickets now come as self-guided entry or a guided tour ending in a tasting lounge.

## How to get there

Bus from central Sapporo or a walk of roughly 10 minutes from Higashi-Kuyakusho-mae Station (Toho Line).

## Tips

Check current prices and hours on the official site; advance tickets went on sale in June 2026. The Sapporo Beer Garden next door serves jingisukan with beer.
