# Moerenuma Park

## Description

Large park on the north-eastern edge of Sapporo designed as a single sculpture by Isamu Noguchi, with an artificial hill, fountains and the Glass Pyramid 'HIDAMARI'.

## How to get there

From Kanjo-dori-higashi Station (Toho Line) take bus 東69 or 東79 to Moerenuma-koen Higashi-guchi, about 25 min.

## Tips

In winter use the east entrance (west and south are closed). Bus fare is given as ¥210 or ¥240 depending on the source.
