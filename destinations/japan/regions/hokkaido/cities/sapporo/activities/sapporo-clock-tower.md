# Sapporo Clock Tower

## Description

Wooden building from the Sapporo Agricultural College era, one of the symbols of the city, with a small museum inside about its history and the clock mechanism.

## How to get there

About 5 minutes' walk from Odori Station.

## Tips

Older guides list ¥200 admission; the official site gives ¥350. Year-end closure follows the city rule (Dec 29–Jan 3); some guides only mention Jan 1–3, and one older listing mentions a 4th-Monday closure in some months.
