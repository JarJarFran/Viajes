# Otaru City Museum (Canal Building and Railway Museum)

## Description

Two city museums: the Canal Building (Ungakan) in a canal-side warehouse covers Otaru's history and nature, from the Ainu to the 1970s canal preservation movement; the Railway Museum (Honkan) on the site of the terminus of Hokkaido's first railway displays full-size trains from several periods.

## How to get there

Canal Building beside the canal, 10 min on foot from Otaru Station; Railway Museum by bus 2 or 10 to Sogo Hakubutsukan (10 min, ¥240) or a further 15-20 min walk west.

## Tips

English pamphlet available. The Railway Museum is the one closed on Tuesdays.
