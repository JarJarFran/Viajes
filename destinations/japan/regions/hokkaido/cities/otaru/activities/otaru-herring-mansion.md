# Herring Mansion (Nishin Goten)

## Description

Large wooden herring-fishery mansion built in 1897 for a fishing magnate and moved here from Tomari in 1958. It shows the workers' sleeping quarters and fishing tools beside the owner's rooms, on a hill above the sea about 5 km from central Otaru.

## How to get there

City bus 10 or 11 from Otaru Station to the Otaru Aquarium terminal (20-25 min, ¥240), then a 5-minute walk.

## Tips

Closed in winter; the Former Aoyama Villa, 1 km inland, stays open. Combine with Otaru Aquarium nearby.
