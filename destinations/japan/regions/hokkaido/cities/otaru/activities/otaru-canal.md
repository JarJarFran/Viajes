# Otaru Canal

## Description

Part of the early-20th-century port canal, restored in the 1980s after a citizens' movement saved it from being filled in. The stone warehouses along it now hold museums, shops and restaurants, and gas lamps light the path in the evening.

## How to get there

10-minute walk (or 2-minute bus ride) down the main road from JR Otaru Station.

## Tips

Main venue of the Snow Light Path festival, when candles float on the canal. Combine with Sakaimachi Street, a short walk away.
