# Nikka Whisky Yoichi Distillery

## Description

Distillery founded in 1934 by Taketsuru Masataka, the 'father of Japanese whisky', in neighboring Yoichi Town. A self-guided walk of about an hour through the original stone buildings follows the whisky process and ends with a museum on the company and its founder and a tasting room.

## How to get there

A few steps from JR Yoichi Station: about 25 min by local train from Otaru (¥470, hourly) or about 1 hour from Sapporo (¥1,380, change at Otaru).

## Tips

Guided tours are in Japanese only; English displays and audio guides cover the self-guided route. Check the official site for any advance reservation requirement before going.
