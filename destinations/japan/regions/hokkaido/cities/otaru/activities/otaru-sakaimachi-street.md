# Sakaimachi Street

## Description

Preserved merchant street of late-1800s/early-1900s Western-style trading-company buildings, now restaurants, cafes, sweet shops, glassware shops and museums. At its southern end are the Otaru Music Box Museum (a large music-box shop with a small museum) and a steam clock donated by Vancouver; nearby is the former Bank of Japan Otaru branch (1912), a free museum about money.

## How to get there

10-minute walk from JR Otaru Station or 5 minutes from Minami-Otaru Station.

## Tips

Several glass shops let visitors make their own cup or vase. The Bank of Japan museum is closed on Wednesdays.
