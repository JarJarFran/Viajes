# Mount Tenguyama Ropeway

## Description

Ropeway up Mount Tenguyama, which rises behind the city, with views over Otaru and the bay. In winter the mountain has a small ski area, and during the Snow Light Path festival the upper station is one of the venues.

## How to get there

Bus 9 from Otaru Station to the lower ropeway station (20 min, ¥240, every 30 min).

## Tips

Fares differ between sources (¥1,600, ¥1,800 and ¥2,000 round trip appear); check before going and check whether it is running in April or November.
