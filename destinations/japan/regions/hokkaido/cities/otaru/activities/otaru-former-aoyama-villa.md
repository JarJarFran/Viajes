# Former Aoyama Villa

## Description

Luxurious villa and gardens built by the Aoyama family, one of the richest herring-fishing families, with no expense spared on materials. It stands about 1 km inland from the Herring Mansion.

## How to get there

City bus 10 or 11 from Otaru Station to the Otaru Aquarium terminal (20-25 min, ¥240), then a 15-minute walk.

## Tips

The family's own herring mansion was moved to the Historic Village of Hokkaido in Sapporo.
