# Otaru Aquarium

## Description

Hokkaido's oldest existing aquarium, opened in 1958, on the coast about 5 km from central Otaru next to the Herring Mansion.

## How to get there

City bus 10 or 11 from Otaru Station to the Otaru Aquarium terminal (20-25 min, ¥240).

## Tips

Check the season dates: it is closed in late November-early December and runs shorter hours in winter.
