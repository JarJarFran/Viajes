# Otaru

Small harbor city about half an hour northwest of Sapporo by train, known for its restored canal lined with old warehouses, Western-style merchant buildings along Sakaimachi Street, glassware and music boxes. It was Hokkaido's main port in the late 1800s and the terminus of the island's first railway; most visitors come on a day trip from Sapporo.

## How to get there

- From Sapporo: JR Hakodate Main Line, several trains per hour; 30 min by rapid train or 45 min by local train, ¥800 one way (covered by the Japan Rail Pass).
- From New Chitose Airport: about two trains per hour run through from Otaru to the airport (70 min, ¥2,040 one way).
- By ferry from Honshu: Shin Nihonkai Ferry from Niigata (18 h, from about ¥8,500) and Maizuru (20 h, from about ¥12,000); the ferry terminal is a 10-min bus ride from Otaru-Chikko Station.

## Getting around

- Most sights are within a 30-minute walk of Otaru Station (the canal is 10 min on foot down the main road).
- Hokkaido Chuo Bus city buses: ¥240 one way; bus 10/11 to Otaru Aquarium and the Herring Mansion (20-25 min, every 20-30 min), bus 9 to the Tenguyama Ropeway (20 min, every 30 min), bus 2/10 to the Railway Museum. No day-pass price was confirmed.

## Local food

- Sushi and fresh seafood: Otaru is known for fresh sushi, with many sushi restaurants in the city center.
- Otaru ankake yakisoba: fried noodles topped with a thick starchy sauce, a regional variety promoted as a local dish.
- Otaru Beer: German-style brewery restaurant in a warehouse next to the canal.

## Seasons

- Winter (Dec-Mar) is snowy: the Snow Light Path festival lights the canal and the old Temiyasen railway path with snow lanterns in early February (2027: Feb 6-13, 17:00-21:00).
- Winter closures: the Herring Mansion is closed from late November to early April; Otaru Aquarium closes between seasons (Nov 24-Dec 11, 2026) and runs a reduced winter season Dec 12, 2026-Feb 23, 2027, 10:00-16:00; the Tenguyama Ropeway stops for maintenance in parts of April and November.
- Several museums shorten winter hours (Former Aoyama Villa until 16:00 Nov-Mar; Bank of Japan museum opens 10:00 Dec-Mar). Mount Tenguyama has a small ski area in winter.
