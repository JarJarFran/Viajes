# Hakodate

Hokkaido's third-largest city, at the island's southern tip, best known for the night view from Mount Hakodate (counted among Japan's three best night views) and for fresh seafood, especially squid. One of the first Japanese ports opened to foreign trade in 1854, it keeps a Western-influenced Motomachi district, red brick warehouses and the star-shaped Fort Goryokaku.

## How to get there

- From Tokyo by train: Tohoku/Hokkaido Shinkansen (Hayabusa, seat reservation mandatory) to Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto in about 4 h, then the Hakodate Liner relay train to Hakodate Station (about 20 min); under 5 h in total, around ¥25,000 one way; covered by the Japan Rail Pass and the JR East South Hokkaido Rail Pass.
- From Tokyo by air: Haneda-Hakodate about 80 min (JAL, ANA, Air Do; Spring Japan also listed). Discount fares typically ¥14,000-25,000; lowest fares seen for late Oct-late Nov 2026: Spring Japan ¥7,463, Air Do ¥12,605. Airport shuttle bus to Hakodate Station 20 min, ¥500.
- From Sapporo: JR limited express Hokuto (all seats reserved), about 3 h 40 min, 12 round trips a day; most trains stop at Onuma-Koen and Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto.

## Getting around

- Two tram lines (2 and 5) link Yunokawa Onsen, Goryokaku, Hakodate Station, Jujigai (for Motomachi, the bay area and the ropeway) and Yachigashira/Hakodate Dock; fares ¥210-260 by distance (figures as of late 2024).
- Passes: 1-day Tram Pass ¥600 (paper ticket sold at Hakodate Station information desks and on trams, or digital); 24-hour Tram Pass ¥900 (digital only); Tram and Bus Pass 1-day ¥1,400 / 2-day ¥2,400 (digital only, excludes the airport shuttle).
- IC cards: ICAS nimoca, Suica, ICOCA and other national IC cards work on trams, buses (not the airport shuttle) and JR between Hakodate and Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto.
- Mount Hakodate: ropeway from Motomachi (10 min walk from Jujigai tram stop), or direct Hakodate Bus from Hakodate Station to the summit in the evenings from late April to mid November (30 min, ¥500).

## Local food

- Seafood rice bowls (kaisendon, e.g. uni-ikura don): breakfast at the Hakodate Morning Market next to Hakodate Station.
- Squid (ika): Hakodate's city fish, served very fresh as sashimi, including 'dancing squid' at market restaurants.
- Hakodate shio ramen: ramen with a pale, clear salt-based broth, the local style (Sapporo is miso, Asahikawa soy sauce).
- Sushi and crab: snow crab, sea urchin and other Hokkaido seafood are sold and served around the morning market.

## Seasons

- Winter (Dec-Mar) is snowy (about 380 cm of snow a year). The ropeway runs 10:00-21:00 from October 1 to about April 19 (until 22:00 in summer); the direct summit bus runs only from late April to mid November and the summit road is open only from mid April to mid November, so in winter the ropeway is the way up.
- Hakodate Christmas Fantasy lights a large tree on the water in the bay area Dec 1-25 (fireworks daily at 18:00).
- The Russian Orthodox Church in Motomachi closes to visitors from Dec 26 to mid March; other Motomachi buildings close only around New Year.
- Cherry blossoms at Fort Goryokaku usually peak around early May; the Hakodate Port Festival (squid dance) is held in August.
