# Mount Hakodate (Ropeway and Observatory)

## Description

334 m wooded mountain at the tip of the peninsula, whose night view over the narrow city between two bays is counted among Japan's three best night views (with Nagasaki's Mount Inasa and Kobe's Mount Rokko). The summit has free observation platforms, souvenir shops, a cafe and a restaurant.

## How to get there

Ropeway lower station: 10-min walk from Jujigai tram stop (5 min from Hakodate Station, ¥210) or direct Hakodate Bus from Hakodate Station (10 min, ¥250).

## Tips

The summit gets very crowded around sunset; arrive before dusk. The summit road and direct bus do not run from mid November to April, so in winter the ropeway is the way up.
