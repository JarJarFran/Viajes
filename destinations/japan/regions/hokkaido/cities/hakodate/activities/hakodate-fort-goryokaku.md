# Fort Goryokaku and Former Magistrate's Office

## Description

Massive star-shaped Western-style citadel built at the end of the Edo period, where shogunate forces made their last stand against the Meiji government in 1869. It became a public park in the 1910s; part of the Magistrate's Office in the center was reconstructed and opened in 2010.

## How to get there

Tram from Hakodate Station to Goryokaku-Koen-mae (15 min, ¥230), then 10 min on foot.

## Tips

Climb Goryokaku Tower next to the park to see the star shape. One of Hokkaido's best cherry blossom spots.
