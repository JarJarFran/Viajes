# Kanemori Red Brick Warehouses

## Description

Waterfront red brick warehouses from Hakodate's early trading days, converted into a shopping and dining complex with souvenir and sweets shops, a restaurant, a beer hall, a wedding chapel and bay cruises.

## How to get there

15-20 min walk from Hakodate Station or 5 min from Jujigai tram stop (5 min, ¥210).

## Tips

Venue area of the Hakodate Christmas Fantasy in December.
