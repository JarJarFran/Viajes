# Motomachi District

## Description

Slope district at the foot of Mount Hakodate where foreign traders settled after the port opened in 1854. Its Western buildings include the Russian Orthodox Church (Holy Resurrection Church), the Old British Consulate, the Chinese Memorial Hall, the former prefectural branch office and the Old Hakodate Public Hall; the Hachiman-zaka slope looks down to the port.

## How to get there

5-min tram ride from Hakodate Station (to Jujigai or Suehirocho) or a 20-30 min walk.

## Tips

The Orthodox Church interior is closed from Dec 26 to mid March. The ropeway lower station is in the district.
