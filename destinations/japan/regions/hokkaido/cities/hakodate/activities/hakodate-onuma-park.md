# Onuma Quasi-National Park

## Description

Island-dotted lakes Onuma and Konuma beneath the volcano Mount Komagatake, about 20 km north of Hakodate. Walking paths of 15-60 minutes cross small bridges between islands; a 10 km cycling course circles Lake Onuma.

## How to get there

JR from Hakodate to Onuma-Koen Station: about 30 min by limited express (¥1,840, reserved seat required) or 40 min by local train (¥680); most Hakodate-Sapporo Hokuto trains stop there.

## Tips

Easy stop-over on the train journey between Hakodate and Sapporo. Boat tours run only in the warmer months.
