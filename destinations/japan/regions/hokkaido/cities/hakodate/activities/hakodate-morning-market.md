# Hakodate Morning Market

## Description

Market of about four city blocks selling crab, salmon roe, sea urchin, squid and produce such as melons, with many restaurants serving seafood rice-bowl breakfasts.

## How to get there

A few steps from JR Hakodate Station.

## Tips

Go early for breakfast; many stalls wind down well before the 14:00 close.
