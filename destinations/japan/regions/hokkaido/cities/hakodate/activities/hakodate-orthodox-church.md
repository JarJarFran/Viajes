# Hakodate Russian Orthodox Church

## Description

White Russian Orthodox church (Holy Resurrection Church) in Motomachi, one of the district's landmarks; its bell is listed among the 100 Soundscapes of Japan.

## How to get there

In Motomachi, a short uphill walk from Jujigai or Suehirocho tram stops (5 min from Hakodate Station).

## Tips

Exterior can be seen all year; interior closed Dec 26 to mid March.
