# Goryokaku Tower

## Description

107 m observation tower next to Fort Goryokaku, built in 2006 to replace a 60 m predecessor, giving a bird's-eye view of the star-shaped fortress.

## How to get there

Tram to Goryokaku-Koen-mae (15 min from Hakodate Station, ¥230), then about 10 min on foot.
