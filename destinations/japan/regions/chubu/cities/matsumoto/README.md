# Matsumoto

City in Nagano Prefecture at the foot of the Japanese Alps, known for Matsumoto Castle, one of Japan's original castle keeps. Its compact centre also has the Nakamachi and Nawate old streets, the Former Kaichi School (a National Treasure) and the Matsumoto City Museum of Art, and it is a gateway to the Kamikochi area.

## How to get there

- From Tokyo: JR limited express Azusa from Shinjuku, direct, about 2h36m-2h37m; about ¥6,730 (¥4,180 fare + ¥2,550 express charge) per a fare search dated 26 Oct 2026; other guides give ¥6,500-6,800.
- One source says a JR East pass covers Matsumoto; Japan Rail Pass coverage was not confirmed.

## Getting around

- Matsumoto Castle, Nakamachi, Nawate Street, Yohashira Shrine, the City Museum of Art and the Former Kaichi School are walkable from each other or served by local buses (per one guide).
- Local bus day-pass prices not confirmed in this research.

## What to see and do

- [Matsumoto Castle](activities/matsumoto-castle.md) — ¥1,300 adults with a paper ticket at the castle, ¥1,200 with an online e-ticket; elementary and junior-high students ¥400 (e-ticket). Older guides list ¥700.
- [Former Kaichi School](activities/matsumoto-former-kaichi-school.md) — ¥700 adults at the desk, ¥600 online (recent sources); an older guide lists ¥400
- [Matsumoto City Museum of Art](activities/matsumoto-city-museum-of-art.md) — Permanent collection ¥800 adults at the desk, ¥700 online; special exhibitions extra
- [Nakamachi Street](activities/matsumoto-nakamachi-street.md) — Free (open street)
- [Nawate Street](activities/matsumoto-nawate-street.md) — Free (open street)

## Seasons

- Matsumoto Castle closes 29-31 Dec and opens with short hours on 1-3 Jan (10:00-15:30); some sources mention extended hours in Golden Week and summer.
- A seismic survey of Matsumoto Castle found earthquake concerns, and reinforcement work was scheduled to begin later in 2026 (one source); check for access restrictions.
- Matsumoto City Museum of Art and the Former Kaichi School close over the year-end and New Year holidays.
