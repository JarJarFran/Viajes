# Kanazawa

Capital of Ishikawa Prefecture on the Sea of Japan coast, known for Kenrokuen garden next to Kanazawa Castle Park, the Higashi Chaya teahouse district, the Nagamachi samurai district and the 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art. It is reached directly from Tokyo by the Hokuriku Shinkansen.

## How to get there

- From Tokyo: Hokuriku Shinkansen. Fastest Kagayaki about 2h26m-2h30m (e.g. Kagayaki 503: Tokyo 07:20, Kanazawa 09:44); Kagayaki trains are all-reserved. A 2026 guide gives ¥14,600 reserved (regular season) and ¥14,070 unreserved on the slower Hakutaka; other sources give ¥14,120 or ¥14,400-15,000 depending on season, so check JR East before booking.
- From Osaka/Kyoto: since March 2024 the Thunderbird limited express runs only to Tsuruga, where you change to the Hokuriku Shinkansen; one guide gives about 2h30m from Osaka. Fares found online disagree and were not confirmed.
- Hokuriku Arch Pass: ¥35,000 for 7 consecutive days (per one 2026 source, as of 14 Mar 2026); buying single tickets is usually cheaper for a Tokyo-Kanazawa round trip.

## Getting around

- Kanazawa Cultural Zone Pass: one guide lists ¥1,000 for 2 days covering 16 sites, including Kenrokuen, the paid areas of Kanazawa Castle Park and the Nomura samurai house; 21st Century Museum collection exhibitions are also covered.
- City loop-bus day-pass price and conditions not confirmed in this research.

## What to see and do

- [Kenrokuen Garden](activities/kanazawa-kenrokuen.md) — ¥320 adults (18+), ¥100 ages 6-17; free early-morning entry before regular opening (per guides)
- [Kanazawa Castle Park](activities/kanazawa-castle-park.md) — Grounds free; reconstructed turrets and storehouse ¥320 adults
- [21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art, Kanazawa](activities/kanazawa-21st-century-museum.md) — Free public zone; exhibition tickets vary by exhibition, typically ¥500-1,500
- [Higashi Chaya District](activities/kanazawa-higashi-chaya.md) — Street free; teahouses charge separately (Shima teahouse ¥500 per one guide)
- [Omicho Market](activities/kanazawa-omicho-market.md) — Free to enter; pay per stall/restaurant
- [Nagamachi Samurai District and Nomura Samurai House](activities/kanazawa-nomura-samurai-house.md) — District free; Nomura House ¥550

## Seasons

- Kenrokuen keeps seasonal hours: 7:00-18:00 from March to October and 8:00-17:00 from November to February (per one guide; sources differ).
- 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art closes for major repairs (ageing building plus damage from the 1 Jan 2024 Noto Peninsula earthquake) from 6 May 2027 until about March 2028 (planned). Its last exhibition before closing, 'From Today to Tomorrow', runs 31 Oct 2026 - 18 Apr 2027.
- New Year: the 21st Century Museum's exhibition zone closes during the New Year holidays.
