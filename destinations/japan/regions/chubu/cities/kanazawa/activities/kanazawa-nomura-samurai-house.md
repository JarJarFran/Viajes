# Nagamachi Samurai District and Nomura Samurai House

## Description

Former samurai quarter of earthen walls and narrow lanes; the restored Nomura family residence has a small garden and can be visited inside.

## Tips

The Nomura House is covered by the Kanazawa Cultural Zone Pass (per one guide).
