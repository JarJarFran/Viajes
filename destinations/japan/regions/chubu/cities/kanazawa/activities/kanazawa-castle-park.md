# Kanazawa Castle Park

## Description

Park on the site of the Maeda clan's castle, with rebuilt gates, turrets and storehouses that can be entered for a fee. It sits directly across from Kenrokuen.

## Tips

Visit together with Kenrokuen; the paid interiors are included in the Kanazawa Cultural Zone Pass.
