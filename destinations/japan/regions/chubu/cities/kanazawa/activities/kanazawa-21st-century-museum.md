# 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art, Kanazawa

## Description

Circular glass museum of contemporary art with a free public zone and paid exhibition galleries. Its last exhibition before the long closure is 'From Today to Tomorrow' (31 Oct 2026 - 18 Apr 2027).

## Tips

Closed Mondays (next weekday if Monday is a holiday). Plan visits before 6 May 2027; the reopening date is a plan and may change.
