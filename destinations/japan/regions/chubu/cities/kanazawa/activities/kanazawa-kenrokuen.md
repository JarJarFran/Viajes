# Kenrokuen Garden

## Description

Landscape garden of the Kaga feudal lords next to Kanazawa Castle Park, ranked among Japan's three great gardens. It is the city's main sight.

## Tips

Combine with Kanazawa Castle Park across the road; the Kanazawa Cultural Zone Pass covers the entry fee. Hours differ between sources, so check before an early visit.
