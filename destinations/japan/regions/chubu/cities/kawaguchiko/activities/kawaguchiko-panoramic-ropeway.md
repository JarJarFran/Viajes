# Mt. Fuji Panoramic Ropeway (Kachi Kachi Ropeway)

## Description

3-minute ropeway from the east shore of Lake Kawaguchi up Mt Tenjo to a viewpoint over the lake and Mt Fuji.

## How to get there

~15 min walk from Kawaguchiko Station or the Omni/Red Line sightseeing bus.

## Tips

Round trip ¥1,000 (some sources ¥900). Winter hours may be shorter (one source: Dec–Feb 9:30–15:30); closes for maintenance in mid-February and in strong wind — check mtfujiropeway.jp.
