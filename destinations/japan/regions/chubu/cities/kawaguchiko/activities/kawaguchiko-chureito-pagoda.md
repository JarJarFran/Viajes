# Chureito Pagoda (Arakurayama Sengen Park)

## Description

Five-storey pagoda on the hillside above Fujiyoshida with Mt Fuji behind it — the classic Fuji postcard view.

## How to get there

Fujikyu Railway to Shimoyoshida (two stops before Kawaguchiko), then ~15 min walk and ~400 steps up from the Arakura Fuji Sengen Shrine.

## Tips

Free and open 24 h, but the stairs are unlit — go in the morning, when Fuji is most often clear in winter. Not wheelchair accessible.
