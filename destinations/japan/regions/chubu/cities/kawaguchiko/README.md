# Kawaguchiko

Largest and most visited of the Fuji Five Lakes, at the northern foot of Mt Fuji. Winter mornings give the clearest views of the mountain.

## How to get there

- Highway bus from Busta Shinjuku to Kawaguchiko Station (~2 h, ¥2,200).
- JR Chuo Line to Otsuki + Fujikyu Railway (~2 h); Shimoyoshida (Chureito Pagoda) is two stops before Kawaguchiko.
- Around the lake: Retro sightseeing buses (Red/Green/Blue lines) from Kawaguchiko Station.

## What to see and do

- [Chureito Pagoda (Arakurayama Sengen Park)](activities/kawaguchiko-chureito-pagoda.md) — free, ~400 steps, best in the morning
- [Mt. Fuji Panoramic Ropeway](activities/kawaguchiko-panoramic-ropeway.md) — ¥1,000 return
