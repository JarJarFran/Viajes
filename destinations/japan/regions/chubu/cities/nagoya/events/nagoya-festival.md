# Nagoya Festival

## Description

Nagoya's main city festival, normally with a costume procession of the three unifiers (Oda Nobunaga, Toyotomi Hideyoshi and Tokugawa Ieyasu), plus bon dances, festival-float music and karakuri puppet shows.
