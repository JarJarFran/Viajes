# Atsuta Festival (Shobu-sai)

## Description

Atsuta Jingu's most important annual festival, attended by an imperial envoy. Martial arts and performing arts are offered in the grounds. In the evening the makiwara lantern displays are lit and about 1,000 fireworks are launched from Jingu Park.
