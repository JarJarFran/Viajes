# Atsuta Jingu

## Description

One of Nagoya's main shrines. Its annual festival on 5 June is attended by an imperial envoy. The grounds hold a Treasure Hall and the Kusanagi-kan, a treasury dedicated to swords.

## Tips

The Treasure Hall closes 25-31 Dec and once a month: the city tourism site says the last Thursday and the day before, other sites say the last Wednesday and the day after. Miya Kishimen inside the grounds serves kishimen noodles.
