# SCMAGLEV and Railway Park

## Description

JR Central's railway museum. It runs themed exhibitions; the 15th covers Tokaido Shinkansen maintenance and service vehicles.

## Tips

When a Tuesday is a public holiday the museum opens and closes on Wednesday instead (e.g. open 3 Nov 2026, closed 4 Nov). Older guides show ¥1,000; that fee is out of date.
