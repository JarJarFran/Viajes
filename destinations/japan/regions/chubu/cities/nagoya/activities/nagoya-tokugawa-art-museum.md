# Tokugawa Art Museum

## Description

Art museum whose collection includes the National Treasure Tale of Genji illustrated handscrolls. These are shown only for short special periods. Exhibitions change through the year.

## Tips

On holiday Mondays it opens and closes on Tuesday instead (2026: open 12 Oct and 23 Nov, closed 13 Oct and 24 Nov). Student prices differ between listings (¥1,200 vs ¥1,600 for high school and university students).
