# Toyota Commemorative Museum of Industry and Technology

## Description

Toyota group museum on the history of industry and technology. From 3 Oct to 13 Dec 2026 its special exhibition room shows 'Japonisme' pieces from the Toyota collection, included in the regular ticket.

## Tips

Same-day re-entry is allowed. Older listings give ¥500 for adults; the current fee is ¥1,000.
