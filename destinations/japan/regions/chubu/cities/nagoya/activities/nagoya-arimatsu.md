# Arimatsu historic townscape

## Description

Town founded in 1608 on the Tokaido road. It developed shibori tie-dyeing as a local industry because it lacked farmland. Its streets, about 500-800 m long, are a protected preservation district.

## How to get there

Meitetsu Nagoya Main Line from Meitetsu Nagoya to Arimatsu Station (about 20-22 min), then a 5-min walk.

## Tips

The Shibori Kaikan closes 28 Dec-4 Jan. Meitetsu's 'Arimatsu Stroll Ticket' (19 Sep-6 Dec 2026) bundles a round-trip ticket from Nagoya (¥1,900) with a Shibori Kaikan discount.
