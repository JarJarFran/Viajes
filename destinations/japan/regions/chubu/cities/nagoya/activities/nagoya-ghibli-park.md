# Ghibli Park

## Description

Studio Ghibli park in Aichi Prefecture with five areas, among them Ghibli's Grand Warehouse, Mononoke Village and the Valley of Witches. The Standard pass covers those three; the Premium pass covers all five areas and the buildings that otherwise need same-day tickets (Okino Residence, Howl's Castle, House of Witches).

## Tips

Tickets are reserve-only. They go on sale on the 10th at 14:00 JST, two months ahead. Sources disagree on whether single-area tickets are still sold to overseas buyers.
