# Nagoya Castle

## Description

Castle whose keep carries a pair of golden shachihoko on the roof. The keep's interior is closed to visitors, but the grounds and the rebuilt Honmaru Palace, opened in full on 8 Jun 2018, can be visited.

## Tips

Admission was free during the Nagoya Festival (17-18 Oct 2026). In Feb 2027 the city plans a trial making the grounds outside the Honmaru free, with tickets checked only at a temporary gate in front of the Omote-ninomon. One older city leaflet lists Monday closures, but current listings give only the year-end closure.
