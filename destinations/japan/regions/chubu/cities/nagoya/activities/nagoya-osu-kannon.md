# Osu Kannon and Osu shopping district

## Description

Buddhist temple (Shinpuku-ji Hosho-in) at the edge of the Osu shopping district. It holds an antique market on the 18th and 28th of every month.

## How to get there

Osu Kannon Station (Tsurumai Line), exit 2, then a 3-min walk.

## Tips

The antique market runs from 5:00 to 16:00, rain or shine. The 28th is also an en-nichi (temple fair) day, with freshly pounded rice cakes handed out from 18:00.
