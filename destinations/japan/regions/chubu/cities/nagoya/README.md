# Nagoya

Capital of Aichi Prefecture, known for Nagoya Castle with the golden shachihoko on its roof, the major Atsuta Jingu shrine and industry museums such as the Toyota Commemorative Museum and JR Central's SCMAGLEV and Railway Park. It is also known for its own cuisine ('Nagoya-meshi'): miso katsu, hitsumabushi, tebasaki and kishimen.

## How to get there

- From Tokyo: Tokaido Shinkansen 'Nozomi' about 1h33-1h40, reserved seat ¥11,300 (regular season; one guide lists ¥10,880); 'Kodama' about 2h40.
- The Nozomi reserved-seat express surcharge rises on 1 Jul 2027 (Tokyo-Nagoya +¥580, to ¥11,880); unreserved seats keep the current price.
- Budget option: JR Central Tours 'Platt Kodama' package, Tokyo-Nagoya from ¥8,710 (regular season, Kodama trains only, bought in advance).

## Getting around

- Subway and city-bus all-lines one-day ticket: ¥870 adults.
- 'Donichi Eco Kippu': ¥620 adults (¥310 children) on Saturdays, Sundays, public holidays and the 8th of every month; covers all subway and city-bus lines, including the 'Me-guru' sightseeing loop bus. Showing it gets discounts at city sights. Not valid on Meitetsu or the Aonami Line.
- Since 3 Sep 2026 all subway stations accept contactless credit-card (touch) payment at the gates.
- Meitetsu Nagoya Main Line from Meitetsu Nagoya (next to JR Nagoya Station) to Arimatsu takes about 20-22 min; IC cards (manaca, TOICA, Suica) are accepted.

## Local food

- Miso katsu (pork cutlet with red miso sauce): Yabaton, which has served it since 1947; main branch in Sakae, other branches around the city, with queues at lunch.
- Hitsumabushi (grilled eel over rice, eaten in three ways): Atsuta Horaiken Honten near Atsuta Jingu (English menu); the Hitsumabushi Bincho chain.
- Tebasaki (fried chicken wings): Torigin Honten; Gomitori Honten serves a full Nagoya-meshi menu.
- Kishimen (flat udon noodles): Miya Kishimen in the Atsuta Jingu grounds; Kishimen-tei and Kishimen Yoshida in the ESCA underground mall at Nagoya Station.

## Seasons

- Spring: Nagoya Castle Spring Festival during cherry-blossom season (2026: 20 Mar-6 May).
- Early summer: Atsuta Festival on 5 June with lanterns and fireworks; Arimatsu Shibori Festival in early June.
- Autumn: Nagoya Castle Autumn Festival with extended evening hours (2026: 19 Sep-23 Nov; 9:00-19:30 until 4 Oct). The Nagoya Festival is held on a weekend in October.
- Year-end closures: Nagoya Castle 29 Dec-1 Jan, SCMAGLEV and Railway Park 28 Dec-1 Jan, Atsuta Jingu Treasure Hall 25-31 Dec; the Toyota Commemorative Museum also closes over the New Year.
- Ghibli Park closes on Tuesdays and the Toyota museum on Mondays; Tuesdays also close the SCMAGLEV and Railway Park outside school holidays.
