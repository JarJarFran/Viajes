# Takayama Jinya

## Description

Former local government office of the shogunate in the Hida region, the only surviving Edo-period government office of its kind in Japan. The morning market is held in the square in front of it.

## Tips

Combine with the Jinya-mae morning market in front of the gate. Closing days differ between sources: 29 Dec-3 Jan in one, only 29 Dec, 31 Dec and 1 Jan in others.
