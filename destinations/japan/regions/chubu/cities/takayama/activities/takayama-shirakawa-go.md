# Shirakawa-go (Ogimachi village)

## Description

Mountain village of steep thatched gassho-style farmhouses, visited as a day trip from Takayama or Kanazawa by Nohi Bus (about 50 min from Takayama).

## How to get there

Nohi Bus from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center next to JR Takayama Station, about 50 min; some departures require a reservation.

## Tips

Book reserved bus departures (marked R on the timetable) in advance. Day-trip and overnight options exist; winter light-up evenings need booking.
