# Takayama

Town in the Hida mountains of Gifu Prefecture, known for its preserved Edo-period old town, morning markets along the Miyagawa river and in front of Takayama Jinya, and the Takayama Jinya, Japan's only surviving Edo-period local government office. It is the usual base for bus day trips to the gassho-style farmhouse village of Shirakawa-go.

## How to get there

- From Nagoya: JR limited express Hida, about 2h20m-2h40m depending on stops (about 2.5 h); ten return services a day between Nagoya and Takayama/Hida-Furukawa/Toyama.
- Hida fares differ between sources: Ekitan lists ¥5,610 unreserved and ¥6,140 reserved (Green car ¥8,410); another Ekitan page shows ¥6,540 reserved and a guide with July 2026 fares lists ¥6,340. Check JR Central before travel.
- Reserved seats normally go on sale one month before travel at 10:00 Japan time.

## Getting around

- Long-distance and Shirakawa-go buses (Nohi Bus) leave from the Takayama Nohi Bus Center beside JR Takayama Station.
- Takayama to Shirakawa-go by Nohi Bus: about 50 min, ¥2,800 one way for adults, rising to ¥2,900 from 1 Dec 2026; departures marked R on the timetable require a reservation, the others are non-reserved.
- Local bus day-pass prices not confirmed in this research.

## What to see and do

- [Takayama Jinya](activities/takayama-jinya.md) — ¥500 adults since 1 Apr 2026 (previously ¥440); high-school students and younger free
- [Jinya-mae Morning Market](activities/takayama-jinya-mae-morning-market.md) — Free; pay per stall
- [Miyagawa Morning Market](activities/takayama-miyagawa-morning-market.md) — Free; pay per stall
- [Takayama Old Town (Sanmachi)](activities/takayama-old-town.md) — Free to walk; shops and museums charge separately
- [Shirakawa-go (Ogimachi village)](activities/takayama-shirakawa-go.md) — Village free to walk; Nohi Bus from Takayama ¥2,800 one way (¥2,900 from 1 Dec 2026)

## Seasons

- Winter hours: the Jinya-mae morning market opens at 7:00 instead of 6:00 from January to March, and the Miyagawa market at 8:00 instead of 7:00 from December to March.
- Takayama Jinya closes earlier from November to March (16:30 instead of 17:00) and over the New Year.
- Shirakawa-go holds winter light-up evenings that require advance booking; 2026-27 dates were not confirmed in this research.
- The spring and autumn Takayama Festivals are the town's main events; their dates were not confirmed in this research.
