# Japan

## General information

- Language: Japanese (in big cities and tourist transport there is English signage; the phone camera translation is very handy).
- Currency: yen (¥). Cash is still very common; it is worth carrying some.
- Tipping: not customary, tips are not given.
- Transport: the train network (JR + private lines) is excellent. The **JR Pass** only pays off on long trips between regions.
- Connectivity: an eSIM or pocket wifi at the airport is recommended.
- Safety: one of the safest countries in the world; still, in very touristy areas keep an eye on your pockets.
- Rail passes: for a Tokyo–Kyoto–Osaka golden route the **JR Pass (7-day ¥50,000) does NOT pay off** — point-to-point Hikari shinkansen tickets cost ~¥29,000 total. Use an IC card (Suica/Pasmo/ICOCA) for subways and local trains. Nozomi/Mizuho need a paid supplement even with a pass.

## Regions

- [Hokkaido](regions/hokkaido/) — nature, snow, Sapporo
- [Tohoku](regions/tohoku/) — Pacific coast, little tourism, Sendai
- [Kanto](regions/kanto/) — Tokyo, Yokohama, Kamakura, Nikko, Hakone
- [Chubu](regions/chubu/) — Japanese Alps, Mount Fuji, Nagoya, Kanazawa, Takayama
- [Kansai](regions/kansai/) — Kyoto, Osaka, Kobe, Nara (cultural and historical heart)
- [Chugoku](regions/chugoku/) — Hiroshima, Okayama, Seto Inland Sea coast
- [Shikoku](regions/shikoku/) — 88-temple pilgrimage, Takamatsu, Matsuyama
- [Kyushu](regions/kyushu/) — onsen (Beppu), Fukuoka, Nagasaki, Kumamoto, Okinawa
