# Tokyo ↔ Hakone

## Description

Odakyu Romancecar Shinjuku → Hakone-Yumoto: ¥1,270 fare + ¥1,200 limited-express fee = ¥2,470 (ticketless fee ¥1,150; IC fare ¥1,261 → ¥2,411), 75–107 min (about 85 min) under the March 2026 timetable. Ordinary Odakyu trains: fare only, ¥1,270, via Odawara (slower; time not confirmed). Round trip to Odawara is included in the Hakone Freepass (2 days ¥7,100 from Shinjuku).
