# Kyoto → Tokyo (Tokaido Shinkansen)

## Description

The main long leg of the golden route. Hikari is the sensible train (Nozomi is faster but needs a ¥4,960 supplement with a JR Pass).

## How to get there

Kyoto Station, Tokaido Shinkansen platforms. Buy tickets at JR ticket office or SmartEX.

## Tips

Reserve seats at New Year period — trains are full. Green car +¥4,870 each way. Forward luggage to Tokyo hotel with takuhaibin.
