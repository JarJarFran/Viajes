# Fukuoka ↔ Kagoshima

## Description

Kyushu Shinkansen Hakata–Kagoshima-Chuo, Mizuho about 1h16–1h20 (Sakura slower; up to about 8 min longer since the Sep 2026 post-quake reopening). Reserved about ¥11,950 (Ekitan; ¥12,150 quoted Kagoshima-Chuo→Hakata, probably a seasonal difference); unreserved = normal reserved − ¥530. Exact post-April-2025 official fare not confirmed.
