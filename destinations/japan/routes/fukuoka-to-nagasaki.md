# Fukuoka ↔ Nagasaki

## Description

Hakata–Nagasaki by Relay Kamome + Nishi-Kyushu Shinkansen Kamome (transfer at Takeo-Onsen, same platform): fastest about 1h20. Unreserved ¥5,960, reserved ¥6,490 (Nov 2026 guide); JR online 7-day-advance tickets about ¥3,400. Reserved seats cost ¥200 less in the off-peak season and ¥200–400 more in peak seasons.
