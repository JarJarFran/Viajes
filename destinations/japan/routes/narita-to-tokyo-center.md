# Narita Airport (NRT) ↔ central Tokyo

## Description

Departure transfer on Jan 23 (if flying out of NRT).

## How to get there

NRT terminals 1/2/3 stations.

## Tips

N'EX needs a reserved seat booking for peak times; Skyliner is faster to Ueno but doesn't reach Shinjuku/Shibuya directly.
