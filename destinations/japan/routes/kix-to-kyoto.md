# Kansai Airport (KIX) → Kyoto

## Description

Direct option from Kansai Airport to Kyoto without changing trains.

## How to get there

KIX stations; Haruka stops at Kyoto Station.

## Tips

Check the current Haruka IC fare; the limousine bus is easier with big luggage.
