# Sapporo ↔ Hakodate

## Description

JR limited express Hokuto, all seats reserved, about 3 h 40 min, 12 round trips a day (also stops at Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto and Onuma-Koen). Fare not confirmed: JR East's site lists ¥9,770, while JR Hokkaido's April 2026 table already gives ¥7,790 (reserved seat) for the shorter Sapporo-Oshamambe section; check JR Hokkaido before buying. Covered by the Japan Rail Pass.
