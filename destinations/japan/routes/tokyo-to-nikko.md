# Tokyo (Asakusa) ↔ Nikko

## Options

- Tobu Limited Express (Kegon / Spacia X) Asakusa → Tobu-Nikko: ~1 h 50 min, ¥3,340 one way (¥1,400 fare + ¥1,940 limited express); Revaty Kegon ~¥3,050. Reserved seats.
- Tobu local/rapid trains: ¥1,400 one way, slower with changes.

## Tips

From Shinjuku, take the Ginza Line to Asakusa (~35 min). Reserve the limited express a few days ahead; buses in Nikko are separate (Tobu bus passes exist).
