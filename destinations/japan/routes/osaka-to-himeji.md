# Osaka ↔ Himeji

## Options

- JR Special Rapid (Kobe Line) from Osaka / Shin-Osaka: ~1 h, cheapest.
- Sanyo Shinkansen from Shin-Osaka: ~30 min, ~¥4,000–5,000.

## Tips

Combine with Kobe on the way back if time allows.
