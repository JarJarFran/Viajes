# Tokyo (Shinjuku) ↔ Kawaguchiko

## Options

- Highway bus Busta Shinjuku → Kawaguchiko Station: ¥2,200 one way, ~1 h 50 min (more with traffic); some buses stop at Fuji-Q Highland and Shimoyoshida area.
- JR Chuo Line to Otsuki + Fujikyu Railway: ~2 h, ~¥4,000 total (more with the Fuji Excursion limited express).

## Tips

Book the bus a few days ahead (winter weekends sell out). Fuji is clearest in the morning: take an early bus.
