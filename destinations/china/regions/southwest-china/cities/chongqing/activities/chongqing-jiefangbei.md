# Jiefangbei (People's Liberation Monument)

## Description

Monument completed after 1945 on the site of a wartime wooden structure to mark the victory over Japan, renamed after the 1949 liberation of Chongqing. It stands at the centre of a pedestrian shopping district (about 24,000 m²) in Yuzhong.

## How to get there

Centre of the Yuzhong district.

## Tips

Free, open-air and busiest in the evening.
