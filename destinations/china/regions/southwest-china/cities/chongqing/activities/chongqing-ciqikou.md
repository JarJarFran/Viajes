# Ciqikou Ancient Town

## Description

Old town in Shapingba District with pedestrian lanes of shops, snack stalls and teahouses, plus Baolun Temple and the Zhong family courtyard.

## How to get there

Metro Line 1 Ciqikou station, exit 1, about 5 min walk; 30–40 min from downtown.

## Tips

Sources disagree on whether Baolun Temple is free or charges about CN¥5–10.
