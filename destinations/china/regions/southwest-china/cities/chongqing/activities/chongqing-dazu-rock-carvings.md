# Dazu Rock Carvings (Baodingshan)

## Description

UNESCO-listed cliff carvings scattered over several hills around Dazu; Baodingshan (with Shengshou Temple) is the main group and Beishan the second.

## How to get there

Bus from Caiyuanba bus station to Dazu (about 2 h) then local bus to Baodingshan; or high-speed train to Dazu South (about 40 km away) and taxi/DiDi (CN¥80–100, 50–60 min) or buses 204 + 205 (about 2 h, CN¥9).

## Tips

Tickets are bought and checked at the Dazu Rock Carvings visitor centre; one 2026 report says advance booking is needed. Prices differ between sources (a 2026 article even gives CN¥60), so check the official "大足石刻" account.
