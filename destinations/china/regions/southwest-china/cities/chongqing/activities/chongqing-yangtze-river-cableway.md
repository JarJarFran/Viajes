# Yangtze River Cableway

## Description

Aerial cable car across the Yangtze from the Yuzhong district, with views of the riverside skyline.

## How to get there

North station at 151 Xinhua Road, Yuzhong District.

## Tips

Buy on the official WeChat account "重庆长江索道" up to 2 days ahead (sales open 7:45, 7:15 on holidays; free refund until 16:00 the same day); tickets give a queue number. On-site counter on the ground floor of the United International Building (联合国际大厦).
