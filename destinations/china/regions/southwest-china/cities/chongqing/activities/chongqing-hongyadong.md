# Hongyadong

## Description

Stilted-house style complex stacked on a riverside cliff in Yuzhong, filled with shops, restaurants and terraces. Its illuminated façade at night is one of Chongqing's best-known images.

## Tips

Arrive around 18:30–19:30 for the lights coming on; on national holidays crowds are very large, so allow time to get in and out. Shop hours and façade lighting are separate schedules.
