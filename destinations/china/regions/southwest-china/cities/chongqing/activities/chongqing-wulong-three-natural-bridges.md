# Wulong Karst – Three Natural Bridges

## Description

Karst gorge spanned by three natural rock bridges, visited on foot after descending by elevator; the Longshuixia fissure is a short shuttle ride away.

## How to get there

From Wulong station or the direct coach, go to the Xiannü Town visitor centre, then a 15-min scenic shuttle to the bridges.

## Tips

Leave Chongqing early; sources disagree on train times to Wulong (35 min to 2.5 h depending on train and station), so check 12306.
