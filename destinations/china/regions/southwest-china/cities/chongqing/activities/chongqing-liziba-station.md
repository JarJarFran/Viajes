# Liziba Station (monorail through a building)

## Description

Line 2 monorail station built into floors 6–8 of a 19-storey residential building, so trains appear to pass straight through it. The station and building were designed and built together (opened 2004–2005).

## How to get there

Line 2 Liziba station, Guihuayuan Road, Yuzhong District; the viewing platform is across the road (Exit 2 area), with a wider view from Beibin Road.

## Tips

Watch from the platform across the road for a few trains, then ride Line 2 through the building for the inside view.
