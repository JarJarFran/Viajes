# Eling Park and Eling Second Factory

## Description

Hilltop park whose Kansheng Tower, at its highest point, looks over the two rivers and the city. About 1 km away, the Eling Second Factory is a revitalised industrial site turned cultural hub with cafés, galleries and panoramic views.

## How to get there

Eling Second Factory is at 1 Eling Zhengjie, Yuzhong District, about 1 km walk from the park.

## Tips

The Kansheng Tower may require ID or an appointment. The Eling skywalk is reached from the south gate of Fotuguan Park with a pass from the "鹅岭公园" WeChat mini program (older information; check).
