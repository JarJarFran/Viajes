# Chongqing China Three Gorges Museum

## Description

Chongqing's main museum (also called Chongqing Museum), with collections on the Three Gorges region and the city's history.

## Tips

Closed Mondays except Chinese public holidays. Newer sources say advance reservation was dropped from October 2025 (show passport at the entrance); older guides still mention WeChat booking, so check before going.
