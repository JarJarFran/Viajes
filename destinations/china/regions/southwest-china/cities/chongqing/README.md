# Chongqing

Hilly river city and municipality on the Yangtze, known for its layered skyline and night views (Hongyadong), the Line 2 monorail that runs through a residential building at Liziba and the Yangtze River Cableway. It is the base for day trips to the UNESCO-listed Dazu Rock Carvings and the Wulong karst (Three Natural Bridges).

## How to get there

- From Chengdu by high-speed rail: about 1 h 15 min Chengdu East → Chongqing West (2nd class about CN¥129 on a sampled G train), about 1.5 h to Chongqing North; D trains about 2 h from CN¥96.
- From Shanghai by air: about 2.5 h; one guide quotes economy fares from about CN¥200 to CN¥1,870.
- From Shanghai by high-speed rail: about 9–14 h (a 2026 G-train example: 12 h 23 min); 2nd class about CN¥840–953 in 2026 guides.

## Getting around

- Rail transit: Line 2 is the monorail that passes through the building at Liziba; Line 1 serves Ciqikou (about 30–40 min from downtown, about CN¥4–5).
- Yangtze River Cableway: a scenic river crossing from Yuzhong (CN¥30 one way / CN¥50 return per an April 2026 local guide; English guides still quote CN¥20/CN¥30).
- Dazu Rock Carvings: bus from Caiyuanba bus station to Dazu (about 2 h) then local bus, or high-speed train to Dazu South, which is about 40 km from the carvings (taxi/DiDi about CN¥80–100, 50–60 min; buses 204 + 205 about 2 h, CN¥9).
- Wulong: train to Wulong station (sources disagree: G trains 35–57 min from Chongqing East vs ~2.5 h from Chongqing North; fares about CN¥24–36), then shuttle/taxi to the Xiannü Town visitor centre; a direct tourist coach from Jiefangbei leaves 7:30 and returns 17:00 (CN¥70 one way / CN¥130 return, promotional source).

## Seasons

- Best seasons: March–May and September–November; avoid Chinese national holidays, when Hongyadong and the cableway are packed (e.g. October 2025 Golden Week).
- Hongyadong is lit up from dusk (roughly 18:30–19:30) until about 23:00.
- Yangtze River Cableway runs shorter hours in winter (about 8:00–21:00 Nov–Feb per one guide).
- Wulong Three Natural Bridges closes earlier from September to April (8:30–16:30); Dazu has lower off-season prices in winter (Dec–Feb per one source).
