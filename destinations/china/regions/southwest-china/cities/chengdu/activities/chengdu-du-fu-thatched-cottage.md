# Du Fu Thatched Cottage Museum

## Description

Museum and garden dedicated to the Tang-dynasty poet Du Fu, on the site of the thatched cottage where he lived in Chengdu.

## Tips

Sources disagree on hours; confirm on the museum's official WeChat account (杜甫草堂博物馆) before visiting.
