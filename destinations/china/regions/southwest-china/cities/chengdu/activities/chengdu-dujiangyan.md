# Dujiangyan Irrigation System

## Description

Ancient irrigation system west of Chengdu that is still in use, visited as a riverside scenic area. Often combined with nearby Mount Qingcheng.

## How to get there

Intercity train from Xipu station (Metro Lines 2/6) towards Dujiangyan/Qingchengshan, about 30 min, then local bus or taxi.

## Tips

Real-name online booking for all visitors up to 15 days ahead via the official WeChat ticket platform "青城山-都江堰购票平台"; entry with the ID used (passport). Often combined with Mount Qingcheng on one day.
