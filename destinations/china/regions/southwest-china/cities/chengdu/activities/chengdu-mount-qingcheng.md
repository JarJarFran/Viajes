# Mount Qingcheng (Front Mountain)

## Description

Forested Taoist mountain near Dujiangyan with temples along stone paths and a cable car on the Front Mountain.

## How to get there

Intercity train from Xipu station (Metro Lines 2/6) to Qingchengshan station, 23–40 min, about CN¥10; then Dujiangyan bus 101 to the gate.

## Tips

Booked on the same real-name platform as Dujiangyan (up to 15 days ahead, enter with the passport used). Older sources give a CN¥90 price and different seasonal hours.
