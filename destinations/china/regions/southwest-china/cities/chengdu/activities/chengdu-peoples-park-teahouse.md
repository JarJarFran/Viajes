# People's Park and Heming Teahouse

## Description

Central city park that shows Chengdu's slow pace of life. Its century-old Heming Teahouse is the classic place to sit with a pot of tea among locals.

## How to get there

Metro Line 2 Renmin Park (People's Park) station.

## Tips

Tea is ordered per person and topped up with hot water as long as you stay. A short Sichuan opera add-on (about CN¥48, 30 min) is offered according to one guide.
