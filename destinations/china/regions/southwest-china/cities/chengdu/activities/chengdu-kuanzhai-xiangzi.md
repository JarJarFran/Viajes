# Kuanzhai Xiangzi (Wide and Narrow Alleys)

## Description

Three restored Qing-dynasty alleys forming a pedestrian quarter of shops, eateries and teahouses. A free street area rather than a ticketed site.

## How to get there

Metro Line 4 Kuanzhaixiangzi Alleys station, exit B (3–5 min walk); or Line 2 Renmin Park station, about 400 m.

## Tips

Easily combined with People's Park, a short walk away.
