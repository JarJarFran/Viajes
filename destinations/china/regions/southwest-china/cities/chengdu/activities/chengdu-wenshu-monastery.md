# Wenshu Monastery

## Description

Active Buddhist monastery in central Chengdu (Qingyang District) with a busy teahouse and a canteen; entry is free and a free incense stick is handed out at the gate. The Wenshufang pedestrian quarter next to it is open all day.

## How to get there

Wenshuyuan Street, Qingyang District, in the city centre.

## Tips

The teahouse and canteen close around 17:00. Hours vary slightly between sources and may change in winter; the monastery's WeChat account is "成都文殊院".
