# Sichuan Opera Face-Changing Show (Shufeng Yayun)

## Description

Variety show in a theatre inside Culture Park combining Sichuan opera face-changing (bian lian), fire-spitting, puppetry, shadow play and traditional music.

## How to get there

Culture Park (文化公园), Qintai Road, Qingyang District.

## Tips

Prices and show times vary by seat class and date; check the current schedule. Listings give the address as No. 73 or No. 132 Qintai Road.
