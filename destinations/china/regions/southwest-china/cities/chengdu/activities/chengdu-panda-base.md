# Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding

## Description

Breeding and research centre for giant pandas where visitors walk between outdoor enclosures and panda houses. It is Chengdu's most visited sight.

## How to get there

1375 Xiongmao (Panda) Avenue, Chenghua District, on the northern outskirts of the city.

## Tips

Real-name booking only: reserve online up to 14 days ahead (official WeChat channel or authorised platforms such as Trip.com) with passport number and nationality, one ticket per passport, choosing a morning or afternoon slot; guides report no on-site ticket window, so bring the same physical passport. Sources disagree on exact hours (and one lists CN¥58), so check the official site before going.
