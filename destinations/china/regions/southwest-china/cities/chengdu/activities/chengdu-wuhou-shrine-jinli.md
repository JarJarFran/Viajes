# Wuhou Shrine and Jinli Street

## Description

Memorial temple to Zhuge Liang (Marquis Wu) and the Three Kingdoms era, now run as the Chengdu Wuhou Shrine Museum. Jinli is a free pedestrian street next to it, open 24 h.

## How to get there

231 Wuhouci Avenue (武侯祠大街), Wuhou District.

## Tips

Buy tickets online in advance through the official channels; entry gates scan a QR code. Hours differ between sources, so check the official site or call 028-85535951; an 80-yuan price on one older site is outdated.
