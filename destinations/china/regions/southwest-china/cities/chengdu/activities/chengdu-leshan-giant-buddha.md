# Leshan Giant Buddha

## Description

Giant Buddha carved into a riverside cliff in Leshan, south of Chengdu. It is seen from the cliff-top paths (walking ticket) or from the river on a sightseeing boat.

## How to get there

From Chengdu about 50–60 min by high-speed train to Leshan, then a local bus to the scenic area (North Gate).

## Tips

Timed, capped entry under real name: book online in advance (booking opens 7 days ahead, stock refreshed 10:00 daily; holiday slots sell out) and bring your physical passport to scan at the gate. Winter hours differ between sources; a night visit is also listed on Ctrip but was not confirmed.
