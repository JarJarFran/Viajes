# Chengdu

Capital of Sichuan province, best known for the Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding, its slow-paced teahouse culture (People's Park, Wenshu Monastery) and Three Kingdoms and Tang-poetry heritage (Wuhou Shrine, Du Fu Thatched Cottage). It is the usual base for day trips to the Leshan Giant Buddha and to the Dujiangyan irrigation system and Mount Qingcheng.

## How to get there

- From Chongqing by high-speed rail to Chengdu East: fastest G trains about 1 h 15 min (2nd class CN¥129 on a sampled G train to/from Chongqing West, CN¥161–175 on G trains via Chongqing North); slower D trains about 1 h 55 min from CN¥96. Around 140–160 trains a day; fares are dynamic.
- From Shanghai by air: about 2 h 45 min non-stop; Chengdu has two airports, Shuangliu (CTU) and Tianfu (TFU), both with flights to Pudong (PVG) and Hongqiao (SHA). Carriers include China Eastern, Sichuan Airlines and Xiamen Airlines.
- From Shanghai by high-speed rail: fastest G239 Shanghai Hongqiao–Chengdu East about 10 h 6 min; other G/D trains up to ~15 h; 2nd class about CN¥721–992 (2026 guide).
- The new Chengdu–Chongqing middle-line HSR (Chengdu Station–Chongqing North, 292 km, 350 km/h) is under construction (track-laying from May 2026) and expected to open in 2027, cutting the trip to about 50 min.

## Getting around

- Metro: Line 4 Kuanzhaixiangzi Alleys station (exit B faces the Wide Alley); Line 2 Renmin Park (People's Park) station, about 400 m from the alleys.
- Day-trip trains: from Xipu station (Metro Lines 2 and 6) intercity trains run to Qingchengshan for Mount Qingcheng / Dujiangyan, 23–40 min, about CN¥10 (2026 guide: ~41 direct services a day); Leshan is about 50–60 min by high-speed train plus a local bus.
- Real-name booking: the Panda Base, Leshan Giant Buddha and Dujiangyan/Mount Qingcheng sell tickets online only or mainly online under the visitor's ID; foreigners book with their passport number and must show the same physical passport at the gate.

## Events

- Chengdu Wuhou Shrine Grand Temple Fair — yearly, around Spring Festival (Chinese New Year)

## Local food

- Teahouse culture: a pot of tea with hot-water refills at Heming Teahouse in People's Park (about CN¥20–35, sources range CN¥16–40) or the teahouse inside Wenshu Monastery.
- Wenshu Monastery also has a canteen open to visitors (closes around 17:00).

## Seasons

- Pandas are most active early in the morning; the Panda Base opens about 7:30 in summer and about 8:00 in winter (third-party sources; exact months differ).
- Spring Festival: the Panda Base uses morning/afternoon timed tickets with a cap of 80,000 visitors a day (official 2026 notice); the Wuhou Shrine holds its big temple fair with night lantern displays.
- Leshan Giant Buddha keeps longer hours from 1 Apr to 7 Oct (7:30–18:30) and shorter ones from 8 Oct to 31 Mar; Dujiangyan charges a lower off-season price Nov–Mar (2025 guide).
- Chinese public holidays (Spring Festival, October Golden Week) sell out timed tickets quickly; book as soon as the window opens (14 days ahead for the Panda Base, 15 for Dujiangyan/Qingcheng, 7 for Leshan).
