# Shanghai Museum East (Pudong)

## Description

New East branch of the Shanghai Museum on Century Avenue in Pudong, opened in phases from 2024, with large permanent galleries of Chinese art.

## How to get there

Century Avenue, Pudong; enter via the B1 East Gate near Dingxiang Road (nearest metro station not verified in this pass).

## Tips

No reservation needed for individual visitors since Sep 2024: enter with a passport through the B1 East Gate (near Dingxiang Road); groups of 20+ should book. One 2026 travel site claims a 7-day-ahead booking is needed, which contradicts the official reservation-free policy.
