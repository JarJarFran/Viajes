# Jing'an Temple

## Description

Buddhist temple on West Nanjing Road surrounded by the Jing'an commercial district.

## How to get there

Next to Jing'an Temple metro station on West Nanjing Road (lines not re-verified in this pass).

## Tips

Hours and prices change around Buddhist festivals and Spring Festival; older guides quoting CN¥30 or 9:00-16:30 appear out of date.
