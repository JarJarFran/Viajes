# Shanghai Tower observation deck (Top of Shanghai)

## Description

China's tallest building, in Lujiazui (Pudong), with an indoor observation deck near the top.

## How to get there

Metro Line 2 Lujiazui station (exit 6 suggested), then a short walk through Lujiazui.

## Tips

Reseller tickets usually need booking at least one day ahead and often cost more than the face price; online bookings use the passport number. Figures come from third-party guides and resellers, not the official site — check the price and last entry before going.
