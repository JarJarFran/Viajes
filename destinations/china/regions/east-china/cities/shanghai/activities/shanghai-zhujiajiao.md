# Zhujiajiao water town

## Description

Canal town with stone bridges and old streets in Qingpu District, in the west of Shanghai municipality, usually visited as a day trip from the city centre.

## How to get there

Metro Line 17 to Zhujiajiao station (about 50 min from central Shanghai; the line starts at Hongqiao Railway Station), then bus 1510 or a 15–30 min walk to the old town.

## Tips

Children 6 and under (or under 1.3 m) free and ages 7–18, students and over-60s half price on the combo ticket per one Oct 2026 guide (another says no senior discount). Exit numbers at Zhujiajiao station differ between guides.
