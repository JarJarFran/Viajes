# Former French Concession and Wukang Road

## Description

Wukang Road (originally Route Ferguson, built 1897) is a 1.17 km historic street in Xuhui District, in the western part of the former French Concession, lined with plane trees and 37 officially protected historic buildings (Mediterranean, French Renaissance, English and Art Deco villas); a National Historic and Cultural Street since 2011. It ends at the Wukang Mansion (Normandie Apartments, László Hudec, 1924) on Middle Huaihai Road. Ba Jin's former residence (No. 113) is open to the public; Soong Ching-ling's Memorial Residence is across Huaihai Road.

## Tips

There is a tourist information centre at 393 Wukang Road with self-guided walking tours. Most houses are private or offices, seen from the street; cafés have made it busy at weekends.
