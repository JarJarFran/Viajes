# Tianzifang

## Description

Maze of shikumen lane houses off Taikang Road converted into small shops, cafes and studios.

## How to get there

Metro Line 9 Dapuqiao station exit 1, about 5 min walk.

## Tips

Shops open from about 10:00, so mornings are quiet; the lanes are narrow and crowded on weekends.
