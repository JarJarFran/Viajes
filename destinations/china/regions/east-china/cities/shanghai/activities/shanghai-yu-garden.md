# Yu Garden and City God Temple

## Description

Ming-dynasty classical Chinese garden in the old walled-city area, next to the Shanghai City God Temple and the surrounding Yuyuan bazaar streets.

## How to get there

Metro Lines 10/14 Yuyuan Garden station (Line 10 exit 1 is the usual one), then a short walk to the bazaar and garden gates.

## Tips

Tickets are sold at the north and south gate windows and online via the official 'Shanghai Yu Garden' WeChat mini-program; online booking (also via resellers) asks for passport details and allows one ticket per person per day. Seasonal price months come from a single guide (another just says high/low season); City God Temple ticket price and hours could not be verified.
