# Lujiazui

## Description

Pudong financial district across the Huangpu from the Bund, with the Oriental Pearl TV Tower, Shanghai World Financial Center, Jin Mao Tower and Shanghai Tower.

## How to get there

Metro Line 2 Lujiazui station.

## Tips

The skyline is best seen lit at night from the Bund across the river; combine with the Shanghai Tower deck for the view from above.
