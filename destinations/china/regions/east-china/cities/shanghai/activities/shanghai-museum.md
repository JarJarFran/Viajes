# Shanghai Museum (People's Square)

## Description

Major museum of ancient Chinese art (bronzes, ceramics, painting, calligraphy) in its 1990s building on People's Square; the larger East branch in Pudong is a separate site.

## How to get there

On the south side of People's Square (enter via the south gate); metro station not re-verified in this pass.

## Tips

Since 1 Sep 2025 individuals no longer need a reservation: enter through the south gate showing a valid ID (passport for foreigners). Special exhibitions need tickets and crowd control may apply at peak times.
