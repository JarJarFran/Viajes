# The Bund

## Description

Riverside promenade on the west bank of the Huangpu River, lined with early-20th-century bank and trading-house buildings and facing the Lujiazui skyline.

## How to get there

Metro Lines 2/10 East Nanjing Road station, then walk east along Nanjing Road to the riverfront and south along the promenade.

## Tips

Light-up times differ between guides (about 18:00/19:00 to 22:00/23:00); go just after dusk to see both the Bund facades and the Lujiazui skyline lit.
