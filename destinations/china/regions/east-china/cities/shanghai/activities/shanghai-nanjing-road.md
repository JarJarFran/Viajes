# Nanjing Road (East) Pedestrian Street

## Description

Main shopping street running west from the Bund towards People's Square, pedestrianised along its eastern section.

## How to get there

Metro Lines 2/10 East Nanjing Road station; the pedestrian street runs from near the Bund west towards People's Square.

## Tips

Combine with the Bund: walk the pedestrian street east from the station to the riverfront at dusk.
