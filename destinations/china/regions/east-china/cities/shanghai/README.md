# Shanghai

Shanghai is mainland China's main financial and commercial centre, on the Huangpu River near the mouth of the Yangtze. It is known for the Bund waterfront facing the Lujiazui skyline, the former French Concession and shikumen lane neighbourhoods, and canal towns such as Zhujiajiao within the municipality.

## How to get there

- Pudong International Airport (PVG): Metro Line 2 to People's Square ~CN¥7, about 1 h; Maglev to Longyang Road CN¥50 (CN¥40 with a same-day air ticket), ~8 min, then Line 2.
- Hongqiao Airport (SHA): Metro Lines 2 and 10 (T2) or Line 10 (T1), ~CN¥5–6 and 30–40 min to People's Square.
- Rail from Chengdu/Chongqing: see the routes researched separately.

## Getting around

- Metro fares are distance-based from CN¥3; a fare review is pending: proposals published 19 Aug 2026 would raise the average fare by about CN¥1 per trip (either keep CN¥3 start with shorter bands, or CN¥4 start) and add 30-day multi-ride tickets (45/60/90 rides for CN¥210/270/390, Maglev excluded); a public hearing was held 7 Sep 2026 and no final decision was found as of mid-Sep reports.
- Metro one-day pass CN¥18 (24 h) and three-day pass CN¥45 (72 h), unlimited metro rides, Maglev not included; Maglev single trip + one-day metro pass combo CN¥55.
- Maglev (Longyang Road–Pudong Airport): CN¥50 one way (CN¥40 with same-day air ticket), CN¥80 return.
- Shanghai Pass: three-day pass launched in 2025 for foreign visitors (240-hour visa-free transit eligible), valid on metro, buses, Airport Link Line, Maglev and ferries (not Jinshan Rail Line); prices quoted by guides differ — check at purchase.

## Events

- Yuyuan Lantern Festival — yearly, from about three weeks before Spring Festival to the Lantern Festival (15th day of the 1st lunar month); 2026 edition 26 Jan–3 Mar (36 nights)

## Local food

- Xiaolongbao (soup dumplings): originated in Nanxiang (Jiading District); classic stop is Nanxiang Steamed Bun Restaurant in Yu Garden; Jia Jia Tang Bao (Huanghe Road) is known for crab-roe versions in autumn.
- Shengjianbao (pan-fried pork buns with soup): Yang's Fry-Dumpling (Xiao Yang Shengjian), a chain with dozens of branches across the city.
- Hairy crab: autumn speciality (roughly Sep/Oct–Nov), traditionally Yangcheng Lake crabs steamed and eaten with ginger and vinegar; Wang Bao He is a long-established crab restaurant.
- Hongshao rou (red-braised pork belly): signature dish of Shanghainese (benbang) home-style cooking.

## Seasons

- National holiday weeks bring heavy domestic crowds and sold-out trains: Spring Festival 15–23 Feb 2026 and 5–13 Feb 2027; National Day Golden Week 1–7 Oct (2026 and 2027); Labour Day 1–5 May (2026 and 2027).
- Autumn (Oct–Nov) is hairy-crab season.
- Yu Garden lantern show runs around Spring Festival until the Lantern Festival (2026: 26 Jan–3 Mar); expect heavy crowds and special ticketing in the Yuyuan area then.
