# China

## General information

- Language: Mandarin Chinese; little English outside hotels, airports and big sights — a translation app with camera mode (installed before arrival) is essential.
- Currency: renminbi (CNY, ¥/元). Mobile payment (Alipay / WeChat Pay) is the norm; carry some cash for small vendors.
- Time zone: UTC+8 everywhere (no daylight saving).
- Tipping: not customary.

## Practical (checked 2026-11-18)

- Visa-free entry (Spain/most EU): ordinary passport holders of Spain (since 1 Dec 2023) and most EU states can enter visa-free for up to 30 days for tourism, business, family visits, exchanges or transit. The scheme was extended on 3 Nov 2025 to 31 Dec 2026. As of 16 Nov 2026 no general extension into 2027 had been announced (only Russia was extended to end-2027; Slovakia said on 10 Nov 2026 that Beijing was willing to extend for Slovak citizens). For trips after 31 Dec 2026, check the Chinese MFA/embassy before travelling.
- 240-hour visa-free transit: Spain is among the eligible nationalities (55 after Indonesia was added in June 2025; 57 since 20 Aug 2026 with Kyrgyzstan and Vietnam). Requires a confirmed onward ticket to a third country/region (not back to the origin) within 240 h; entry/exit via 65 designated ports in 24 provincial-level regions, staying within the permitted regions, and entry and exit may be at different ports. Work, study or journalism still need a visa.
- Arrival card: since 20 Nov 2025 it can be filed online for free before arrival (NIA website s.nia.gov.cn, NIA 12367 app, WeChat/Alipay mini-programs; 13 languages) or on paper/kiosks at the border. NIA warns of fake paid websites.
- Payments: Alipay and WeChat Pay accept linked foreign Visa/Mastercard after passport verification (set up before the trip). No service fee up to CN¥200 per payment; above that 3% is charged on the whole amount; your bank may add its own fee. Transaction/annual limits differ between sources. Foreign cards are still not accepted everywhere, so carry some cash.
- Internet: Google services (including Maps), WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook are blocked on Chinese networks (local SIMs, hotel Wi-Fi). Foreign roaming SIMs/travel eSIMs route data abroad and are widely used to reach them, but sources disagree on reliability; VPN websites are blocked inside China, so install apps before arrival and keep offline maps or Amap.
- Trains: the 12306 website/app has an English version and accepts registration with a passport (name, number, expiry, nationality). Tickets are real-name, tied to the passport. At stations, use the 12306 'Ride Code' QR or show the passport at the manual check lane (sources differ on whether passports work at automatic gates). Foreign cards are occasionally rejected at payment; agencies such as Trip.com are an alternative.
- Taxis/ride-hailing: DiDi (Greater China app with English mode) registers with a foreign phone number and accepts international Visa/Mastercard. The DiDi mini-program inside Alipay works in English without a Chinese number and is paid through Alipay. Amap added English ride-hailing in 2025.
- Carry the passport at all times: train tickets, hotel check-in and many museum/attraction reservations are real-name (passport number). Popular museums often require advance real-name booking and are often closed on Mondays.
- Public holidays: the holiday list shows every official day off. The State Council also sets make-up working days: 2026 Jan 4, Feb 14, Feb 28, May 9, Sep 20, Oct 10; 2027 Jan 31, Feb 14, May 8, Sep 26, Oct 9. Spring Festival and Golden Week bring peak domestic travel.
