# Shanghai airports ↔ city centre

## Description

Airports to city centre. Pudong (PVG): Metro Line 2 from Pudong Airport T1&2 to People's Square ~CN¥7, about 1 h (cheapest); or Maglev PVG–Longyang Road CN¥50 one way (CN¥40 with a same-day air ticket; CN¥80 return within 7 days; VIP CN¥100), ~8 min, then Line 2 to the centre (~30–40 min more). Maglev first/last trains reported as 06:45–21:40 from Longyang Road and 07:02–21:42 from PVG (third-party guide; check on the day). Hongqiao (SHA): T2 station on Lines 2 and 10, T1 on Line 10; ~CN¥5–6 and 30–40 min to People's Square. The Maglev is excluded from the 2026 metro fare review.
