# 2027-01-21 — Tokyo

**Nikko day trip (UNESCO)**

## Itinerary
- early: Tobu Limited Express Asakusa→Tobu-Nikko ¥3,340 each way (~1 h 50 min) — reserve seats
- morning: Nikko Toshogu Shrine (UNESCO, ~¥1,600; closes 16:00 in winter) — Rinno-ji next door if time
- afternoon: bus up the Irohazaka to Kegon Falls (~45 min; elevator ¥600) — don't try to do all of Nikko
- lunch in Nikko (yuba / soba)
- back by evening
- dinner: udon at Udon Manda Jiro, Shinjuku Gyoen-mae (3.59)
