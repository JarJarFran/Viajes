# 2027-01-06 — Kyoto

**Arashiyama**

## Itinerary
- early (before 9:00): Arashiyama Bamboo Grove + Togetsukyo Bridge (free)
- morning: Iwatayama Monkey Park (¥800, 20 min hike, Fuji view on clear days)
- lunch: soba with the river view at Yoshimura (3.50, lunch ¥1,000–1,999)
- afternoon: free — riverside walk or back to the hotel to rest
- dinner: Tsukumo Udon near Kyoto Station (3.60)

## Notes
- Sagano Romantic Train often paused in deep winter — skip unless you really want it.
