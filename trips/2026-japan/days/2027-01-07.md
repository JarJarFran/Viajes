# 2027-01-07 — Kyoto

**Fushimi — calm day**

## Itinerary
- 7:00: Fushimi Inari Senbon Torii at dawn — empty, magical (free, 2–3 h if you hike to the top)
- late morning: Fushimi sake district, Gekkeikan Okura Sake Museum (¥600 with 3 tastings)
- lunch: udon at Honkaku Teuchi-Udon Taiga, by Fujinomori Station (3.72) — on the Keihan line between the shrine and the sake district
- afternoon: FREE — Kawaramachi shopping, cafe, or nap at the hotel
- dinner: wagyu nabe at KANEGURA, Shijo (3.69)

## Notes

- Mugi no Yoake (planned before) is near Tambaguchi Station, ~7 km from Fushimi — swapped for a lunch on the way.
- Deliberately light: the north temples (Kinkaku-ji/Ryoan-ji) moved to Jan 8 morning.
