# Japan: Osaka → Kyoto → Tokyo (New Year)

29 days, 2025-12-26 → 2026-01-23. Route: fly into **Kansai Airport (KIX)**, out of **Tokyo (NRT or HND)** — open jaw.

## Summary

Osaka (Dec 26–Jan 2, incl. New Year's Eve) → Kyoto (Jan 2–8, hatsumode + Toka Ebisu) → Tokyo (Jan 9–23, winter illuminations, Kamakura day trip).

## Transport plan

- **No JR Pass**: golden route Tokyo–Kyoto–Osaka point-to-point ≈ ¥29,000 vs ¥50,000 for a 7-day pass. Buy individual shinkansen tickets (Hikari) + IC card.
- Kyoto → Tokyo (Jan 8 or 9): Hikari shinkansen Kyoto → Tokyo ~2 h 15 min, ~¥14,000 reserved.
- Osaka → Kyoto (Jan 2): JR Rapid ~30 min ¥580 or Hankyu ¥410 — no shinkansen needed.
- Local: ICOCA in Kansai, Suica/Pasmo in Tokyo. Osaka Amazing Pass (¥2,800/¥3,600) optional; Kyoto Subway & Bus 1-Day Pass ¥1,100.
